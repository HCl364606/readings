The Strength of Weak Ties 1^{1}
弱关系的力量 1^{1}

Mark S. Granovetter  马克·S·格拉诺维特

Johns Hopkins University
约翰·霍普金斯大学

Analysis of social networks is suggested as a tool for linking micro and macro levels of sociological theory. The procedure is illustrated by elaboration of the macro implications of one aspect of small-scale interaction: the strength of dyadic ties. It is argued that the degree of overlap of two individuals’ friendship networks varies directly with the strength of their tie to one another. The impact of this principle on diffusion of influence and information, mobility opportunity, and community organization is explored. Stress is laid on the cohesive power of weak ties. Most network models deal, implicitly, with strong ties, thus confining their applicability to small, well-defined groups. Emphasis on weak ties lends itself to discussion of relations between groups and to analysis of segments of social structure not easily defined in terms of primary groups.
社会网络分析被认为是一种连接社会学理论微观和宏观层面的工具。本文以小规模互动的一个方面——二元关系强度——的宏观影响为例，阐述了这一方法。文章指出，两个人的友谊网络重叠程度与其彼此关系的强度直接相关。文章探讨了这一原则对影响和信息扩散、流动机会以及社区组织的影响，并着重强调了弱关系的凝聚力。大多数网络模型都隐含地关注强关系，因此其适用范围仅限于小型、界限分明的群体。而对弱关系的关注则有助于探讨群体间的关系，并分析那些难以用基本群体来界定的社会结构片段。

A fundamental weakness of current sociological theory is that it does not relate micro-level interactions to macro-level patterns in any convincing way. Large-scale statistical, as well as qualitative, studies offer a good deal of insight into such macro phenomena as social mobility, community organization, and political structure. At the micro level, a large and increasing body of data and theory offers useful and illuminating ideas about what transpires within the confines of the small group. But how interaction in small groups aggregates to form large-scale patterns eludes us in most cases.
当前社会学理论的一个根本缺陷在于，它无法以令人信服的方式将微观层面的互动与宏观层面的模式联系起来。大规模的统计研究以及定性研究为社会流动、社区组织和政治结构等宏观现象提供了诸多深刻的见解。在微观层面，大量且不断增长的数据和理论为我们理解小群体内部发生的事情提供了有益且富有启发性的思路。然而，在大多数情况下，我们仍然无法理解小群体中的互动是如何聚合形成大规模模式的。

I will argue, in this paper, that the analysis of processes in interpersonal networks provides the most fruitful micro-macro bridge. In one way or another, it is through these networks that small-scale interaction becomes translated into large-scale patterns, and that these, in turn, feed back into small groups.
本文将论证，对人际网络过程的分析是连接微观与宏观的最有效桥梁。小规模的互动正是通过这些网络转化为大规模的模式，而这些模式反过来又会反馈到小群体中。

Sociometry, the precursor of network analysis, has always been curiously peripheral—invisible, really—in sociological theory. This is partly because it has usually been studied and applied only as a branch of social psychology; it is also because of the inherent complexities of precise network analysis. We have had neither the theory nor the measurement and sampling techniques to move sociometry from the usual small-group level to that of larger structures. While a number of stimulating and suggestive studies have recently moved in this direction (Bott 1957; Mayer 1961; Milgram 1967; Boissevain 1968; Mitchell 1969), they do not treat structural issues in much theoretical detail. Studies which do so usually involve a level of technical complexity appropriate to such forbidding sources as the Bulletin of Mathematical Biophysics, where the original motivation for the study of networks was that of developing a theory of neural, rather than social, interaction (see the useful review of this literature by Coleman [1960]; also Rapoport [1963]).
社会计量学作为网络分析的前身，在社会学理论中一直处于一种奇特的边缘地位——实际上可以说是隐形的。这部分是因为它通常只作为社会心理学的一个分支进行研究和应用；也因为精确的网络分析本身就非常复杂。我们既缺乏理论基础，也缺乏测量和抽样技术，无法将社会计量学从通常的小群体层面扩展到更大的结构层面。尽管近年来有一些颇具启发性和启发性的研究朝着这个方向发展（Bott 1957; Mayer 1961; Milgram 1967; Boissevain 1968; Mitchell 1969），但它们并未对结构性问题进行深入的理论探讨。此类研究通常涉及与《数学生物物理学公报》等令人望而生畏的来源相适应的技术复杂程度，该公报最初研究网络的动机是发展神经交互理论，而不是社会交互理论（参见 Coleman [1960] 对此文献的有用评论；另见 Rapoport [1963]）。

The strategy of the present paper is to choose a rather limited aspect of small-scale interaction—the strength of interpersonal ties—and to show, in some detail, how the use of network analysis can relate this aspect to such varied macro phenomena as diffusion, social mobility, political organization, and social cohesion in general. While the analysis is essentially qualitative, a mathematically inclined reader will recognize the potential for models; mathematical arguments, leads, and references are suggested mostly in footnotes.
本文的策略是选取小规模互动中一个较为有限的方面——人际关系的强度——并详细阐述如何运用网络分析将这一方面与扩散、社会流动、政治组织以及社会凝聚力等各种宏观现象联系起来。尽管分析本质上是定性的，但对数学感兴趣的读者会发现其中蕴含的建模潜力；相关的数学论证、线索和参考文献主要以脚注的形式呈现。

THE STRENGTH OF TIES
关系的力量

Most intuitive notions of the “strength” of an interpersonal tie should be satisfied by the following definition: the strength of a tie is a (probably linear) combination of the amount of time, the emotional intensity, the intimacy (mutual confiding), and the reciprocal services which characterize the tie. 2^{2} Each of these is somewhat independent of the other, though the set is obviously highly intracorrelated. Discussion of operational measures of and weights attaching to each of the four elements is postponed to future empirical studies. 3^{3} It is sufficient for the present purpose if most of us can agree, on a rough intuitive basis, whether a given tie is strong, weak, or absent. 4^{4}
大多数关于人际关系“强度”的直觉概念都可以用以下定义来解释：人际关系的强度是时间、情感强度、亲密程度（相互倾诉）以及互惠服务这四个要素的（可能呈线性关系）组合。 2^{2} 这些要素彼此之间有一定的独立性，但显然它们之间存在高度的内部相关性。关于这四个要素的操作性测量方法及其权重的讨论将留待未来的实证研究。 3^{3} 就目前而言，只要我们大多数人能够基于粗略的直觉，就对一段特定的人际关系是强、弱还是不存在达成共识，就足够了。 4^{4}

2^{2} Ties discussed in this paper are assumed to be positive and symmetric; a comprehensive theory might require discussion of negative and/or asymmetric ties, but this would add unnecessary complexity to the present, exploratory comments.
2^{2} 本文讨论的关系假定为正向和对称的；一个全面的理论可能需要讨论负向和/或不对称的关系，但这会给目前的探索性评论增加不必要的复杂性。

3^{3} Some anthropologists suggest “multiplexity,” that is, multiple contents in a relationship, as indicating a strong tie (Kapferer 1969, p. 213). While this may be accurate in some circumstances, ties with only one content or with diffuse content may be strong as well (Simmel 1950, pp. 317–29). The present definition would show most multiplex ties to be strong but also allow for other possibilities.
3^{3} 一些人类学家认为，“多重性”（即关系中包含多种内容）是强关系的一个标志（Kapferer 1969，第 213 页）。虽然在某些情况下这种说法可能是准确的，但仅包含单一内容或内容分散的关系也可能很牢固（Simmel 1950，第 317-329 页）。本文的定义表明，大多数多重关系都是强关系，但也允许存在其他可能性。

4^{4} Included in “absent” are both the lack of any relationship and ties without substantial significance, such as a “nodding” relationship between people living on the same street, or the “tie” to the vendor from whom one customarily buys a morning newspaper. That two people “know” each other by name need not move their relation out of this category if their interaction is negligible. In some contexts, however (disasters, for example), such “negligible” ties might usefully be distinguished from the absence of one. This is an ambiguity caused by substitution, for convenience of exposition, of discrete values for an underlying continuous variable.
4^{4} “缺失”既包括完全没有关系的情况，也包括没有实质性意义的联系，例如住在同一条街上的人之间的点头之交，或者与每天早上购买报纸的报贩之间的“联系”。即使两个人彼此“认识”，如果他们的互动微乎其微，他们的关系也不一定属于“缺失”范畴。然而，在某些情况下（例如灾难），这种“微不足道”的联系可能需要与完全没有联系的情况区分开来。这种歧义是由于为了便于阐述而用离散值代替了潜在的连续变量造成的。

Consider, now, any two arbitrarily selected individuals—call them A and B—and the set, S=C,D,E,…S = C, D, E, \ldots , of all persons with ties to either or both of them. 5^{5} The hypothesis which enables us to relate dyadic ties to larger structures is: the stronger the tie between A and B, the larger the proportion of individuals in S to whom they will both be tied, that is, connected by a weak or strong tie. This overlap in their friendship circles is predicted to be least when their tie is absent, most when it is strong, and intermediate when it is weak.
现在，考虑任意两个任意选择的个体——称他们为 A 和 B——以及与他们中任何一个或两个都有联系的所有人的集合 S=C,D,E,…S = C, D, E, \ldots 。 5^{5} 使我们能够将二元关系与更大的结构联系起来的假设是：A 和 B 之间的联系越强，集合 S 中与他们都有联系的个体比例就越大，也就是说，通过弱联系或强联系相互关联的个体比例就越大。我们预测，当他们之间没有联系时，他们朋友圈的重叠程度最小；当他们之间有强联系时，重叠程度最大；当他们之间有弱联系时，重叠程度居中。

The proposed relationship results, first, from the tendency (by definition) of stronger ties to involve larger time commitments. If A-B and A-C ties exist, then the amount of time C spends with B depends (in part) on the amount A spends with B and C, respectively. (If the events “A is with B” and “A is with C” were independent, then the event “C is with A and B” would have probability equal to the product of their probabilities. For example, if A and B are together 60% of the time, and A and C 40%, then C, A, and B would be together 24% of the time. Such independence would be less likely after than before B and C became acquainted.) If C and B have no relationship, common strong ties to A will probably bring them into interaction and generate one. Implicit here is Homans’s idea that “the more frequently persons interact with one another, the stronger their sentiments of friendship for one another are apt to be” (1950, p. 133).
首先，这种关系源于（根据定义）更强的关系往往意味着更多的时间投入。如果存在 AB 和 AC 关系，那么 C 与 B 相处的时间（部分地）取决于 A 分别与 B 和 C 相处的时间。（如果“A 与 B 在一起”和“A 与 C 在一起”这两个事件相互独立，那么“C 与 A 和 B 在一起”的概率等于它们各自概率的乘积。例如，如果 A 和 B 在一起的时间占 60%，A 和 C 在一起的时间占 40%，那么 C、A 和 B 三人在一起的时间占 24%。在 B 和 C 相识之后，这种独立性的可能性会降低。）如果 C 和 B 之间没有关系，那么他们与 A 的共同强关系很可能会促使他们互动并建立关系。这里隐含了霍曼斯的观点，即“人们彼此互动越频繁，他们之间的友谊就越深厚”（1950，第 133 页）。

The hypothesis is made plausible also by empirical evidence that the stronger the tie connecting two individuals, the more similar they are, in various ways (Berscheid and Walster 1969, pp. 69–91; Bramel 1969, pp. 9–16; Brown 1965, pp. 71–90; Laumann 1968; Newcomb 1961, chap. 5; Precker 1952). Thus, if strong ties connect A to B and A to C, both C and B, being similar to A, are probably similar to one another, increasing the likelihood of a friendship once they have met. Applied in reverse, these two factors—time and similarity—indicate why weaker A-B and A-C ties make a C-B tie less likely than strong ones: C and B are less likely to interact and less likely to be compatible if they do.
经验证据也支持这一假设，即两个人之间的联系越紧密，他们在各个方面就越相似（Berscheid 和 Walster 1969，第 69-91 页；Bramel 1969，第 9-16 页；Brown 1965，第 71-90 页；Laumann 1968；Newcomb 1961，第 5 章；Precker 1952）。因此，如果 A 与 B 以及 A 与 C 之间存在强联系，那么 C 和 B 都与 A 相似，彼此之间也可能相似，这增加了他们见面后成为朋友的可能性。反过来，这两个因素——时间和相似性——解释了为什么较弱的 AB 和 AC 关系会降低 CB 关系的形成概率：C 和 B 互动的可能性较低，即使互动，彼此的契合度也较低。

The theory of cognitive balance, as formulated by Heider (1958) and especially by Newcomb (1961, pp. 4–23), also predicts this result. If strong ties A-B and A-C exist, and if B and C are aware of one another, anything short of a positive tie would introduce a “psychological strain” into the situation since C will want his own feelings to be congruent with those of his good friend, A, and similarly, for B and his friend, A. Where the ties are weak, however, such consistency is psychologically less crucial. (On this point see also Homans [1950, p. 255] and Davis [1963, p. 448].)
海德（Heider，1958）提出的认知平衡理论，尤其是纽科姆（Newcomb，1961，第 4-23 页）的理论，也预测了这一结果。如果 AB 和 AC 之间存在强关系，且 B 和 C 彼此知晓，那么任何非积极的关系都会给这种情境带来“心理压力”，因为 C 会希望自己的感受与好友 A 的感受一致，B 也同样希望与好友 A 的感受一致。然而，如果关系较弱，这种一致性在心理上就不那么重要了。（关于这一点，另见霍曼斯[Homans，1950，第 255 页]和戴维斯[Davis，1963，第 448 页]。）

Some direct evidence for the basic hypothesis exists (Kapferer 1969, p. 229 n.; Laumann and Schuman 1967; Rapoport and Horvath 1961;
基本假设存在一些直接证据（Kapferer 1969，第 229 页脚注；Laumann 和 Schuman 1967；Rapoport 和 Horvath 1961；

Rapoport 1963). 6^{6} This evidence is less comprehensive than one might hope. In addition, however, certain inferences from the hypothesis have received empirical support. Description of these inferences will suggest some of the substantive implications of the above argument.
Rapoport 1963）。 6^{6} 此类证据不如人们所期望的那样全面。然而，该假设的某些推论已获得实证支持。对这些推论的描述将有助于理解上述论证的一些实质性含义。

WEAK TIES IN DIFFUSION PROCESSES
扩散过程中的弱联系

To derive implications for large networks of relations, it is necessary to frame the basic hypothesis more precisely. This can be done by investigating the possible triads consisting of strong, weak, or absent ties among A, B, and any arbitrarily chosen friend of either or both (i.e., some member of the set S, described above). A thorough mathematical model would do this in some detail, suggesting probabilities for various types. This analysis becomes rather involved, however, and it is sufficient for my purpose in this paper to say that the triad which is most unlikely to occur, under the hypothesis stated above, is that in which A and B are strongly linked, A has a strong tie to some friend C, but the tie between C and B is absent. This triad is shown in figure 1. To see the consequences of this assertion,
为了推导出对大型关系网络的影响，有必要更精确地构建基本假设。这可以通过研究 A、B 以及任意选定的 A 或 B 的朋友（即上述集合 S 中的某个成员）之间可能存在的强、弱或无联系的三元组来实现。一个完善的数学模型可以对此进行更详细的分析，并给出各种类型的概率。然而，这种分析相当复杂，因此，就本文的目的而言，只需指出在上述假设下，最不可能出现的三元组是 A 和 B 之间存在强联系，A 与某个朋友 C 之间也存在强联系，但 C 和 B 之间不存在联系。该三元组如图 1 所示。为了理解这一论断的后果，

I will exaggerate it in what follows by supposing that the triad shown never occurs—that is, that the B-C tie is always present (whether weak or strong), given the other two strong ties. Whatever results are inferred from this supposition should tend to occur in the degree that the triad in question tends to be absent.
接下来，我将夸大其词，假设所示的三元组永远不会出现——也就是说，在另外两个强关系存在的情况下，BC 关系始终存在（无论强弱）。由此假设推导出的任何结果，都应倾向于与该三元组倾向于不存在的程度成正比。

6^{6} The models and experiments of Rapoport and his associates have been a major stimulus to this paper. In 1954 he commented on the “well-known fact that the likely contacts of two individuals who are closely acquainted tend to be more overlapping than those of two arbitrarily selected individuals” (p. 75). His and Horvath’s 1961 hypothesis is even closer to mine: “one would expect the friendship relations, and therefore the overlap bias of the acquaintance circles, to become less tight with increasing numerical rank-order” (p. 290). (I.e., best friend, second-best friend, third-best, etc.) Their development of this hypothesis, however, is quite different, substantively and mathematically, from mine (Rapoport 1953a, 1953b, 1954, 1963; Rapoport and Horvath 1961).
6^{6} 拉波波特及其同事的模型和实验对本文的撰写起到了重要的启发作用。1954 年，他评论道：“众所周知，两个关系密切的人之间的潜在联系往往比两个任意选择的个体之间的潜在联系更为重叠”（第 75 页）。他和霍瓦特在 1961 年提出的假设与我的假设更为接近：“人们会预期，随着朋友等级顺序的提高，友谊关系（以及由此产生的熟人圈子的重叠偏差）会变得不那么紧密”（第 290 页）。（例如，最好的朋友、第二好的朋友、第三好的朋友等等。）然而，他们对这一假设的阐述在实质和数学上都与我的假设截然不同（Rapoport 1953a, 1953b, 1954, 1963; Rapoport and Horvath 1961）。

Some evidence exists for this absence. Analyzing 651 sociograms, Davis (1970, p. 845) found that in 90% of them triads consisting of two mutual choices and one nonchoice occurred less than the expected random number of times. If we assume that mutual choice indicates a strong tie, this is strong evidence in the direction of my argument. 7^{7} Newcomb (1961, pp. 160–65) reports that in triads consisting of dyads expressing mutual “high attraction,” the configuration of three strong ties became increasingly frequent as people knew one another longer and better; the frequency of the triad pictured in figure 1 is not analyzed, but it is implied that processes of cognitive balance tended to eliminate it.
一些证据表明这种现象并不存在。戴维斯（Davis，1970，第 845 页）分析了 651 个社会关系图，发现其中 90%的图中，由两个相互选择和一个非选择组成的三元组出现的次数少于预期随机次数。如果我们假设相互选择表明存在强关系，那么这为我的论点提供了强有力的证据。纽科姆（Newcomb，1961，第 160-165 页）报告说，在由相互“高度吸引”的二元组构成的三元组中，随着人们彼此了解的时间更长、程度更深，三个强关系的组合出现的频率越来越高；图 1 所示的三元组的频率并未进行分析，但暗示认知平衡过程倾向于消除这种组合。

The significance of this triad’s absence can be shown by using the concept of a “bridge”; this is a line in a network which provides the only path between two points (Harary, Norman, and Cartwright 1965, p. 198). Since, in general, each person has a great many contacts, a bridge between A and B provides the only route along which information or influence can flow from any contact of A to any contact of B, and, consequently, from anyone connected indirectly to A to anyone connected indirectly to B. Thus, in the study of diffusion, we can expect bridges to assume an important role.
我们可以运用“桥梁”的概念来阐释这一三元组缺失的意义；桥梁是网络中连接两点的唯一路径（Harary、Norman 和 Cartwright，1965，第 198 页）。由于每个人通常都拥有大量的联系人，A 和 B 之间的桥梁提供了信息或影响从 A 的任何联系人流向 B 的任何联系人的唯一途径，进而，也使得信息或影响能够从与 A 间接相关的任何人流向与 B 间接相关的任何人。因此，在信息传播的研究中，我们可以预期桥梁将扮演重要的角色。

Now, if the stipulated triad is absent, it follows that, except under unlikely conditions, no strong tie is a bridge. Consider the strong tie A-B: if A has another strong tie to C, then forbidding the triad of figure 1 implies that a tie exists between C and B, so that the path A-C-B exists between A and B; hence, A-B is not a bridge. A strong tie can be a bridge, therefore, only if neither party to it has any other strong ties, unlikely in a social network of any size (though possible in a small group). Intuitively speaking, this means that whatever is to be diffused can reach a larger number of people, and traverse greater social distance (i.e., path length), 10^{10} when passed through weak ties rather than strong. If one tells a rumor to all his close friends, and they do likewise, many will hear the rumor a second and third time, since those linked by strong ties tend to share friends. If the motivation to spread the rumor is dampened a bit on each wave of retelling, then the rumor moving through strong ties is much more likely to be limited to a few cliques than that going via weak ones; bridges will not be crossed. 11^{11}
直观地说，这意味着任何待传播的信息，如果通过弱关系而非强关系传播，就能触及更多的人，跨越更大的社会距离（即路径长度） 10^{10} 。如果一个人把谣言告诉所有亲密的朋友，而他们也同样告诉了，那么很多人会第二次、第三次听到这个谣言，因为强关系中的人往往有共同的朋友。如果每次重复传播谣言的动机有所减弱，那么通过强关系传播的谣言就更有可能局限于少数几个小圈子，而不是通过弱关系传播；谣言不会越过任何障碍。 11^{11}

Since sociologists and anthropologists have carried out many hundreds of diffusion studies—Rogers’s 1962 review dealt with 506—one might suppose that the above claims could easily be put to test. But this is not so, for several reasons. To begin with, though most diffusion studies find that personal contacts are crucial, many undertake no sociometric investigation. (Rogers [1962] discusses this point.) When sociometric techniques are used, they tend to discourage the naming of those weakly tied to the respondent by sharply limiting the numbers of choices allowed. Hence, the proposed importance of weak ties in diffusion is not measured. Even when more sociometric information is collected there is almost never an attempt to directly retrace the exact interpersonal paths traversed by an (idea, rumor, or) innovation. More commonly, the time when each individual adopted the innovation is recorded, as is the number of sociometric choices he received from others in the study. Those receiving many choices are characterized as “central,” those with few as “marginal”; this variable is then correlated with time of adoption and inferences made about what paths were probably followed by the innovation.
由于社会学家和人类学家已经开展了数百项扩散研究——罗杰斯 1962 年的综述就涉及了 506 项——人们可能会认为上述论断很容易得到验证。但事实并非如此，原因有几个。首先，尽管大多数扩散研究发现人际关系至关重要，但许多研究并未进行社会计量学调查。（罗杰斯[1962]讨论了这一点。）当使用社会计量学技术时，它们往往会通过严格限制选项数量来阻止受访者提及与自己联系较弱的人。因此，弱关系在扩散中的重要性并未得到衡量。即使收集了更多的社会计量学信息，也几乎从未尝试直接追溯（思想、谣言或）创新所经历的确切人际传播路径。更常见的情况是，记录每个人接受创新的时间，以及他从研究中的其他人那里获得的社会计量学选择的数量。那些获得较多选择的人被定义为“核心”，那些获得较少选择的人被定义为“边缘”。然后将该变量与采用时间进行关联，并推断创新可能遵循的路径。

One point of controversy in diffusion studies can be related to my argument. Some have indicated that early innovators are marginal, that they “underconform to norms to such a degree that they are perceived as highly deviant” (Rogers 1962, p. 197). Others (e.g., Coleman, Katz, and Menzel [1966] on the adoption of a new drug by doctors) find that those named more frequently adopt an innovation substantially earlier. Becker (1970) tries to resolve the question of whether early innovators are “central” or “marginal” by referring to the “perceived risks of adoption of a given innovation.” His study of public health innovations shows that when a new program is thought relatively safe and uncontroversial (as with the drug of Coleman et al.), central figures lead in its adoption; otherwise, marginal ones do (p. 273). He explains the difference in terms of a greater desire of “central” figures to protect their professional reputation.
扩散研究中的一个争议点与我的论点相关。一些研究指出，早期创新者往往处于边缘地位，他们“对规范的违背程度之高，以至于被视为高度离经叛道”（Rogers 1962，第 197 页）。另一些研究（例如，Coleman、Katz 和 Menzel [1966] 对医生采用新药的研究）则发现，那些被提及的创新者往往更早地采用创新。Becker (1970) 试图通过考察“采用特定创新的感知风险”来解决早期创新者是“核心”还是“边缘”的问题。他对公共卫生创新的研究表明，当一项新方案被认为相对安全且无争议时（例如 Coleman 等人研究的药物），核心人物会引领其采用；反之，边缘人物则会引领（第 273 页）。他用“核心”人物更渴望维护自身职业声誉这一理由来解释这种差异。

Kerckhoff, Back, and Miller (1965) reach a similar conclusion in a different type of study. A Southern textile plant had been swept by “hysterical contagion”: a few, then more and more workers, claiming bites from a mysterious “insect,” became nauseous, numb, and weak, leading to a plant shutdown. When the affected workers were asked to name their three best friends, many named one another, but the very earliest to be stricken were social isolates, receiving almost no choices. An explanation, compatible with Becker’s, is offered: since the symptoms might be thought odd, early “adopters” were likely to be found among the marginal, those less subject to social pressures. Later, “it is increasingly likely that some persons who are socially integrated will be affected. . . . The contagion enters social networks and is disseminated with increasing rapidity” (p. 13). This is consistent with Rogers’s comment that while the first adopters of innovations are marginal, the next group, “early adopters,” “are a more integrated part of the local social system than the innovators” (1962, p. 183).
克尔克霍夫、巴克和米勒（1965）在一项不同类型的研究中也得出了类似的结论。南方一家纺织厂爆发了一场“歇斯底里传染病”：一些工人声称被一种神秘的“昆虫”叮咬，随后越来越多的工人出现恶心、麻木和虚弱的症状，最终导致工厂停工。当被感染的工人被要求说出他们最好的三个朋友时，许多人互相提及，但最早感染的却是那些与社会隔绝的人，他们几乎没有选择余地。他们提出了一种与贝克尔的观点相符的解释：由于这些症状可能被认为是奇怪的，因此早期的“感染者”很可能来自边缘群体，那些较少受到社会压力的人。之后，“一些融入社会的人也越来越有可能受到影响……这种传染病进入社交网络，并以越来越快的速度传播”（第 13 页）。这与罗杰斯的评论一致，即虽然创新的第一批采用者是边缘群体，但下一组“早期采用者”“比创新者更融入当地社会系统”（1962 年，第 183 页）。

“Central” and “marginal” individuals may well be motivated as claimed; but if the marginal are genuinely so, it is difficult to see how they can ever spread innovations successfully. We may surmise that since the resistance to a risky or deviant activity is greater than to a safe or normal one, a larger number of people will have to be exposed to it and adopt it, in the early stages, before it will spread in a chain reaction. Individuals with many weak ties are, by my arguments, best placed to diffuse such a difficult innovation, since some of those ties will be local bridges. 12^{12} An initially unpopular innovation spread by those with few weak ties is more likely to be confined to a few cliques, thus being stillborn and never finding its way into a diffusion study.
“核心”和“边缘”个体或许确实如其所言，具有一定的动机；但如果边缘个体真的如此，很难想象他们如何能够成功传播创新。我们可以推测，由于人们对冒险或离经叛道的行为的抵触情绪大于对安全或正常行为的抵触情绪，因此在早期阶段，需要更多的人接触并接受这种行为，才能引发连锁反应。根据我的论证，拥有众多弱关系的人最适合传播这种难以推广的创新，因为其中一些关系可以起到局部桥梁的作用。 12^{12} 最初不受欢迎的创新如​​果由那些弱关系较少的人传播，则更有可能局限于少数小圈子，从而胎死腹中，最终无法被纳入扩散研究。

That the “marginal” innovators of diffusion studies might actually be rich in weak ties is possible, given the usual sociometric technique, but in most cases this is purely speculative. Kerckhoff and Back, however, in a later more detailed analysis of the hysteria incident, indicate that besides asking about one’s “three best friends,” they also asked with whom workers ate, worked, shared car pools, etc. They report that five of the six workers earliest affected “are social isolates when friendship choices are used as the basis of analysis. Only 1 of the 6 is mentioned as a friend by anyone in our sample. This is made even more striking when we note that these 6 women are mentioned with considerable frequency when other bases for choice are used. In fact, they are chosen more frequently on a ‘non-friendship’ basis than are the women in any of the other categories” (1968, p. 112).
鉴于通常的社会计量学方法，扩散研究中那些“边缘”创新者实际上可能拥有丰富的弱关系，这并非不可能，但在大多数情况下，这纯粹是推测。然而，克尔克霍夫和巴克在后来对歇斯底里事件进行更详细的分析时指出，除了询问受访者的“三个最好的朋友”之外，他们还询问了受访者与谁一起吃饭、工作、拼车等等。他们报告说，最早受影响的六名员工中有五名“如果以友谊选择作为分析基础，则属于社会孤立者。在我们的样本中，只有一人被提及为朋友。当我们注意到，当使用其他选择基础时，这六名女性被提及的频率相当高，这一点就更加引人注目了。事实上，她们在‘非友谊’基础上被选择的频率比其他任何类别中的女性都要高”（1968，第 112 页）。

This finding lends credence to the weak-tie argument, but is inconclusive. A somewhat different kind of diffusion study offers more direct support: the “small-world” investigations of Milgram and his associates. The name of these studies stems from the typical comment of newly introduced individuals who discover some common acquaintance; this situation is generalized in an attempt to measure, for arbitrarily chosen pairs of individuals in the United States, how long a path of personal contacts would be needed to connect them. A booklet is given to randomly designated senders who are asked to forward it toward some named target person, via someone the sender knows personally who would be more likely than himself to know the target. The new recipient then advances the booklet similarly; eventually it reaches the target or someone fails to send it on. The proportion of such chains completed has ranged from 12% to 33% in different studies, and the number of links in completed chains has ranged from two to 10, averaging between five and eight (Milgram 1967; Travers and Milgram 1969; Korte and Milgram 1970).
这一发现为弱关系论提供了佐证，但尚无定论。另一种扩散研究则提供了更直接的支持：米尔格拉姆及其同事的“小世界”实验。这类研究的名称源于初次见面的人发现彼此有共同熟人的典型对话；研究人员将这种情况推广开来，试图测量在美国任意选取的几对人，需要多长的私人联系路径才能将他们联系起来。他们随机指定一些寄件人，并要求他们将小册子转发给指定的目标人物，转发人必须通过自己认识的人，并且这个人比寄件人更有可能认识目标人物。新的接收者随后以类似的方式传递小册子；最终，小册子要么到达目标人物手中，要么无人转发。不同研究中，此类链的完成比例从 12% 到 33% 不等，已完成链的链数从 2 到 10 不等，平均为 5 到 8 个（Milgram 1967；Travers 和 Milgram 1969；Korte 和 Milgram 1970）。

Another relevant study, by Rapoport and Horvath (1961), is not exactly one of diffusion but is closely related in that it traces out paths along which diffusion could take place. They asked each individual in a Michigan junior high school N=851N = 851 to list his eight best friends in order of preference. Then, taking a number of random samples from the group (sample size, an arbitrary number, was nine), they traced out, for each sample, and averaged over all the samples, the total number of people reached by following along the network of first and second choices. That is, the first and second choices of each sample member were tabulated, then the first and second choices of these people were added in, etc., counting, at each remove, only names not previously chosen, and continuing until no new people were reached. The same procedure was followed using second and third choices, third and fourth, etc., up to seventh and eighth. (The theoretical connection of this tracing procedure to diffusion is discussed by Rapoport [1953a, 1953b, and especially 1954].)

以中心为中心的网络中的弱关系

In this section and the next, I want to discuss the general significance of the above findings and arguments at two levels: first that of individuals, then that of communities. These discussions make no pretense of being comprehensive; they are meant only to illustrate possible applications.
在本节和下一节中，我想从两个层面探讨上述发现和论点的普遍意义：首先是个人层面，其次是社群层面。这些讨论并非旨在面面俱到，而仅仅是为了说明其可能的应用。

In recent years, a great deal of literature has appeared analyzing the impact on the behavior of individuals of the social networks in which they are imbedded. Some of the studies have emphasized the ways in which behavior is shaped and constrained by one’s network (Bott 1957; Mayer 1961; Frankenberg 1965), others the ways in which individuals can manipulate these networks to achieve specific goals (Mayer 1966; Boissevain 1968; Kapferer 1969). Both facets are generally supposed to be affected by the structure of one’s network. Bott argued that the crucial variable is that of whether one’s friends tend to know one another (“close-knit” network) or not (“loose-knit” network). Barnes makes this dichotomy into a continuous variable by counting the number of ties observed in the network formed by ego and his friends and dividing it by the ratio of possible ones; this then corresponds to what is often called network “density” (Barnes 1969; Tilly 1969). 14^{14}
近年来，大量文献分析了个人所处的社交网络对其行为的影响。一些研究强调了行为如何受到社交网络的塑造和制约（Bott 1957; Mayer 1961; Frankenberg 1965），另一些研究则强调了个人如何操纵这些网络以实现特定目标（Mayer 1966; Boissevain 1968; Kapferer 1969）。这两个方面通常都被认为会受到社交网络结构的影响。Bott 认为，关键变量在于朋友之间是否彼此认识（“紧密型”网络）或不认识（“松散型”网络）。Barnes 通过统计自我及其朋友构成的网络中观察到的关系数量，并将其除以可能关系的数量，将这种二分法转化为一个连续变量。这就对应于通常所说的网络“密度”（Barnes 1969；Tilly 1969）。 14^{14}

Epstein (1969) points out, however, that different parts of ego’s network may have different density. He calls those with whom one “interacts most intensely and most regularly, and who are therefore also likely to come to know one another,” the “effective network”; the “remainder constitute the extended network” (pp. 110–11). This is close to saying, in my terms, that one’s strong ties form a dense network, one’s weak ties a less dense one. I would add that one’s weak ties which are not local bridges might as well be counted with the strong ties, to maximize separation of the dense from the less dense network sectors.
然而，爱泼斯坦（1969）指出，自我网络的不同部分可能具有不同的密度。他将那些“互动最频繁、最密切，因而也最有可能彼此认识”的人称为“有效网络”；“其余部分构成扩展网络”（第 110-111 页）。在我看来，这相当于说，一个人的强关系构成了一个密集网络，而弱关系则构成了一个密度较低的网络。我还要补充一点，那些并非局部桥梁的弱关系也可以被计入强关系中，以便最大限度地将密集网络和密度较低的网络区分开来。

One point on which there is no general agreement is whether ego’s network should be treated as composed only of those to whom he is tied directly, or should include the contacts of his contacts, and/or others. Analyses stressing encapsulation of an individual by his network tend to take the former position, those stressing manipulation of networks, the latter, since information or favors available through direct contacts may depend on who their contacts are. I would argue that by dividing ego’s network into that part made up of strong and nonbridging weak ties on the one hand, and that of bridging weak ties on the other, both orientations can be dealt with. Ties in the former part should tend to be to people who not only know one another, but who also have few contacts not tied to ego as well. In the “weak” sector, however, not only will ego’s contacts not be tied to one another, but they will be tied to individuals not tied to ego. Indirect contacts are thus typically reached through ties in this sector; such ties are then of importance not only in ego’s manipulation of networks, but also in that they are the channels through which ideas, influences, or information socially distant from ego may reach him. The fewer indirect contacts one has the more encapsulated he will be in terms of knowledge of the world beyond his own friendship circle; thus, bridging weak ties (and the consequent indirect contacts) are important in both ways.
关于自我的人际网络究竟应该仅由与其直接相关的人组成，还是应该包括其联系人的联系人，以及/或者其他人，目前尚无定论。强调个人被其人际网络所包围的分析倾向于前者，而强调人际网络操控的分析则倾向于后者，因为通过直接联系获得的信息或好处可能取决于其联系人是谁。我认为，通过将自我的人际网络分为两部分：一部分是由强关系和非桥接弱关系构成的，另一部分是由桥接弱关系构成的，就可以兼顾这两种观点。前一部分中的联系人不仅彼此认识，而且与自我没有直接联系的人也很少。然而，在“弱”关系部分，自我的联系人不仅彼此之间没有直接联系，而且还与那些与自我没有直接联系的人有联系。因此，间接联系通常是通过这一部分中的关系建立的。因此，这类关系不仅对自我操控人际网络至关重要，而且也是将与自我社会距离较远的思想、影响或信息传递给自我的渠道。一个人的间接联系越少，他对自身朋友圈之外的世界的认知就越封闭；因此，建立和维系弱关系（以及由此产生的间接联系）在这两方面都至关重要。

I will develop this point empirically by citing some results from a labor-market study I have recently completed. Labor economists have long been aware that American blue-collar workers find out about new jobs more through personal contacts than by any other method. (Many studies are reviewed by Parnes 1954, chap. 5.) Recent studies suggest that this is also true for those in professional, technical, and managerial positions (Shapero, Howell, and Tombaugh 1965; Brown 1967; Granovetter 1970). My study of this question laid special emphasis on the nature of the tie between the job changer and the contact person who provided the necessary information.
我将通过引用我近期完成的一项劳动力市场研究的一些结果，从实证角度阐述这一观点。劳动经济学家早已意识到，美国蓝领工人获取新工作机会的主要途径是人脉关系，而非其他任何方式。（帕内斯 (Parnes) 1954 年著作第五章回顾了许多相关研究。）近期研究表明，专业、技术和管理岗位的人员也同样如此（Shapero、Howell 和 Tombaugh 1965；Brown 1967；Granovetter 1970）。我对这一问题的研究特别关注了求职者与提供必要信息的联系人之间的联系性质。

In a random sample of recent professional, technical, and managerial job changers living in a Boston suburb, I asked those who found a new job through contacts how often they saw the contact around the time that he passed on job information to them. I will use this as a measure of tie strength. 15^{15} A natural a priori idea is that those with whom one has strong ties are more motivated to help with job information. Opposed to this greater motivation are the structural arguments I have been making: those to whom we are weakly tied are more likely to move in circles different from our own and will thus have access to information different from that which we receive.
在波士顿郊区，我随机抽取了一批近期更换过专业、技术和管理岗位的求职者，询问那些通过人脉找到新工作的人，在对方提供工作信息前后，他们与这位人脉保持联系的频率。我将以此作为衡量人脉关系强度的指标。 15^{15} 一个自然而然的先验观点是，与自己关系密切的人更有动力提供工作信息。与这种更强的动力相反的是，我一直在提出的结构性论点：与我们关系较弱的人更有可能身处与我们不同的社交圈，因此他们能够获取的信息也与我们所获得的信息不同。

I have used the following categories for frequency of contact: often = at least twice a week; occasionally = more than once a year but less than twice a week; rarely = once a year or less. Of those finding a job through contacts, 16.7% reported that they saw their contact often at the time, 55.6% said occasionally, and 27.8% rarely (N = 54). 16^{16} The skew is clearly to the weak end of the continuum, suggesting the primacy of structure over motivation.
我使用以下类别来划分联系频率：经常 = 至少每周两次；偶尔 = 每年超过一次但少于每周两次；很少 = 每年一次或更少。在通过人脉找到工作的人中，16.7% 的人表示当时经常见到联系人，55.6% 的人表示偶尔见到，27.8% 的人表示很少见到（N = 54）。 16^{16} 显然，这种偏斜现象倾向于联系频率较低的一端，表明结构比动机更为重要。

In many cases, the contact was someone only marginally included in the current network of contacts, such as an old college friend or a former workmate or employer, with whom sporadic contact had been maintained (Granovetter 1970, pp. 76–80). Usually such ties had not even been very strong when first forged. For work-related ties, respondents almost invariably said that they never saw the person in a nonwork context. 17^{17} Chance meetings or mutual friends operated to reactivate such ties. It is remarkable that people receive crucial information from individuals whose very existence they have forgotten. 18^{18}
在许多情况下，联系人只是当前人脉网络中的边缘人物，例如老同学、前同事或前雇主，彼此之间仅保持着零星联系（Granovetter 1970，第 76-80 页）。通常，这些关系在最初建立时并不牢固。对于工作相关的关系，受访者几乎无一例外地表示，他们从未在工作以外的场合见过对方。 17^{17} 偶然的相遇或共同的朋友促成了这些关系的重新激活。令人惊讶的是，人们竟然能从那些他们早已遗忘的人那里获得关键信息。 18^{18}

I also asked respondents where their contacts got the information they transmitted. In most cases, I traced the information to its initial source. I had expected that, as in the diffusion of rumors or diseases, long paths would be involved. But in 39.1% of the cases information came directly from the prospective employer, whom the respondent already knew; 45.3% said that there was one intermediary between himself and the employer; 12.5% reported two; and 3.1% more than two (N = 64). This suggests that for some important purposes it may be sufficient to discuss, as I have, the egocentric network made up of ego, his contacts, and their contacts. Had long information paths been involved, large numbers might have found out about any given job, and no particular tie would have been crucial. Such a model of job-information flow actually does correspond to the economists’ model of a “perfect” labor market. But those few who did acquire information through paths with more than one intermediary tended to be young and under the threat of unemployment; influence was much less likely to have been exerted by their contact on their behalf. These respondents were, in fact, more similar to those using formal intermediaries (agencies, advertisements) than to those hearing through short paths: both of the former are badly placed and dissatisfied in the labor market, and both receive information without influence. Just as reading about a job in the newspaper affords one no recommendation in applying for it, neither does it to have heard about it fifth hand.
我还询问了受访者，他们的联系人是从哪里获得他们所传递的信息的。在大多数情况下，我都能追溯到信息的最初来源。我原本预期，就像谣言或疾病的传播一样，信息传播会涉及很长的路径。但在 39.1%的案例中，信息直接来自受访者已经认识的潜在雇主；45.3%的受访者表示，他们与雇主之间有一个中间人；12.5%的受访者表示有两个中间人；3.1%的受访者表示有两个以上的中间人（N=64）。这表明，对于某些重要的目的而言，或许只需像我一样，讨论由自我、其联系人及其联系人构成的以自我为中心的网络即可。如果信息传播涉及很长的路径，那么任何特定的工作机会都可能被很多人知道，任何特定的关系都不会至关重要。这种工作信息流动模式实际上与经济学家提出的“完美”劳动力市场模型相符。但是，那些通过多个中间人获取信息的少数人往往是年轻人，并且面临失业的风险。他们通过人脉关系获得信息的可能性要小得多。事实上，这些受访者更类似于那些使用正式中介机构（中介、广告）的人，而不是那些通过非正式渠道获取信息的人：前者在劳动力市场中处境不佳且不满，而且两者都是在不受任何影响的情况下获得信息。正如在报纸上看到招聘信息并不能为申请该职位提供任何推荐一样，通过他人转述的信息也同样如此。

The usual dichotomy between “formal” or mass procedures and diffusion through personal contacts may thus be invalid in some cases where, instead, the former may be seen as a limiting case of long diffusion chains. This is especially likely where information of instrumental significance is involved. Such information is most valuable when earmarked for one person.
因此，通常将信息传播方式二分为“正式”或大规模传播与通过人脉关系传播可能并不成立，在某些情况下，前者反而可以被视为长传播链的一种极端情况。当信息具有工具性意义时，这种情况尤其可能发生。此类信息在专门针对特定人员时最为有价值。

From the individual’s point of view, then, weak ties are an important resource in making possible mobility opportunity. Seen from a more macroscopic vantage, weak ties play a role in effecting social cohesion. When a man changes jobs, he is not only moving from one network of ties to another, but also establishing a link between these. Such a link is often of the same kind which facilitated his own movement. Especially within professional and technical specialties which are well defined and limited in size, this mobility sets up elaborate structures of bridging weak ties between the more coherent clusters that constitute operative networks in particular locations. Information and ideas thus flow more easily through the specialty, giving it some “sense of community,” activated at meetings and conventions. Maintenance of weak ties may well be the most important consequence of such meetings.
因此，从个人角度来看，弱关系是实现流动机会的重要资源。从更宏观的角度来看，弱关系在促进社会凝聚力方面发挥着作用。当一个人更换工作时，他不仅是从一个关系网络转移到另一个关系网络，而且还在这两个网络之间建立联系。这种联系通常与促成他自身流动的那种联系相同。尤其是在那些界限分明、规模有限的专业技术领域，这种流动性会在特定地点形成复杂的桥梁式弱关系网络，连接起各个更具凝聚力的群体，从而构建起有效的运作网络。信息和思想因此更容易在专业领域内流动，赋予其某种“社群感”，这种社群感会在会议和研讨会上得到激活。维护弱关系很可能是此类会议最重要的成果。

WEAK TIES AND COMMUNITY ORGANIZATION
弱关系与社区组织

These comments about sense of community may remind us that in many cases it is desirable to deal with a unit of analysis larger than a single individual. I would like to develop my argument further by analyzing, in this section, why some communities organize for common goals easily and effectively whereas others seem unable to mobilize resources, even against dire threats. The Italian community of Boston’s West End, for example, was unable to even form an organization to fight against the “urban renewal” which ultimately destroyed it. This seems especially anomalous in view of Gans’s description of West End social structure as cohesive (1962).
这些关于社群意识的评论或许提醒我们，在许多情况下，分析的单位应该大于单个个体。在本节中，我想进一步阐述我的观点，分析为什么有些社群能够轻松有效地组织起来实现共同目标，而另一些社群即使面对严峻的威胁，似乎也无法调动资源。例如，波士顿西区的意大利社群甚至无法组建组织来对抗最终摧毁他们的“城市更新”。考虑到甘斯（Gans）在 1962 年对西区社会结构的描述——其社会结构具有凝聚力——这种情况显得尤为反常。

Variations in culture and personality are often cited to explain such anomalies. Gans contrasts “lower”-, “working”-, and “middle”-class subcultures, concluding that only the last provides sufficient trust in leaders and practice in working toward common goals to enable formation of an effective organization. Thus, the working-class West End could not resist urban renewal (pp. 229–304). Yet, numerous well-documented cases show that some working-class communities have mobilized quite successfully against comparable or lesser threats (Dahl 1961, pp. 192–99; Keyes 1969; Davies 1966, chap. 4). 19^{19} I would suggest, as a sharper analytical tool, examination of the network of ties comprising a community to see whether aspects of its structure might facilitate or block organization.
人们常常援引文化和人格差异来解释这类反常现象。甘斯对比了“下层”、“工人”和“中产”阶级亚文化，得出结论：只有中产阶级才能提供足够的领导者信任和为共同目标而努力的实践经验，从而形成有效的组织。因此，工人阶级聚居的西区无法抵制城市更新（第 229-304 页）。然而，大量有据可查的案例表明，一些工人阶级社区已经成功地动员起来，抵御了类似或更小的威胁（达尔，1961 年，第 192-199 页；基斯，1969 年；戴维斯，1966 年，第四章）。 19^{19} 我建议，作为一种更精准的分析工具，可以考察构成社区的各种关系网络，看看其结构中的哪些方面会促进或阻碍组织。

The problem of trust is closely related. I would propose that whether a person trusts a given leader depends heavily on whether there exist intermediary personal contacts who can, from their own knowledge, assure him that the leader is trustworthy, and who can, if necessary, intercede with the leader or his lieutenants on his behalf. Trust in leaders is integrally related to the capacity to predict and affect their behavior. Leaders, for their part, have little motivation to be responsive or even trustworthy toward those to whom they have no direct or indirect connection. MICRO AND MACRO NETWORK MODELS
微观和宏观网络模型

Unlike most models of interpersonal networks, the one presented here is not meant primarily for application to small, face-to-face groups or to groups in confined institutional or organizational settings. Rather, it is meant for linkage of such small-scale levels with one another and with larger, more amorphous ones. This is why emphasis here has been placed more on weak ties than on strong. Weak ties are more likely to link members of different small groups than are strong ones, which tend to be concentrated within particular groups.
与大多数人际网络模型不同，本文提出的模型并非主要针对小型面对面群体或封闭的机构或组织环境中的群体。相反，它旨在将这些小规模群体彼此连接，并与更大、更模糊的群体联系起来。因此，本文更侧重于弱关系而非强关系。弱关系比强关系更有可能连接不同小群体的成员，而强关系则往往集中在特定群体内部。

For this reason, my discussion does not lend itself to elucidation of the internal structure of small groups. This point can be made more clearly by contrasting the model of this paper to one with which it shares many similarities, that of James Davis, Paul Holland, and Samuel Leinhardt (hereafter, the DHL model) (Davis 1970; Davis and Leinhardt 1971; Holland and Leinhardt 1970, 1971a, 1971b; Davis, Holland, and Leinhardt 1971; Leinhardt 1972). The authors, inspired by certain propositions in George Homans’s The Human Group (1950), argue that “the central proposition in structural sociometry is this: Interpersonal choices tend to be transitive—if P chooses O and O chooses X, then P is likely to choose X” (Davis et al. 1971, p. 309). When this is true without exception, a sociogram can be divided into cliques in which every individual chooses every other; any asymmetric choices or nonchoices are between such cliques, and asymmetry, if present, runs only in one direction. A partial ordering of cliques may thus be inferred. If mutual choice implies equal, and asymmetric choice unequal, status, then this ordering reflects the stratification structure of the group (Holland and Leinhardt 1971a, pp. 107–14).
因此，我的讨论并不适用于阐明小群体的内部结构。这一点可以通过将本文的模型与詹姆斯·戴维斯、保罗·霍兰德和塞缪尔·莱因哈特（以下简称 DHL 模型）的模型进行对比来更清晰地说明，因为 DHL 模型与本文的模型有很多相似之处（Davis 1970; Davis and Leinhardt 1971; Holland and Leinhardt 1970, 1971a, 1971b; Davis, Holland, and Leinhardt 1971; Leinhardt 1972）。受乔治·霍曼斯在《人类群体》（1950）一书中某些命题的启发，作者认为“结构社会计量学的核心命题是：人际选择往往具有传递性——如果 P 选择 O，而 O 选择 X，那么 P 很可能选择 X”（Davis et al. 1971, p. 309）。当这种情况无一例外地成立时，社会关系图可以被划分为若干个小团体，每个小团体中的每个人都会选择其他所有成员；任何不对称的选择或不选择都发生在这些小团体之间，而且不对称性（如果存在）也仅是单向的。由此可以推断出小团体的局部排序。如果相互选择意味着地位平等，而不对称选择意味着地位不平等，那么这种排序就反映了群体的分层结构（Holland and Leinhardt 1971a, pp. 107–14）。

One immediate difference between this model and mine is that it is cast in terms of “choices” rather than ties. Most sociometric tests ask people whom they like best or would prefer to do something with, rather than with whom they actually spend time. If transitivity is built more into our cognitive than our social structure, this method might overstate its prevalence. But since the DHL model could recast in terms of ties, this is not a conclusive difference.
这个模型与我的模型的一个直接区别在于，它基于“选择”而非关系来构建模型。大多数社会测量测试询问人们最喜欢谁或更愿意与谁一起做某事，而不是他们实际与谁共度时光。如果传递性更多地体现在我们的认知结构而非社会结构中，那么这种方法可能会高估其普遍性。但由于 DHL 模型可以重新构建为基于关系的模型，因此这并非决定性的差异。

O and O choose P): then I assert that transitivity—P choosing X (or X, P)—is most likely when both ties—P-O and O-X—are strong, least likely when both are weak, and of intermediate probability if one is strong and one weak. Transitivity, then, is claimed to be a function of the strength of ties, rather than a general feature of social structure.
如果 O 和 O 选择 P），那么我认为，当 PO 和 OX 这两个关系都很强时，传递性（即 P 选择 X（或 X，P））的可能性最大；当这两个关系都很弱时，传递性的可能性最小；而当一个关系强而另一个关系弱时，传递性的概率则介于两者之间。因此，传递性被认为是关系强度的函数，而不是社会结构的一般特征。

The justification of this assertion is, in part, identical with that offered earlier for the triad designated A-B-C. In addition, it is important to point out here that the DHL model was designed for small groups, and with increasing size of the group considered the rationale for transitivity weakens. If P chooses O and O chooses X, P should choose X out of consistency; but if P does not know or barely knows X, nonchoice implies no inconsistency. For the logic of transitivity to apply, a group must be small enough so that any person knows enough about every other person to be able to decide whether to “choose” him, and encounters him often enough that he feels the need for such a decision. Including weak ties in my model, then, lessens the expectation of transitivity and permits analysis of intergroup relationships and also of amorphous chunks of social structure which an analyst may ferret out as being of interest, but which are not easily defined in terms of face-to-face groups. Anthropologists have recently referred to such chunks as “quasi-groups” (Mayer 1966; Boissevain 1968).
这一论断的合理性部分与之前针对 ABC 三元组的论证相同。此外，需要指出的是，DHL 模型是为小型群体设计的，随着群体规模的扩大，传递性的合理性会减弱。如果 P 选择 O，O 选择 X，出于一致性考虑，P 应该选择 X；但如果 P 不认识 X 或对 X 知之甚少，不选择 X 并不意味着不一致。传递性的逻辑要成立，群体必须足够小，使得任何成员都对其他成员有足够的了解，从而能够决定是否“选择”对方，并且与对方接触足够频繁，以至于感到有必要做出这样的决定。因此，在我的模型中纳入弱关系，降低了对传递性的预期，并允许分析群体间关系，以及分析分析者可能发现的、具有研究价值但难以用面对面群体来定义的模糊社会结构片段。人类学家最近将这类群体称为“准群体”（Mayer 1966；Boissevain 1968）。

Since, as I have argued above, weak ties are poorly represented in sociograms, there is little in the DHL empirical studies—which apply statistical tests to sociometric data—to confirm or disconfirm my argument on transitivity. One finding does lend itself to speculation, however. Leinhardt (1972) shows that the sociograms of schoolchildren conform more and more closely to the transitive model as they become older, sixth graders being the oldest tested. He interprets this as reflecting cognitive development—increasing capacity to make use of transitive logic. If my assertion is correct, an alternative possibility would be that children develop stronger ties with increasing age. This is consistent with some theories of child development (see especially Sullivan 1953, chap. 16) and would imply, on my argument, greater transitivity of structure. Some support for this explanation comes from Leinhardt’s finding that proportion of choices which were mutual was positively correlated with both grade level and degree of transitivity. In these sociograms, with an average of only about four choices per child, it seems likely that most mutual choices reflected strong ties (see n. 7, above).
正如我上文所述，由于社会关系图中弱关系的呈现不足，DHL 实证研究（即运用统计检验分析社会测量数据的研究）中鲜有证据能够证实或反驳我关于传递性的论点。然而，有一项发现确实引人深思。莱因哈特（Leinhardt，1972）的研究表明，随着年龄的增长，小学生的社会关系图越来越符合传递性模型，六年级学生是接受测试的年龄最大的学生。他将此解释为认知发展的结果——运用传递逻辑的能力不断增强。如果我的论断正确，那么另一种可能性是，儿童随着年龄的增长会发展出更牢固的关系。这与一些儿童发展理论相符（尤其参见 Sullivan，1953，第 16 章），并且根据我的论点，这意味着社会结构的传递性更强。莱因哈特的研究发现，相互选择的比例与年级和传递性程度均呈正相关，这为上述解释提供了一定的佐证。在这些社会关系图中，每个孩子平均只有大约四个选择，因此大多数相互选择很可能反映了牢固的关系（见上文第 7 点）。

CONCLUSION  结论

The major implication intended by this paper is that the personal experience of individuals is closely bound up with larger-scale aspects of social structure, well beyond the purview or control of particular individuals.
本文的主要含义是，个人的个人经历与社会结构的更大层面密切相关，远远超出了特定个人的视野或控制范围。

Linkage of micro and macro levels is thus no luxury but of central importance to the development of sociological theory. Such linkage generates paradoxes: weak ties, often denounced as generative of alienation (Wirth 1938) are here seen as indispensable to individuals’ opportunities and to their integration into communities; strong ties, breeding local cohesion, lead to overall fragmentation. Paradoxes are a welcome antidote to theories which explain everything all too neatly.
因此，微观层面与宏观层面的联系并非可有可无，而是社会学理论发展的核心所在。这种联系会产生悖论：弱关系常被斥为异化的根源（Wirth 1938），但在这里却被视为个人发展机遇及其融入社群的必要条件；强关系虽然能促进地方凝聚力，却会导致整体的分裂。这些悖论恰恰是对那些试图将一切解释得过于简单的理论的一种有益的反驳。

The model offered here is a very limited step in the linking of levels; it is a fragment of a theory. Treating only the strength of ties ignores, for instance, all the important issues involving their content. What is the relation between strength and degree of specialization of ties, or between strength and hierarchical structure? How can “negative” ties be handled? Should tie strength be developed as a continuous variable? What is the developmental sequence of network structure over time?
本文提出的模型只是层级连接中非常有限的一步，它只是理论的一个片段。例如，仅仅关注关系强度会忽略关系内容的所有重要问题。关系强度与关系的专业化程度之间有何关系？关系强度与层级结构之间又有何关系？如何处理“负面”关系？关系强度是否应该被视为一个连续变量？网络结构随时间推移的发展序列是怎样的？

As such questions are resolved, others will arise. Demography, coalition structure, and mobility are just a few of the variables which would be of special importance in developing micro-macro linkage with the help of network analysis; how these are related to the present discussion needs specification. My contribution here is mainly, then, exploratory and programmatic, its primary purpose being to generate interest in the proposed program of theory and research.
随着这些问题的解决，其他问题也会随之出现。人口统计、联盟结构和流动性只是众多变量中的几个，它们在借助网络分析构建微观与宏观联系的过程中尤为重要；这些变量与当前讨论的关联性尚需进一步阐明。因此，我在此的贡献主要在于探索性和方案性，其主要目的是激发人们对所提出的理论和研究方案的兴趣。
