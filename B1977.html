A Macrosociological Theory of Social Structure 1^{1}

彼得·M·布劳 哥伦比亚大学

Social structure is conceptualized as the distributions of a population among social positions in a multidimensional space of positions. This quantitative conception of social structure is the basis for a deductive theory of the macrostructure of social associations in society. The likelihood that people engage in intergroup associations under specifiable structural conditions can be deduced from analytic propositions about structural properties without any assumption about sociopsychological dispositions to establish intergroup associations, indeed, on the assumption that people prefer ingroup relations. Group size governs the probability of intergroup relations, a fact that has paradoxical implications for discrimination by a majority against a minority. Inequality impedes and heterogeneity promotes intergroup relations. The major structural condition that governs intergroup relations is the degree of connection of parameters. Intersecting parameters exert structural constraints to participate in intergroup relations; consolidated parameters impede them. The more differentiation of any kind penetrates into the substructures of society, the greater is the probability that extensive social relations integrate various segments in society.
社会结构被概念化为人口在多维社会地位空间中的分布。这种社会结构的量化概念是社会关系宏观结构演绎理论的基础。在特定的结构条件下，人们参与群体间交往的可能性可以从关于结构属性的分析命题中推断出来，而无需假设人们具有建立群体间交往的社会心理倾向，实际上，这基于人们更倾向于群体内部关系的假设。群体规模决定了群体间关系发生的概率，这一事实对多数群体歧视少数群体具有悖论性的影响。不平等阻碍群体间关系，而异质性则促进群体间关系。决定群体间关系的主要结构条件是参数之间的关联程度。交叉参数会对参与群体间关系施加结构性约束；而整合的参数则会阻碍群体间关系的发生。任何形式的分化越深入社会亚结构，广泛的社会关系就越有可能整合社会中的各个阶层。

This paper presents a deductive theory of the structure of social associations, which rests on a quantitative conception of social structure. It is inspired by Simmel (1908), the father of quantitative sociology. To be sure, Simmel did not employ quantitative methods in his work. But by quantitative sociology I mean a subject matter, not the procedures used in investigating it. Quantitative sociology is the conceptual and theoretical analysis of the quantitative dimension of social life—of the implications of the numbers and distributions of people for their social relations—and in this Simmel was a pioneer. The conception of social structure adopted is also akin to that of the British structuralists in anthropology, notably Radcliffe-Brown (1940), Evans-Pritchard (1940), and Nadel (1957), except that their concepts pertaining to the structure of small tribes are adapted to make them applicable to the macrosociological study of large societies as well.
本文提出了一种基于社会结构定量概念的演绎式社会组织结构理论。该理论的灵感来源于定量社会学之父齐美尔（1908）。诚然，齐美尔在其著作中并未采用定量方法。但我所说的定量社会学指的是研究对象，而非研究方法。定量社会学是对社会生活定量维度——即人口数量和分布对其社会关系的影响——进行概念和理论分析，而齐美尔正是这一领域的先驱。本文所采用的社会结构概念也与英国人类学结构主义者，特别是拉德克利夫-布朗（1940）、埃文斯-普里查德（1940）和纳德尔（1957）的观点相近，不同之处在于，本文对他们关于小型部落结构的概念进行了调整，使其同样适用于大型社会的宏观社会学研究。

The analysis to be presented may be described as a primitive theory of social structure, in two senses of the term. First, it is a deductive theory, in which theorems are derived from axioms or primitive propositions that logically imply them. These axioms are either analytic propositions, which are true by definition and refer to the way social structure is defined, or synthetic propositions, which are assumed to be true and rest on simple, plausible, and testable assumptions, for example, that social associations depend on opportunities for contact. Second, the theory is rooted in a primitive, rudimentary conception of social structure. The concept of social structure is confined to the distributions of people among different social positions. This is a very narrow view of social structure, which leaves out of consideration numerous broader implications and connotations of the term, such as value consensus, normative orientations, institutional systems, and functional interdependence. Not everything about social life can be explained in structural terms so narrowly conceived, but the endeavor here is to see how much can be.
本文提出的分析可以被描述为一种原始的社会结构理论，这个术语包含两层含义。首先，它是一种演绎理论，其中的定理是从逻辑上蕴含它们的公理或原始命题中推导出来的。这些公理要么是分析命题（根据定义即为真，并指社会结构的定义方式），要么是综合命题（假定为真，并基于简单、合理且可检验的假设，例如，社会关系依赖于接触机会）。其次，该理论植根于一种原始的、粗浅的社会结构概念。社会结构的概念仅限于人们在不同社会地位之间的分布。这是一种非常狭隘的社会结构观，忽略了该术语的诸多更广泛的含义和内涵，例如价值共识、规范取向、制度体系和功能相互依存。社会生活并非事事都能用如此狭隘的结构术语来解释，但这里要探讨的是，究竟有多少事可以用这种结构术语来解释。

CONCEPTUAL FRAMEWORK  概念框架

There are a variety of approaches to the study of social structure, and implicit in them are different ways of conceptualizing social structure. The focus may be on the class structure or value orientations, on networks of social relations or institutional integration, on the division of labor or the construction of social reality, on status sets and role sets or the ecosystem. Yet certain elemental properties of social structure are recognized by most social scientists, notwithstanding differences in approach and focus. Whatever else may be encompassed by social structure, it nearly always includes the concepts that there are differences in social positions, that there are social relations among these positions, and that people’s positions and corresponding roles influence their social relations. Typically, however, theories of social structure extend the concept beyond these elemental properties. Thus, Marx explains the class structure and the conflicting relations between classes on the basis of the dialectical interplay of productive forces with productive relations. Although Parsons explicitly distinguishes structures of roles and social interaction, which constitute social systems, from patterns of values and meanings, which constitute cultural systems (Parsons and Shils 1951, pp. 20–26; Kroeber and Parsons 1958), his theoretical explanation of social relations and interaction is in terms of value orientations, that is, in cultural rather than structural terms, and he acknowledges that this makes him a “cultural determinist” (Parsons 1966, p. 113). Homans’s (1961) deductive theory explains social interaction and role relations on the basis of psychological principles that govern human motivation.
社会结构的研究方法多种多样，其中蕴含着对社会结构的不同概念化方式。研究的重点可能在于阶级结构或价值取向，社会关系网络或制度整合，劳动分工或社会现实的建构，地位集合和角色集合或生态系统。然而，尽管研究方法和重点有所不同，大多数社会科学家都认可社会结构的某些基本属性。无论社会结构还包含哪些其他内容，它几乎总是包含以下概念：社会地位存在差异，这些地位之间存在社会关系，以及人们的地位和相应的角色会影响他们的社会关系。然而，通常情况下，社会结构理论会将这一概念扩展到这些基本属性之外。例如，马克思基于生产力与生产关系的辩证互动来解释阶级结构和阶级之间的冲突关系。尽管帕森斯明确区分了构成社会系统的角色结构和社会互动结构，以及构成文化系统的价值和意义模式（Parsons and Shils 1951, pp. 20–26; Kroeber and Parsons 1958），但他对社会关系和互动的理论解释却是基于价值取向，即从文化而非结构的角度出发，他也承认这使他成为了一位“文化决定论者”（Parsons 1966, p. 113）。霍曼斯（Homans 1961）的演绎理论则基于支配人类动机的心理学原理来解释社会互动和角色关系。

Macrostructure  宏观结构

Social structure is conceptualized narrowly as referring to the distributions of a population among different social positions that reflect and affect people’s relations with one another. To speak of social structure is to speak of differentiation among people. For social structures, as conceptualized, are rooted in the social distinctions people make in their role relations and social associations. These social distinctions find expression in differences in roles and positions, which in turn influence subsequent social associations. But when the structure of an entire society or community is under consideration, persons naturally occupy several social positions simultaneously, not just one; they have occupations, belong to religious groups, live in communities, work in establishments, are more or less educated, and occupy socioeconomic statuses. A population distribution exists for each type of position.
社会结构的概念通常被狭义地理解为人口在不同社会地位上的分布，这种分布反映并影响着人与人之间的关系。谈论社会结构，实际上就是谈论人与人之间的差异。因为，就其概念而言，社会结构根植于人们在角色关系和社会交往中做出的社会区分。这些社会区分体现在角色和地位的差异上，而这些差异反过来又会影响后续的社会交往。但是，当考察整个社会或社区的结构时，人们自然会同时占据多个社会地位，而不仅仅是一个；他们有职业，属于不同的宗教团体，居住在不同的社区，在不同的机构工作，受教育程度也各不相同，并且拥有不同的社会经济地位。每一种社会地位都存在着相应的人口分布。

Accordingly, the macrostructure of societies can be defined as a multidimensional space of social positions among which people are distributed and which affect their social relations. This abstract conception makes society’s macrostructure homologous to the microstructures of role relations of individuals. In both cases, formal properties of social positions and relations are abstracted from their substantive contents, notably from cultural and psychological orientations. A fundamental difference, however, is the way in which social positions and relations are defined. Two important problems the macrosociological analysis of social structure must solve are how to deal with the huge number of personal relations in a society or community and how to take into account the multiple positions persons occupy, as Laumann (1973, pp. 2–7) notes.
因此，社会宏观结构可以定义为社会地位的多维空间，人们分布于其中，并受其社会关系的影响。这种抽象概念使得社会宏观结构与个体角色关系的微观结构同源。在这两种情况下，社会地位和关系的正式属性都从其实质内容（尤其是文化和心理取向）中抽象出来。然而，一个根本区别在于社会地位和关系的定义方式。正如劳曼（Laumann，1973，第 2-7 页）所指出的，社会结构的宏观社会学分析必须解决的两个重要问题是：如何处理社会或社区中数量庞大的人际关系，以及如何考虑个人所占据的多重地位。

Microstructures are the networks of interpersonal relations anchored in individuals, as illustrated by a sociogram of links between persons in a small group. Originating in the tradition of sociometry (Moreno 1934), microstructural inquiry has given rise to three main approaches: graph theory, network analysis, and block models. Graph theory (Harary, Norman, and Cartwright 1965; Davis 1967) uses psychological assumptions and mathematical principles to derive propositions about the configurations of links that are most likely to develop. Network analysis (Barnes 1954, 1972; Mitchell 1969) dissects in detail the networks of person-to-person links in which specific individuals are involved, and the study of these microstructures anchored in individuals is explicitly juxtaposed to the macrosociological study of positions and groups in society represented by British structuralists in anthropology (Mitchell 1969, pp. 1–10). The block models constructed by White and his collaborators (White, Boorman, and Breiger 1976) divide a group into blocks of individuals with equivalent structural positions, defined on the basis of the similarity of the links of the persons in one block with those in others, which means that a block is not necessarily a subgroup of individuals who have direct links to one another. Whether microstructural studies center attention on direct links, as graph theory does, or not, like block models, they define the location of individuals in the social structure on the basis of an examination of all, potential as well as existing, person-to-person links. Such an analysis is not possible without modification for large collectivities. It is usually applied to groups of fewer than 100 persons, though procedures to apply it to groups of up to 1,000 persons have been developed (Coleman and MacRae 1960).
微观结构是指以个体为中心的各种人际关系网络，例如小群体中成员间联系的社会关系图。微观结构研究起源于社会计量学传统（Moreno 1934），并衍生出三种主要方法：图论、网络分析和块模型。图论（Harary、Norman 和 Cartwright 1965；Davis 1967）运用心理学假设和数学原理，推导出关于最有可能形成的联系结构的命题。网络分析（Barnes 1954, 1972；Mitchell 1969）详细剖析了涉及特定个体的个体间联系网络，并将这种以个体为中心的微观结构研究与英国结构主义者在人类学中代表的对社会地位和群体的宏观社会学研究明确地并置在一起（Mitchell 1969，第 1-10 页）。怀特及其合作者（White、Boorman 和 Breiger，1976）构建的块模型将群体划分为若干结构位置相同的个体块，这些块的划分依据是同一块内个体与其他块内个体之间联系的相似性。这意味着，一个块并不一定是由彼此之间存在直接联系的个体组成的子群体。无论微观结构研究是否像图论那样关注直接联系，它们都像块模型一样，通过考察所有潜在的以及已存在的个体间联系来确定个体在社会结构中的位置。对于大型群体而言，如果不进行修正，这种分析是行不通的。 它通常适用于少于 100 人的群体，尽管已经开发出将其应用于多达 1000 人的群体的程序（Coleman 和 MacRae 1960）。

Macrostructural inquiry is concerned with the patterns of social relations among different social positions occupied by many persons, not with the networks of all relations between individuals. This requires re-definitions of two central concepts, position and relation, as Laumann and Pappi (1976, pp. 18–20) point out. Whereas microstructural studies define the positions of individuals in terms of the social relations in which they are involved, the definition of social position in macrostructural analysis is initially in terms of common or similar social attributes of people, such as their religion or socioeconomic status. The social relations between positions with many incumbents are not dichotomous links that either exist or do not exist but are defined as the variable likelihood or rate of association of incumbents of one position with those of another, for example, the rate of intermarriage or the frequency of intergroup contacts. Note that this macrostructural definition of social relations rests on actual associations between persons, as the microstructural definition does—except that a variable rate is substituted for a dichotomous link—which distinguishes the structural approach from theories that focus on the relations of functions, institutions, and values.
宏观结构研究关注的是众多个体所处不同社会地位之间的社会关系模式，而非个体间所有关系的网络。正如劳曼和帕皮（1976，第 18-20 页）所指出的，这需要重新定义两个核心概念：地位和关系。微观结构研究根据个体所处的社会关系来定义其地位，而宏观结构分析中社会地位的定义则首先基于人们共同或相似的社会属性，例如宗教信仰或社会经济地位。拥有众多从业者的同一地位之间的社会关系并非非此即彼的二元对立关系，而是被定义为不同地位的从业者之间关联的可能性或频率，例如，跨群体通婚率或群体间接触的频率。请注意，这种社会关系的宏观结构定义与微观结构定义一样，都基于人与人之间的实际联系——只不过用可变比率代替了二分联系——这使得结构方法与那些关注功能、制度和价值观关系的理论区别开来。

One strategy for dealing with this problem is to select one dimension of social positions, which is considered on a priori grounds to be of special importance, and to infer from the empirical analysis of observed social relations among these positions other dimensions that affect social relations. Structural Parameters  结构参数

The structures of societies and communities are delineated by parameters (Blau 1974). Structural parameters are the axes in the multidimensional space of social positions among which the population is distributed. They are attributes of people that underlie the distinctions they themselves generally make in their social relations, such as age, race, education, and socioeconomic status. People can be classified on the basis of innumerable attributes, any of which may be a parameter. But if a classification made by an investigator does not influence social relations at all, or exerts only idiosyncratic influence on the personal relations of some individuals, it is not meaningful to consider it indicative of social positions. Hence, the double criterion of a parameter circumscribing social positions is that it is an attribute by which a population is classified and that the social relations among persons similarly classified differ on the average from the relations between persons in widely different categories. A typical difference, according to an assumption to be introduced, is that the social associations among incumbents of the same position or proximate ones are more prevalent than those between incumbents of different or distant positions. In short, a parameter is a variable that characterizes individuals and differentiates their role relations and social positions. At the same time, the distribution of the population among these positions yields a new variable that characterizes the degree of differentiation of the society in terms of the parameter. The focus of structural analysis is on the derived variables indicative of the degrees of differentiation of societies in various respects and on their implications for social life.
社会和社群的结构由参数界定（Blau 1974）。结构参数是社会地位多维空间中的坐标轴，人口就分布在这些坐标轴之间。它们是构成人们在社会关系中通常做出区分的属性，例如年龄、种族、教育程度和社会经济地位。人们可以根据无数属性进行分类，其中任何一个属性都可以成为一个参数。但是，如果研究者所做的分类完全不影响社会关系，或者只对某些个体的人际关系产生特殊影响，那么将其视为社会地位的指标就毫无意义。因此，界定社会地位的参数的双重标准是：它既是用于对人口进行分类的属性，又具有以下特点：分类相似的人群之间的社会关系平均而言与分类差异很大的人群之间的社会关系存在差异。根据即将提出的假设，一个典型的区别在于，同一职位或职位相近的在职者之间的社会联系比职位不同或职位相远的在职者之间的社会联系更为普遍。简而言之，参数是表征个体并区分其角色关系和社会地位的变量。同时，人口在这些职位上的分布产生了一个新的变量，该变量表征了社会在该参数方面的分化程度。结构分析的重点在于这些衍生变量，它们指示了社会在各个方面的分化程度及其对社会生活的影响。

The two basic types of parameters are nominal and graduated parameters. A nominal parameter divides the population into subgroups with distinct boundaries and without an inherent rank order. Sex, religion, race, and place of residence are nominal parameters. A graduated parameter differentiates people in terms of a status rank order, which is in principle continuous, so that the parameter does not draw boundaries between strata. Income, wealth, education, and power are graduated parameters. The social positions delineated by nominal parameters are designated as group memberships, those delineated by graduated parameters as status. Hence, group and status are defined very broadly; for instance, females are a group and age is a status. Groups are not confined to collectivities all of whose members associate with one another, and status is not confined to differences in deference and compliance.
参数的基本类型有两种：名义参数和分级参数。名义参数将人群划分为边界清晰、不具有内在等级顺序的子群体。性别、宗教、种族和居住地都是名义参数。分级参数则根据地位等级顺序来区分人群，这种等级顺序原则上是连续的，因此该参数不会在不同阶层之间划定界限。收入、财富、教育和权力都是分级参数。名义参数所划分的社会地位被称为群体成员身份，而分级参数所划分的社会地位被称为地位。因此，群体和地位的定义非常宽泛；例如，女性是一个群体，年龄是一种地位。群体并非仅限于所有成员都相互联系的集体，地位也并非仅限于尊重和服从程度的差异。

The two generic forms of differentiation, under which its specific forms can be subsumed, are heterogeneity and inequality. Heterogeneity refers to the population distribution in terms of a nominal parameter. The criterion of degree of heterogeneity is the probability that two randomly selected persons do not belong to the same group. For any nominal parameter, the larger the number of groups and the more evenly the population is divided among them, the greater is the heterogeneity. Thus, a community’s ethnic heterogeneity is greater if there are many than if there are few ethnic groups; but it is not so great if most people belong to one ethnic group as it is if the population is more evenly divided among several. The criterion takes both components of heterogeneity into account. 3^{3}
差异的两种通用形式是异质性和不平等，其具体形式可归于这两种形式之下。异质性指的是人口分布中某个名义参数的差异。异质性程度的衡量标准是随机选择的两个人不属于同一群体的概率。对于任何名义参数，群体数量越多，人口在各群体间的分布越均匀，异质性就越大。因此，如果一个社区的族群数量较多，其族群异质性就比族群数量较少时更大；但如果大多数人属于同一个族群，其异质性则不如人口在多个族群间分布较为均匀时那么大。该标准同时考虑了异质性的这两个组成部分。 3^{3}

Inequality pertains to the population distribution in terms of a graduated parameter. The criterion of degree of inequality is the average difference in status between any two pairs relative to average status. For example, the more the average difference in years of schooling exceeds the average number of years of schooling in a society, the greater is the inequality in formal education. Another way of looking at inequality is that it refers to the extent to which a status resource is concentrated. For instance, the more the national wealth is concentrated in the hands of the richest persons, the greater the inequality in wealth is. It turns out that these two ways of conceptualizing inequality are actually equivalent, and they are indicated by the most widely used empirical measure of inequality, the Gini coefficient. 4^{4} The former conception of inequality is meaningful for any status criterion, even when the latter is not (which discloses that the Gini index is substantively appropriate for any status criterion). It is not very meaningful to speak of the degree of concentration of years of schooling, mathematical aptitudes, or intelligence, but it is meaningful to speak of the average difference in these characteristics.
不平等是指人口分布中存在一个分级参数。不平等程度的标准是任意两对人群之间地位差异相对于平均地位的平均值。例如，一个社会中受教育年限的平均差异越大，超过该社会平均受教育年限的程度就越高，正规教育的不平等程度就越大。另一种看待不平等的方式是将其视为地位资源集中程度的体现。例如，国家财富越集中在最富有的人手中，财富不平等程度就越高。事实上，这两种不平等概念是等价的，它们都体现在最广泛使用的不平等实证指标——基尼系数上。 4^{4} 前一种不平等概念对任何地位标准都适用，即使后一种概念不适用（这表明基尼系数实质上适用于任何地位标准）。谈论受教育年限、数学能力或智力的集中程度意义不大，但谈论这些特征的平均差异是有意义的。

Parameters are not orthogonal dimensions of social structure. On the contrary, their correlations are fundamental characteristics of macrostructures of primary interest in their analysis. The correlations of a nominal parameter with graduated ones indicate status differences among groups, for example, the differences in education and income among ethnic groups. Then one can examine how the status distance between groups affects the associations between their members. 5^{5} If a nominal parameter with many categories is substantially correlated with graduated parameters, a new graduated parameter can be derived from the ranking of the categories. Thus, occupation is a nominal parameter, but classifying occupations by education and income yields an index of occupational status (Duncan 1961). Generally, the degree to which parameters intersect, or alternatively consolidate differences in social positions through their strong correlations, reflects the most important structural conditions in a society, which have crucial consequences for conflict (Coleman 1957) and for social integration.
参数并非社会结构的正交维度。相反，它们的相关性是宏观结构的基本特征，也是分析中重点关注的对象。名义参数与分级参数的相关性表明了群体间的地位差异，例如，不同族群在教育和收入方面的差异。由此可以考察群体间的地位差距如何影响其成员之间的联系。如果一个具有多个类别的名义参数与分级参数显著相关，则可以根据类别的排序推导出一个新的分级参数。例如，职业是一个名义参数，但根据教育和收入对职业进行分类，可以得到一个职业地位指数（Duncan 1961）。一般来说，参数的交叉程度，或者说通过其强相关性巩固社会地位差异的程度，反映了社会中最重要的结构性条件，这些条件对冲突（Coleman 1957）和社会融合具有至关重要的影响。

Societies vary in the extent to which their structures are differentiated along various lines, and they also vary in the extent to which the different segments are integrated. Differentiation and integration are complementary concepts, and the definition of integration takes this into account. Both inequality and heterogeneity, the two forms of differentiation, are barriers to social intercourse, on the assumption that proximate status and common group membership promote social associations. If there were no connections among different social positions, however, these positions would not constitute elements of a single social structure. These connections that integrate the various segments of society are produced by the social associations between persons who occupy positions in different segments, in different groups or hierarchical strata. Society’s integration is conceptualized as resting on the actual social associations between persons in different segments, not on functional interdependence or common values, though these may contribute to integration by promoting social interaction between persons in different groups and strata. Although the assumption is that social associations are less extensive between persons in different social positions than among those in the same ones, high rates of associations among different groups and hierarchical strata are the criterion of macrosocial integration. 6^{6} Dense networks of ingroup relations integrate individuals in their groups, but they threaten to fragment society, the integration of which depends on extensive intergroup relations. 7^{7}
社会结构在各个方面的分化程度各不相同，不同阶层之间的融合程度也存在差异。分化和融合是互补的概念，融合的定义也考虑到了这一点。不平等和异质性这两种分化形式都会阻碍社会交往，因为人们普遍认为，地位接近和共同的群体成员身份能够促进社会联系。然而，如果不同社会地位之间没有联系，这些地位就无法构成单一的社会结构要素。正是由于处于不同阶层、不同群体或不同等级的人们之间的社会交往，才使得社会各阶层得以融合。社会融合的概念基础在于不同阶层人们之间实际存在的社会交往，而非功能上的相互依赖或共同的价值观，尽管功能上的相互依赖或共同的价值观可以通过促进不同群体和不同等级的人们之间的社会互动来促进融合。尽管人们普遍认为，不同社会地位者之间的社会联系不如相同社会地位者之间的联系广泛，但不同群体和等级阶层之间的高联系率却是宏观社会整合的标志。 6^{6} 密集的群体内部关系网络能够整合群体内的个体，但它们也可能导致社会分裂，而社会整合依赖于广泛的群体间关系。 7^{7}

In accordance with this conception of social structure, the theory centers attention on its quantitative properties: the number of persons in different positions and the size of groups; frequency distributions among positions indicative of inequality or heterogeneity; whether various parameters are nearly orthogonal or highly correlated; the degree to which differentiation occurs within or among society’s substructures; and how these structural conditions affect the rates of social association among groups and strata. Concern is with the implications for social life, not of the attributes of individuals, but of the distributions of their socially relevant attributes and the correlations of these distributions in society, which are considered the fundamental properties of social structure. Starting with propositions that employ simple concepts, like group size, the theory progresses to more complex terms derived from the simpler ones, such as heterogeneity, inequality, intersecting parameters, consolidation, penetrating differentiation. It is a theory of the influences of structural conditions, conceived narrowly and in quantitative terms, on social interaction, a theory of the structure of social association. 8^{8}
根据这种社会结构概念，该理论将注意力集中在其量化特征上：不同地位的人数和群体规模；体现不平等或异质性的地位频率分布；各种参数是近乎正交还是高度相关；社会各子结构内部或之间的分化程度；以及这些结构条件如何影响群体和阶层之间的社会交往率。该理论关注的是社会相关属性的分布及其在社会中的相关性，而非个体的属性，这些被认为是社会结构的基本属性，并探讨其对社会生活的影响。该理论从运用群体规模等简单概念的命题出发，逐步过渡到由这些简单概念衍生出的更复杂的术语，例如异质性、不平等、交叉参数、巩固和渗透性分化。它是一种关于结构条件（以狭义的量化方式理解）对社会互动的影响的理论，一种关于社会交往结构的理论。

Minority Groups  少数群体

Empirical research could answer such questions, though it would be difficult to obtain reliable information for answering some of them, like the amount of time spent and the frequency of premarital intercourse. In any case, a hypothesis is needed to guide the research, or at least to interpret its findings concerning what properties of groups account for differences in extent of intergroup relations. A relevant hypothesis is derivable from Durkheim’s (1951) theory that explains the lower suicide rates of Catholics and Jews than of Protestants on the basis of their stronger ingroup integration, which in turn is attributable to the deemphasis on individualism in Catholicism and Judaism and, in part, to position as a minority group. On the plausible assumption that strong ingroup bonds inhibit intergroup relations, one might infer that intergroup relations of various kinds are comparatively infrequent among Catholics and among Jews, and probably also among American blacks, owing to their being a minority. Other considerations lead to the opposite conclusion, however. Research indicates that the religious affiliations of Jews are less strong and have declined more than those of Protestants or Catholics (Lazerwitz 1961; Lenski 1961, pp. 44–53), which suggests that Jews are more likely than Christians to enter into interreligious marriages, and possibly premarital sex as well. 9^{9} One might also conjecture, on the basis of admittedly quite limited empirical evidence, that premarital sex relations are more prevalent among blacks than whites (Sorenson 1973, pp. 172, 255) and more prevalent among Catholics than puritanical Protestants (Kinsey, Pomeroy, and Martin 1948, pp. 469, 472) and hence that such relations with the outgroup are correspondingly more prevalent among blacks and among Catholics.
实证研究可以回答这些问题，但要获得可靠的信息来回答其中一些问题则较为困难，例如婚前性行为的时间长短和频率。无论如何，都需要一个假设来指导研究，或者至少来解释研究结果，即哪些群体特征导致了群体间关系程度的差异。涂尔干（1951）的理论提供了一个相关的假设，该理论解释了天主教徒和犹太教徒的自杀率低于新教徒的原因，认为这是因为他们的群体内部整合程度更高，而这又归因于天主教和犹太教对个人主义的弱化，以及他们作为少数群体的地位。基于“强大的群体内部纽带会抑制群体间关系”这一合理的假设，人们可能会推断，由于美国黑人是少数群体，因此各种类型的群体间关系在天主教徒和犹太人中相对较少，可能在美国黑人中也是如此。然而，其他一些因素却得出了相反的结论。研究表明，犹太人的宗教信仰不如新教徒或天主教徒坚定，且衰落程度也更高（Lazerwitz 1961; Lenski 1961, pp. 44–53），这表明犹太人比基督徒更有可能缔结跨宗教婚姻，甚至可能发生婚前性行为。 9^{9} 基于目前已知的相当有限的经验证据，人们或许还可以推测，婚前性行为在黑人中比白人中更为普遍（Sorenson 1973, pp. 172, 255），在天主教徒中比清教徒新教徒中更为普遍（Kinsey, Pomeroy, and Martin 1948, pp. 172, 255）。 469, 472) 因此，这种与外群体的关系在黑人和天主教徒中更为普遍。

Jews do have higher rates of premarital sex relations with the outgroup than Christians in the United States and in most western countries; blacks have higher rates than whites; Catholics have higher rates than Protestants. But the reason is not that Jews are less religious, nor is it that blacks or Catholics have fewer puritanical inhibitions about premarital sexual intercourse. It is simply that there are fewer of them than of their counterparts. Every act of intergroup sexual intercourse involves one person from each group, so that the difference between two groups in the rate of such acts is an inverse function of their size. The same is true of all dyadic symmetric association: 10^{10} the rate of intergroup associations of the smaller of two groups must exceed that of the larger. The inference derived from Durkheim’s theory is wrong. Minority groups may have stronger ingroup bonds, but their rates of social association with the majority exceed the majority’s rates with them.
在美国和大多数西方国家，犹太人与外群体发生婚前性行为的比例确实高于基督徒；黑人的比例高于白人；天主教徒的比例高于新教徒。但这并非因为犹太人的宗教信仰程度较低，也并非因为黑人或天主教徒对婚前性行为的清教徒式禁忌较少。原因很简单，就是他们的人数少于其他群体。每一次跨群体性行为都涉及两个群体中的一人，因此两个群体发生此类行为的比例差异与其人口规模成反比。所有二元对称关系也遵循同样的规律：两个群体中较小群体的跨群体交往率必然高于较大群体。由此得出的推论是错误的。少数群体内部的联系可能更紧密，但他们与多数群体的社会交往率高于多数群体与他们的社会交往率。

Hence, the arithmetic properties of groups imply the theorem that in the relation between any two groups, the rate of intergroup associations of the smaller group exceeds that of the larger. This first theorem (T-1) applies to three forms of associations and all their specific manifestations: (1) the proportion intermarried (or having another exclusive association, as mutual best friends) in the smaller group exceeds that in the larger (T-1.1); (2) the mean number of intergroup associates in the smaller group exceeds that in the larger (T-1.2); (3) the mean amount of time spent in intergroup associations is greater for the smaller than for the larger group (T-1.3). 11^{11} Although the same principle does not apply to the proportion of members who have any intergroup associates, which can be greater in the small than in the large group, this is only possible if some members of the small group have particularly many intergroup associates and spend much time with them. The smaller group is more involved than the larger in the intergroup relations between the two, either because a greater proportion of the smaller group’s members have intergroup associates with whom they spend time, or because those who do have intergroup associates have more of them and spend more time with them.
因此，群体的算术性质蕴含了以下定理：在任意两个群体之间的关系中，较小群体的群体间交往率高于较大群体。第一个定理（T-1）适用于三种交往形式及其所有具体表现：（1）较小群体中跨群体通婚（或拥有其他排他性交往，例如共同的挚友）的比例高于较大群体（T-1.1）；（2）较小群体中跨群体交往者的平均数量高于较大群体（T-1.2）；（3）较小群体成员在跨群体交往中花费的平均时间长于较大群体（T-1.3）。 11^{11} 虽然同样的原理并不适用于拥有跨群体交往者的成员比例（较小群体中该比例可能高于较大群体），但这只有在较小群体中的某些成员拥有特别多的跨群体交往者并花费大量时间与他们相处时才有可能发生。较小的群体比较大的群体更积极地参与到两个群体之间的群体关系中，这可能是因为较小的群体中有更多比例的成员与群体间的朋友相处，或者是因为那些与群体间的朋友相处的人拥有更多的群体间朋友，并且与他们相处的时间也更长。

Implications  影响

These theorems are tautological, entailed by the definitions of group size and intergroup relations. But they have implications that are not tautological, and even those that are not self-evident. When differences in group size are very great, most members of the majority have no social contact with the minority. This implies that most WASPs have no social contacts with blacks, Jews, and other small minorities, notwithstanding extensive associations of minorities with the majority. The social experience of associating with persons with different backgrounds undoubtedly affects attitudes and conduct. It may well broaden people’s horizons, promote tolerance, and stimulate intellectual endeavors (Simmel 1908, pp. 685–87; Laumann 1973, pp. 98–105, 126–28). The structurally generated differences in intergroup experience between small minorities and a large majority would lead one to expect these characteristics to be more prevalent among the minorities (Veblen 1919; Seeman 1956). 12^{12} For example, high intellectual achievements would be expected of disproportionate members of minorities, which is apparently the case for some minorities, such as Japanese and Jews. The low intellectual achievements of blacks conflict with this expectation. But a recent study shows that the educational achievements of blacks, when their initial handicaps are controlled, exceed those of whites (Portes and Wilson 1976), which conforms to the expectation that the more extensive intergroup experience of minorities furnishes intellectual stimulation.
这些定理是同义反复的，由群体规模和群体间关系的定义推导而来。但它们也蕴含着一些并非同义反复，甚至并非不言自明的含义。当群体规模差异巨大时，多数群体中的大多数成员与少数群体之间几乎没有社会接触。这意味着，尽管少数群体与多数群体之间存在广泛的联系，但大多数盎格鲁-撒克逊新教徒（WASP）与黑人、犹太人和其他少数族裔群体之间却缺乏社会联系。与不同背景的人交往的社会经验无疑会影响人们的态度和行为。它很可能拓宽人们的视野，促进宽容，并激发智力活动（西美尔，1908，第 685-687 页；劳曼，1973，第 98-105 页，126-128 页）。少数族裔与多数族裔之间群体间经验的结构性差异，会使人们预期这些特征在少数族裔中更为普遍（Veblen 1919; Seeman 1956）。例如，人们会预期少数族裔中人数较多的成员拥有较高的智力成就，而某些少数族裔，如日裔和犹太裔，显然确实如此。黑人的低智力成就与这种预期相悖。但最近的一项研究表明，在控制了黑人的初始劣势之后，他们的教育成就超过了白人（Portes and Wilson 1976），这符合少数族裔更广泛的群体间经验能够提供智力刺激的预期。

For the groups delineated by a given nominal parameter, the probability of extensive intergroup relations increases with decreasing size (T-2.1). Specifically, group size is inversely correlated with the proportion intermarried (T-2.1), the mean number of intergroup associates (T-2.2), and the mean amount of time spent with intergroup associates (T-2.3). These probability theorems, which are not tautological, logically follow from the earlier deterministic ones. If in every dichotomy of groups the rate of intergroup associations of the smaller exceeds that of the larger group (T-1), the average rate of all small with larger groups must exceed that of all large with smaller groups, wherever the array of groups by size is divided into smaller and larger ones. Although some small groups may have lower rates of intergroup associations than some large ones, this necessitates that other small groups have exceptionally high rates to compensate for it. The theorems are restricted to comparisons of groups in terms of a given parameter, because differences in the salience of parameters independently influence the extent of intergroup relations. One can hardly expect the proportion of blacks married to whites to exceed the proportion of blue-eyed persons married to brown-eyed ones in the United States, though there are undoubtedly fewer blacks than blue-eyed persons, since skin-color, so-called, has much and eye-color little salience in American social life.

Discrimination  歧视

The tautological theorem initially advanced (T-1) has unexpected implications for discrimination against minorities. The rate of intergroup relations depends on group size; so does a change in this rate, because the numerators are again identical while the denominators (group size) vary. A given number of associations yields a higher rate for a smaller group, and a given change in this number yields a greater change in the rate for the smaller group, owing to the smaller denominator. This has paradoxical implications for discrimination by a majority group against a minority, “discrimination” meaning simply the reluctance of majority members to associate with minority members, not any other bias in making decisions.
最初提出的同义反复定理（T-1）对歧视少数群体有着意想不到的启示。群体间关系的发生率取决于群体规模；这种发生率的变化也取决于群体规模，因为分子相同而分母（群体规模）不同。在群体规模较小的情况下，相同数量的关联关系会导致更高的发生率；而由于分母较小，相同数量的关联关系变化也会导致较小群体的发生率发生更大的变化。这对于多数群体歧视少数群体而言具有悖论性的意义，这里的“歧视”仅仅是指多数群体成员不愿与少数群体成员交往，而非决策中的任何其他偏见。

A corollary of T-3 is: As a majority’s discrimination in social intercourse against a minority declines, the difference between the majority’s lower and the minority’s higher rate of intergroup associations becomes greater (T-3.1). The proportion of group members insulated from any intergroup associations probably changes in complementary fashion with changes in discrimination, but not necessarily: If some minority members have many intergroup associates, the proportion of the minority who have none could be the same as (or greater than) the proportion of the majority who have none. The size of collectivities and the size distributions within and among them have a dominant impact on social life. This profound insight of Simmel’s has long been neglected in social theory and research, particularly in macro-sociological inquiries, which have either ignored size completely or tried to control it in order to search for other social influences presumed to be more interesting. 16^{16} Recently, however, social scientists have begun to take size seriously as a concept of fundamental theoretical importance for understanding social relations and social structures. Assumptions  假设

The first assumption is that social associations are more prevalent among persons in proximate than those in distant social positions (A-1). Social proximity is defined in terms of parameters, not in terms of rates of social association. Hence, it is a dichotomy for a nominal parameter but varies by degree for a graduated parameter. The assumption has accordingly two corollaries: for nominal parameters, ingroup associations are more prevalent than outgroup associations (A-1.1); for graduate parameters, the prevalence of social associations declines with status distance (A-1.2). The prevalence of social associations refers not to their absolute rates but to the excess of the observed rates over those theoretically expected on the basis of the population distribution. 17^{17} (The degree to which these observed values exceed the expected ones indicates the salience of the parameter.)
第一个假设是，社会关系在社会地位相近的人之间比在社会地位相远的人之间更为普遍（A-1）。社会接近程度是根据参数定义的，而不是根据社会交往率定义的。因此，对于名义参数而言，这是一个二分变量；而对于分级参数而言，它则随社会地位的远近而变化。该假设相应地有两个推论：对于名义参数，群体内交往比群体外交往更为普遍（A-1.1）；对于分级参数，社会交往的普遍程度随社会地位距离的增加而降低（A-1.2）。社会交往的普遍程度并非指其绝对比率，而是指观察到的比率与基于人口分布的理论预期比率之间的差值。 17^{17} （这些观察值超过预期值的程度表明了该参数的显著性。）

There is much empirical evidence that supports this assumption. For example, disproportionate numbers of marriages involve spouses of the same religion (Kennedy 1944; Hollingshead 1950; Coombs 1962; Willits, Bealer, and Bender 1963); ethnic origin (Murdock 1949; Coombs 1962); residential area (Schapera 1946; Murdock 1949); education (Blau and Duncan 1967); socioeconomic status (Centers 1949; Murdock 1949; Hollingshead 1950; Goode 1962; Blau and Duncan 1967). Friendships, too, are disproportionately often formed with persons who share one’s religion and socioeconomic background (Hollingshead 1949; Laumann 1973), as well as with those of the same sex and race (Lazarsfeld and Merton 1954). To be sure, the assumption is undoubtedly not met for all possible attributes of people. Those attributes for which it is not met may be considered not to refer to social positions, which transforms the principle that prevalence of associations depends on proximity from a substantive assumption into an analytic proposition that defines social positions and parameters.
有大量的实证研究支持这一假设。例如，婚姻中夫妻双方信仰相同宗教的比例过高（Kennedy 1944; Hollingshead 1950; Coombs 1962; Willits, Bealer, and Bender 1963）；种族出身相同（Murdock 1949; Coombs 1962）；居住地相同（Schapera 1946; Murdock 1949）；教育程度相同（Blau and Duncan 1967）；社会经济地位相同（Centers 1949; Murdock 1949; Hollingshead 1950; Goode 1962; Blau and Duncan 1967）。友谊也往往不成比例地建立在与自己宗教信仰和社会经济背景相同的人之间（Hollingshead 1949; Laumann 1973），以及与同性同种族的人之间（Lazarsfeld and Merton 1954）。当然，这一假设并非适用于所有人的所有属性。那些不适用该假设的属性可以被认为与社会地位无关，这使得“人际关系的普遍性取决于接近程度”这一原则从一个实质性假设转变为一个定义社会地位及其参数的分析命题。

A second assumption is that social associations depend on opportunities for social contacts (A-2). This assumption is hardly open to question, because persons cannot associate without having opportunities for contact, and a direct implication of it is corroborated by empirical evidence. Inasmuch as the physical propinquity of people increases their opportunities for contacts, the assumption implies that physical propinquity increases the probability of social association (T-5). Research shows that propinquity, including very small differences in physical distance, increases the likelihood of friendships (Festinger, Schachter, and Back 1950) and of marriage (Christensen 1958).
第二个假设是社会交往依赖于社交接触的机会（A-2）。这一假设几乎无可置疑，因为人们如果没有接触机会就无法建立交往，而且这一假设的直接推论也得到了实证研究的证实。鉴于人与人之间的物理距离越近，接触的机会就越多，因此该假设意味着物理距离越近，建立社会交往的可能性就越大（T-5）。研究表明，即使是微小的物理距离差异，也会增加建立友谊（Festinger、Schachter 和 Back，1950）和婚姻（Christensen，1958）的可能性。

A final assumption is that the influences of various parameters on social associations are partly additive, not entirely contingent on one another (A-3). The assumption is not that parameters have no interaction or joint effects on social relations, merely that the effects of any one are not entirely dependent on and cannot be completely suppressed by variations in other parameters. In other words, it is assumed that persons who share several social positions are more likely to associate with each other than are those who share fewer and that sharing a given social position makes associations more likely between both those who do and those who do not share other positions. For example, people who have the same religion or education or any other salient social attribute are expected to be more likely to associate with each other than are others, regardless of whether they also share other social attributes. Actually, A-3 simply makes explicit what is already implicit in A-1—that any common or similar attribute that is considered a parameter makes social associations more probable.
最后一个假设是，各种参数对社会交往的影响部分是累加的，并非完全相互依存（A-3）。这个假设并非认为参数之间不存在交互作用或联合效应，而是指任何一个参数的影响并非完全依赖于其他参数的变化，也无法被其他参数的变化完全抑制。换言之，假设拥有多个社会地位的人比拥有较少社会地位的人更有可能彼此交往；并且，拥有某个社会地位会使拥有相同社会地位的人与不拥有相同社会地位的人之间更容易建立联系。例如，拥有相同宗教信仰、教育背景或其他显著社会属性的人比其他人更有可能彼此交往，无论他们是否还拥有其他社会属性。实际上，A-3 只是明确阐述了 A-1 中已经隐含的内容——任何被视为参数的共同或相似属性都会增加社会交往的可能性。

Differentiation in a Single Dimension
一维微分

Before turning to the implications of these assumptions for differentiation, implications of the initial theorems for status differences are examined. Status distributions are nearly always positively skewed, with few persons in high status and many in lower status. Some status structures are pyramids, with the largest frequencies at the bottom and declining frequencies as one moves up. Authority in organizations and wealth in society are typically distributed in this manner. Other status structures are truncated diamonds, with frequencies first increasing as one goes up from the bottom and only then decreasing. This tends to be the case for income distribution in western societies. 在探讨这些假设对分化的影响之前，首先考察初始定理对地位差异的影响。地位分布几乎总是正偏态的，即高地位者少，低地位者多。有些地位结构呈金字塔形，底部频率最高，向上递减。组织中的权力和社会中的财富通常以这种方式分布。另一些地位结构呈截角菱形，频率先从底部向上递增，然后递减。西方社会的收入分配往往属于这种情况。从定理 T-1 可以推导出一个新的定理：在金字塔形地位结构或另一种地位结构金字塔形上部，给定群体规模差异的情况下，较小群体与较大群体之间的社会关联率高于较大群体与较小群体之间的社会关联率。

In a pyramidal status structure, the rate of social association of any higher with any lower stratum exceeds that of this lower with this higher stratum (T-6). 18^{18} If such a difference necessarily characterizes the rates of association between any two strata in a pyramid, it is probable that all strata except the lowest have higher rates of association with lower than with equivalent higher strata. The reason is that all strata in a pyramid except the lowest are smaller than others below and larger than others above them, and the theorem that a group’s probable rate of association is an inverse function of its comparative size (T-2) implies that the association rate of a group with another compared to which it is small probably exceeds its association rate with another group compared to which it is large. Consequently, an inference from T-2 is: People in middle as well as in high strata in a pyramid probably associate more with others below them than with others above them in status (T-7).
在金字塔式的社会地位结构中，任何较高阶层与任何较低阶层之间的社会交往率都高于该较低阶层与该较高阶层之间的交往率（T-6）。 18^{18} 如果这种差异必然表征金字塔中任意两个阶层之间的交往率，那么除了最底层之外，所有阶层与其下层阶层的交往率都可能高于与其同等地位的较高阶层的交往率。原因是，金字塔中除最底层之外的所有阶层都小于其下层，大于其上层，而群体交往率与其相对规模成反比的定理（T-2）表明，一个群体与其相对规模较小的群体之间的交往率可能高于其与其相对规模较大的群体之间的交往率。因此，从 T-2 可以推断：金字塔中层和高层的人们可能更多地与地位低于自己的人交往，而不是与地位高于自己的人交往（T-7）。

Reductions in inequality diminish the impact of status on social associations (T-8). This theorem follows from the assumption that status distance discourages social associations (A-1.2) and the definition of inequality in terms of average status distance. Note that there is no reason to assume that a decline in educational inequality, for instance, lessens the salience of education, at least in the short run; the implication is that a given difference in education will continue to have the same adverse effect on social intercourse. However, after educational inequality has declined, fewer people than before differ widely in education; therefore educational differences have less impact on social intercourse in the society. Whether reductions in inequality affect in the long run the salience of education (or another status) and consequently have further repercussions for social associations is a moot question.
不平等程度的降低会削弱社会地位对社会交往的影响（T-8）。这一定理源于以下假设：地位差距会阻碍社会交往（A-1.2），以及不平等是以平均地位差距来定义的。需要注意的是，没有理由假设教育不平等程度的降低会削弱教育的重要性，至少在短期内如此；这意味着，既定的教育差异仍会对社会交往产生同样的负面影响。然而，在教育不平等程度降低之后，教育水平差异显著的人数会减少；因此，教育差异对社会交往的影响也随之减弱。至于不平等程度的降低是否会在长期内影响教育（或其他地位）的重要性，并因此对社会交往产生进一步的影响，目前尚无定论。

Multiple Differentiation
多重微分

Only the influences of a single parameter on social life have been considered so far, not yet those of combinations of parameters. People in all societies have several group affiliations, and those in complex societies have many, which are partly intersecting. Differences in sex, race, national background, religion, and occupation do not coincide, though some are correlated. The numerous, partly intersecting nominal parameters of complex social structures engender multiform heterogeneity, which exerts much greater structural constraints on intergroup relations than simple heterogeneity in one dimension.
迄今为止，人们只考虑了单一参数对社会生活的影响，而尚未考虑参数组合的影响。所有社会中的人们都拥有多个群体归属感，复杂社会中的人们则拥有更多群体归属感，这些群体归属感之间部分相互交叉。性别、种族、国籍、宗教和职业等方面的差异并非完全一致，尽管有些差异之间存在相关性。复杂社会结构中众多且部分交叉的名义参数造就了多形式的异质性，这种异质性对群体间关系施加的结构性约束远大于单一维度上的简单异质性。

Pronounced multiform heterogeneity compels people to establish intergroup relations, because it implies that ingroup relations are simultaneously intergroup relations in terms of different parameters (Blau 1974). It is impossible not to associate with outsiders when one’s ingroup associates in one dimension are, in several others, members of other groups than one’s own (Merton 1972, pp. 22–25). For individuals to satisfy their most salient ingroup preferences, they must set aside other ingroup preferences and enter into intergroup relations along other lines. The very fact that prejudiced persons discriminate against associating with out groups of various kinds restricts their other choices and constrains them to be more tolerant about associating with outsiders in terms of these other parameters. For example, many academics seem to exhibit strong ingroup biases in their conduct in favor of associates with similar advanced education, who are also academics, and who often are even in the same discipline. One might conjecture that these ingroup biases, by restricting freedom of choice along other lines, are in part responsible for the tolerance of academics about associating with colleagues regardless of religion, class origin, and ethnic background.
显著的多元异质性迫使人们建立群体间关系，因为它意味着群体内部关系在不同的维度上同时也是群体间关系（Blau 1974）。当一个人的群体内部成员在某一维度上属于其他群体时，他不可能不与群体外部成员交往（Merton 1972，第 22-25 页）。为了满足个体最显著的群体内部偏好，他们必须搁置其他群体内部偏好，并以其他方式建立群体间关系。事实上，抱有偏见的人歧视与各种群体外部成员交往，这限制了他们的其他选择，并迫使他们在这些其他维度上对与群体外部成员交往持更宽容的态度。例如，许多学者似乎在行为上表现出强烈的群体内部偏好，倾向于与拥有相似高等教育背景、同样是学者，甚至往往是同一学科领域的学者交往。人们可能会推测，这些群体内部的偏见，通过限制其他方面的选择自由，在一定程度上导致了学术界对与同事交往的容忍度，而不管他们的宗教信仰、阶级出身和种族背景如何。

What generates these structural constraints on intergroup relations is that parameters are intersecting—that differences of one kind among people are not related or are only slightly related to differences of other kinds among them. Intersecting parameters promote intergroup relations (T-10). This theorem applies to the intersection among nominal, among graduated, and of graduated with nominal parameters, and it follows from the assumptions that proximity promotes social associations (A-1) and that the influences of parameters are partly additive (A-3). If differences in income are little related to differences in religion, education, and other social attributes, ingroup preferences in regard to these attributes prompt persons to associate with others whose income differs from theirs. The opposite is the case, however, if income differences are strongly related to differences in other social attributes. Under this condition, the ingroup preferences in regard to the other attributes lead—inadvertently, as it were—to disproportionate associations among persons whose income is similar too, reinforcing the independent effect of income on social associations. Accordingly, a theorem complementary to T-10 is implied by the same two assumptions (A-1 and A-3).
造成群体间关系结构性约束的原因在于参数的交叉性——即人与人之间不同类型的差异之间要么没有关联，要么关联甚微。交叉参数促进群体间关系（T-10）。该定理适用于名义参数、递增参数以及递增参数与名义参数的交叉情况，其推论基于以下假设：邻近性促进社会交往（A-1），以及参数的影响部分具有叠加性（A-3）。如果收入差异与宗教、教育和其他社会属性的差异关联甚微，那么群体内部对这些属性的偏好会促使人们与收入不同的人交往。然而，如果收入差异与其他社会属性的差异密切相关，情况则恰恰相反。在这种情况下，群体内部对其他属性的偏好会——可以说是无意中——导致收入相近的人之间出现不成比例的交往，从而强化收入对社会交往的独立影响。因此，同样的两个假设（A-1 和 A-3）可以推导出与 T-10 互补的定理。

Strongly correlated parameters consolidate status and group differences and thereby impede intergroup relations (T-11). When the social differences delineated by various parameters largely coincide, their inhibiting effects on social intercourse reinforce one another. Individuals who differ on many salient social attributes do not have sufficient common interests to sustain extensive social associations, and differences in numerous social positions also reduce the likelihood that instrumental activities bring persons together. If parameters are consolidated, even individuals who do not have ingroup preferences in terms of a given parameter will mostly associate with the ingroup, owing to this parameter’s correlations with other parameters in terms of which they do have ingroup preferences. This can be illustrated with the case of the academics mentioned. Tendencies to associate with persons in the same discipline lead to intergroup relations in terms of social background only if academic discipline is little related to background characteristics and colleagues in a discipline greatly vary in religion, class origin, and ethnic background. If academic discipline and background characteristics are strongly correlated, most colleagues in a discipline have the same social background. In this situation, the tendency to associate with colleagues in one’s discipline entails associating mostly with persons who also share one’s religion, class origin, and ethnic background, even for individuals who have absolutely no ingroup preferences in terms of these background characteristics.
高度相关的参数会巩固地位和群体差异，从而阻碍群体间关系（T-11）。当各种参数所界定的社会差异高度重合时，它们对社会交往的抑制作用会相互强化。在许多显著社会属性上存在差异的个体缺乏足够的共同利益来维持广泛的社会联系，而众多社会地位的差异也会降低工具性活动将人们聚集在一起的可能性。如果参数被巩固，即使个体在特定参数上没有群体偏好，他们也会因为该参数与其他他们有群体偏好的参数存在相关性而倾向于与该群体成员交往。这一点可以用前面提到的学者案例来说明。只有当学科与背景特征关联度较低，且同一学科的同事在宗教、阶级出身和种族背景方面差异很大时，与同一学科的人交往的倾向才会导致基于社会背景的群体间关系。如果学科与背景特征高度相关，那么同一学科的大多数同事都具有相同的社会背景。在这种情况下，与本学科同事交往的倾向意味着，即使个人在这些背景特征方面完全没有群体偏好，也大多会与那些与自己有相同宗教信仰、阶级出身和种族背景的人交往。

When parameters are intersecting, ingroup bias leads to intergroup relations; when they are consolidated, lack of ingroup bias leads to ingroup relations. Variations in structural conditions—the interrelations of parameters—determine what consequences given sociopsychological tendencies have for the social processes that integrate the various segments of society. The consolidation and intersection of parameters are polar opposites on the same continuum, indicated by the correlations of parameters. The stronger their positive correlation, the more consolidated parameters are. The weaker their positive correlation, the more parameters intersect. 20^{20}
当参数交叉时，群体内偏见会导致群体间关系；当参数趋于一致时，缺乏群体内偏见会导致群体内关系。结构条件的变化——即参数之间的相互关系——决定了特定社会心理倾向对整合社会各阶层的社会过程的影响。参数的趋于一致和交叉是同一连续体上的两个极端，这由参数之间的相关性来体现。参数的相关性越强，其趋于一致；相关性越弱，参数的交叉就越多。 20^{20}

Intersecting parameters reflect a highly differentiated social structure, whereas consolidated parameters are indicative of a less complex structure with fewer independent lines of differentiation. At the same time, intersecting parameters further the integration of the different segments of society by promoting extensive associations among groups and strata. No assumption about value consensus or functional interdependence has been made to arrive at this conclusion. Functional interdependence typically entails much one-sided dependence and power inequality, which are more likely to impede than to foster social integration. The only substantive assumptions needed to derive the conclusion are that people associate more with others in proximate than with those in distant social positions (A-1) and that this is the case for every parameter (A-3). Although the assumptions stipulate merely proclivities for ingroup associations, nothing about intergroup associations, they suffice to deduce from them, jointly with analytic propositions defining structural properties, tendencies to engage in intergroup associations under specified structural conditions. Variations in structural constraints explain the influence that given (invariant) sociopsychological dispositions exert on the social processes that integrate the segments of society.
交叉参数反映了高度分化的社会结构，而整合参数则表明结构较为简单，独立的分化线较少。同时，交叉参数通过促进群体和阶层间的广泛联系，进一步推动了社会不同阶层的融合。得出此结论并未假设价值共识或功能相互依存。功能相互依存通常意味着大量的单方面依赖和权力不平等，这更有可能阻碍而非促进社会融合。得出此结论所需的唯一实质性假设是：人们更倾向于与社会地位相近的人交往，而非与社会地位相远的人交往（A-1），并且这一假设适用于所有参数（A-3）。尽管这些假设仅规定了群体内交往的倾向，而未涉及群体间交往，但它们足以结合定义结构属性的分析命题，推导出在特定结构条件下人们参与群体间交往的倾向。结构性约束的变化解释了既定的（不变的）社会心理倾向对整合社会各部分的社会过程的影响。

Decomposition  分解

The influence exerted on social associations by a given form of differentiation in society can be decomposed into the influence of the differentiation within communities and that of the differentiation among them. The question raised is not what influences various forms of differentiation within communities exert on social relations. This question has been partly answered in the preceding discussion, which applies to the structure of communities, as noted. The problem posed now is what relative significance differentiation within and differentiation among communities, respectively, have for society’s intergroup relations and integration. Does the existing degree of heterogeneity or of inequality in society at large influence intergroup relations more if it occurs primarily within the various communities or if it is largely the result of differences among communities?
社会中某种分化形式对社会关系的影响可以分解为社群内部分化的影响和社群间分化的影响。这里提出的问题并非社群内部各种分化形式对社会关系的影响。如前所述，前文已部分解答了这个问题，该解答主要针对社群结构。现在的问题是，社群内部分化和社群间分化分别对社会群体间关系和融合具有怎样的相对重要性。社会整体上存在的异质性或不平等程度，如果主要发生在各个社群内部，还是主要源于社群间的差异，对群体间关系的影响更大？

A corollary of the theorem previously advanced that propinquity increases the probability of social associations (T-5) is that most social associations take place in people’s own communities (T-5.1). One would therefore surmise that intergroup relations are more affected by heterogeneity and inequality within communities, where most social associations occur, than by average group and status differences among communities. Since heterogeneity promotes intergroup relations (T-9), one would expect heterogeneity within communities to promote them more than society’s heterogeneity that is largely the result of group differences among communities. The more society’s heterogeneity results from heterogeneity within rather than among communities, the more probable are both intergroup associations and associations among different communities (T-12). This theorem is deducible from T-5 (or T-9) and A-1. The propinquity of different groups (T-5) living in the same heterogeneous communities increases opportunities for and probabilities of intergroup relations. Besides, mostly within-community heterogeneity implies that the members of a group are dispersed among different communities and few of them are in the same community, which makes it more difficult to satisfy ingroup preferences (A-1) within one’s community and encourages associations across communities to satisfy them. These tendencies will be most pronounced for members of small groups living in small communities, for instance, black professionals living in small towns. On the other hand, heterogeneity that exists mostly among communities entails greater homogeneity within communities, with different groups largely living in different communities. In this situation, physical (T-5) and social distances (A-1) tend to coincide and discourage both intergroup and intercommunity associations. In short, the segregation of groups in different communities counteracts the positive effect of heterogeneity on intergroup relations.
社会异质性越是源于社区内部而非社区之间的异质性，群体间联系以及不同社区间联系的可能性就越大（T-12）。该定理可由 T-5（或 T-9）和 A-1 推导得出。居住在同一异质性社区中的不同群体（T-5）的邻近性增加了群体间关系的机会和概率。此外，主要存在于社区内部的异质性意味着一个群体的成员分散在不同的社区中，很少有人居住在同一社区，这使得在社区内满足群体偏好（A-1）变得更加困难，并促使人们跨社区建立联系以满足这些偏好。对于居住在小型社区的小群体成员来说，这些趋势最为显著，例如居住在小城镇的黑人专业人士。另一方面，主要存在于社区之间的异质性意味着社区内部的同质性更高，不同的群体大多居住在不同的社区。在这种情况下，物理距离（T-5）和社会距离（A-1）往往重合，从而阻碍了群体间和社区间的交往。简而言之，不同社区中群体的隔离抵消了异质性对群体间关系的积极影响。

The more society’s inequality results from inequality within rather than among communities, the more probable are social associations both among different strata and among different communities (T-13). This conclusion is quite unexpected, but it follows from the same premises as the preceding theorem. To be sure, great inequality makes social associations among different strata less likely, not more likely. The comparison in the theorem is not among communities that differ in inequality, however, but among societies that vary in regard to the extent to which their existing inequality is mostly owing to inequality within or mostly to that among communities. Still, one might have thought that inequality within communities would have greater adverse effects on social associations than would average status differences among communities. Yet this is wrong, as an illustration clearly shows. When poor and rich live in different communities, the inhibiting effects of status distance (A-1) and physical distance (T-5) on social associations reinforce each other. Mostly within-community inequality implies that different strata live in the same communities and that the same strata are dispersed among different communities. Under these conditions, persons from different strata are more likely to associate than when they live in different communities, owing to physical propinquity (T-5), and the common interests of persons in the same stratum who live in different communities promote some association among them, owing to status proximity (A-1.2). Although inequalities within commu nities inhibit social associations, they do not inhibit them as much as do inequalities that are reinforced by residential segregation among strata.
社会不平等越是源于社区内部而非社区之间的不平等，不同阶层之间以及不同社区之间就越有可能存在社会联系（T-13）。这一结论出乎意料，但它与前一个定理的前提相同。诚然，巨大的不平等会降低而非增加不同阶层之间社会联系的可能性。然而，该定理中的比较并非在不平等程度不同的社区之间，而是在不同社会之间，这些社会的不平等主要源于社区内部的不平等还是社区之间的不平等。尽管如此，人们或许会认为社区内部的不平等对社会联系的负面影响会大于社区间平均地位差异的影响。然而，事实并非如此，一个例子清楚地表明了这一点。当贫富居住在不同的社区时，地位距离（A-1）和物理距离（T-5）对社会联系的抑制作用会相互增强。社区内部的不平等主要体现在两个方面：一是不同阶层的人居住在同一社区，二是同一阶层的人分散在不同的社区。在这种情况下，由于地理位置的接近（T-5），不同阶层的人比居住在不同社区的人更容易建立联系；而居住在不同社区的同一阶层的人，由于社会地位的接近（A-1.2），他们的共同利益也会促进彼此之间的联系。尽管社区内部的不平等会抑制社会交往，但其抑制程度远不及由阶层间居住隔离所加剧的不平等。

Surprisingly, society’s inequality as well as its heterogeneity is more compatible with integrative social associations among its different segments when it exists primarily within communities than when it results mostly from the great differences among communities. Much differentiation of any kind within communities and little among them furthers social relations among the various segments of society. Indeed, a still broader generalization can be derived.
令人惊讶的是，当社会不平等和异质性主要存在于社区内部时，它们比主要源于社区间巨大差异时，更有利于不同群体之间建立融合性的社会关系。社区内部的各种分化程度越高，社区之间的分化程度越低，就越有利于促进社会各群体之间的社会关系。事实上，我们还可以得出更广泛的结论。

Penetrating Differentiation
穿透性分化

Communities are not the only substructures into which a society can be divided. Place of work instead of place of residence can be the criterion of substructure, and one can partition the division of labor, for instance, into the occupational differences within and those among work organizations. As a matter of fact, any nominal parameter can be the criterion of substructure. Moreover, successive levels of subdivision can be taken into account: nation, province, town, neighborhood; or, organization, department, section, subunit; or, major occupational group, detailed occupation, specialty. The following proposition applies to any form of differentiation (consolidated as well as simple inequalities and heterogeneities), any type of substructure, on any level.
社区并非社会划分的唯一子结构。工作地点而非居住地可以作为划分子结构的依据，例如，劳动分工可以细分为工作组织内部的职业差异和组织间的职业差异。事实上，任何名义参数都可以作为划分子结构的依据。此外，还可以考虑更高层次的划分：国家、省份、城镇、社区；或者组织、部门、科室、小组；或者主要职业群体、细分职业、专业。以下命题适用于任何形式的差异（包括既有的以及简单的不平等和异质性）、任何类型的子结构以及任何层次。

The penetration of differentiation into substructure promotes intergroup relations of all kinds, that is, it increases the probabilities of social associations among the differentiated groups and strata and among the substructures (T-14). This theorem is deduced from T-10, that intersecting parameters promote associations among groups and strata, since greater penetration into substructures entails more intersection of the parameters delineating differentiation with the parameter defining the substructures. 21^{21} For example, if there is much income inequality within communities but little within the neighborhoods of the various communities, inequality penetrates less deeply and income intersects less with location than if there is also much income inequality within neighborhoods. Similarly, if great ethnic differences exist among major occupational groups and also among detailed occupations but not within detailed occupations, ethnic heterogeneity penetrates less into the occupational structure and ethnic differences intersect less and are more correlated with occupational differences than if there is much ethnic variation within the detailed occupations. Penetrating differentiation exerts a centrifugal force on social relations that directs them outward and integrates the diverse segments in society. The social integration of a large population in a complex society cannot rest solely on some common values and some interdependence; it requires that the diverse groups and hierarchical strata are not isolated from one another but connected through social associations among their members. This is the reason that society’s integration is here defined in terms of the extent of social associations among different groups and strata. The plausible sociopsychological assumption that people prefer ingroup (and proximate-status) to outgroup (and distant-status) associates seems to imply that large societies are necessarily fragmented into segments with few social relations among them. This inference is wrong, however, because it ignores the impact of structural conditions on social relations. When the multiple parameters characterizing social structures are taken into account, it becomes apparent that this very assumption has implications for intergroup relations. The extent of social relations among society’s different segments depends on structural conditions. Specifically, the more structural parameters intersect, the more extensive are intergroup relations. Although the homogeneity of narrow social circles discourages intergroup relations, intersecting parameters disturb this homogeneity. Numerous strongly intersecting parameters imply that differentiation in various forms penetrates into the interstices of society, exerting structural constraints to engage in intergroup associations and thereby integrating the diverse segments of society.
深入的分化对社会关系施加了一种离心力，引导其向外扩散，并将社会中不同的群体整合起来。在一个复杂的社会中，庞大人口的社会整合不能仅仅依赖于某些共同的价值观和相互依存；它要求不同的群体和等级阶层并非彼此孤立，而是通过成员间的社会联系相互连接。正因如此，本文将社会整合定义为不同群体和阶层间社会联系的程度。人们倾向于与内群体（以及地位接近的人）而非外群体（以及地位疏远的人）交往，这种看似合理的社会心理学假设似乎暗示着，大型社会必然会分裂成彼此间社会联系较少的群体。然而，这种推论是错误的，因为它忽略了结构性条件对社会关系的影响。当考虑表征社会结构的多种参数时，就会发现这一假设本身对群体间关系有着重要影响。社会不同群体间社会联系的程度取决于结构性条件。具体而言，结构参数的交叉越多，群体间关系就越广泛。尽管狭隘社交圈的同质性会阻碍群体间关系，但交叉参数会打破这种同质性。众多高度交叉的参数意味着各种形式的分化渗透到社会各个层面，对群体间交往施加结构性约束，从而阻碍社会各阶层的融合。

CONCLUSIONS  结论

This paper has outlined a theory of the structure of social associations which is elaborated elsewhere (Blau 1977). The foundation of the theory is a quantitative conception of social structure as the distributions of a population among social positions in a multidimensional space of positions. The axes in this space are termed “parameters,” which distinguish either nominal positions, like sex and race, or status gradations, like education and income. The two generic forms of differentiation are heterogeneity, defined by the distribution of people among nominal positions, and inequality, defined by their distribution in terms of a status criterion.
本文概述了一种社会关系结构理论，该理论在其他文献中有更详细的阐述（Blau 1977）。该理论的基础是对社会结构的定量概念化，即人口在多维社会地位空间中的分布。该空间中的轴被称为“参数”，它们区分名义上的地位（例如性别和种族）或社会等级（例如教育程度和收入）。两种常见的区分形式是异质性（由人们在名义地位上的分布定义）和不平等（由人们根据社会地位标准进行的分布定义）。

The substantive focus of the theory is on the influences exerted by structural conditions, particularly the relationships of parameters, on the rates of social associations among different groups and strata, inasmuch as social integration is conceived to rest on extensive social relations among the different segments of society. The theory constructed is a deductive one: 14 theorems and a number of corollaries have been derived—in strict logic, I believe—from the analytical propositions defining properties of social structure and three assumptions, which are synthetic propositions. The assumptions are: (1) social proximity promotes social associations; (2) social associations depend on opportunities for contacts; (3) the influences of parameters are partly additive. The role of these assumptions in the theory is as givens, equivalent to the role of assumption of maximizing utility in economic theory. The substantive focus is not on the significance of these assumptions but, given them, on the significance of variation in structural conditions for processes of social association and social integration.
该理论的实质重点在于结构条件，特别是参数之间的关系，对不同群体和阶层间社会交往率的影响，因为社会整合被认为建立在社会不同群体间广泛的社会关系之上。该理论是一个演绎理论：从定义社会结构属性的分析命题和三个综合命题假设中，严格按照逻辑推导出了 14 个定理和若干推论。这三个假设是：（1）社会接近性促进社会交往；（2）社会交往依赖于接触机会；（3）参数的影响部分是累加的。这些假设在该理论中的作用是既定的，相当于经济理论中效用最大化假设的作用。该理论的实质重点并非这些假设的意义，而是在既定假设的前提下，结构条件的变化对社会交往和社会整合过程的意义。
