The Strong Programme in the Sociology of Knowledge
知识社会学中的强纲领

Can the sociology of knowledge investigate and explain the very content and nature of scientific knowledge? Many sociologists believe that it cannot. They say that knowledge as such, as distinct from the circumstances surrounding its production, is beyond their grasp. They voluntarily limit the scope of their own enquiries. I shall argue that this is a betrayal of their disciplinary standpoint. All knowledge, whether it be in the empirical sciences or even in mathematics, should be treated, through and through, as material for investigation. Such limitations as do exist for the sociologist consist in handing over material to allied sciences like psychology or in depending on the researches of specialists in other disciplines. There are no limitations which lie in the absolute or transcendent character of scientific knowledge itself, or in the special nature of rationality, validity, truth or objectivity.
知识社会学能否探究并解释科学知识的内容和本质？许多社会学家认为不能。他们认为，知识本身，而非其产生的背景，超出了他们的理解范围。他们主动限制了自身研究的范围。我认为，这背离了他们的学科立场。所有知识，无论是经验科学领域的知识，还是数学领域的知识，都应从根本上被视为研究对象。社会学家所面临的局限在于，他们不得不将研究对象交给心理学等相关学科，或者依赖其他学科专家的研究成果。科学知识本身的绝对性或超越性，以及理性、有效性、真理或客观性的特殊性质，并不存在任何局限。

It might be expected that the natural tendency of a discipline such as the sociology of knowledge would be to expand and generalise itself: moving from studies of primitive cosmologies to that of our own culture. This is precisely the step that sociologists have been reluctant to take. Again, the sociology of knowledge might well have pressed more strongly into the area currently occupied by philosophers, who have been allowed to take upon themselves the task of defining the nature of knowledge. In fact sociologists have been only too eager to limit their concern with science to its institutional framework and external factors relating to its rate of growth or direction. This leaves untouched the nature of the knowledge thus created (cf. Ben-David (1971), DeGré (1967), Merton (1964) and Stark (1958)).
人们或许会认为，像知识社会学这样的学科，其自然发展趋势应该是不断扩展和概括自身：从研究原始宇宙论转向研究我们自身的文化。然而，这恰恰是社会学家一直不愿迈出的一步。此外，知识社会学本可以更积极地涉足目前由哲学家占据的领域，让哲学家承担起定义知识本质的任务。事实上，社会学家们更热衷于将他们对科学的关注局限于其制度框架以及与其发展速度或方向相关的外部因素。这使得他们忽略了由此产生的知识的本质（参见 Ben-David (1971)、DeGré (1967)、Merton (1964)和 Stark (1958)）。

What is the cause for this hesitation and pessimism? Is it the enormous intellectual and practical difficulties which would attend such a programme? Certainly these must not be underestimated. A measure of their extent can be gained from the effort that has been expended on the more limited aims. But these are not the reasons that are in fact advanced. Is the sociologist at a loss for theories and methods with which to handle scientific knowledge? Surely not. His own discipline provides him with exemplary studies of the knowledge of other cultures which could be used as models and sources of inspiration. Durkheim’s classic study ‘The Elementary Forms of the Religious Life’ shows how a sociologist can penetrate to the very depths of a form of knowledge. What is more Durkheim dropped a number of hints as to how his findings might relate to the study of scientific knowledge. The hints have fallen on deaf ears.
这种犹豫和悲观情绪的根源是什么？是这项计划所面临的巨大智力和实践困难吗？当然，这些困难不容低估。从人们为更有限的目标所付出的努力，便可窥见其程度。但这并非人们真正提出的原因。难道社会学家缺乏处理科学知识的理论和方法吗？当然不是。他所在的学科就为他提供了其他文化知识的典范研究，这些研究可以作为模型和灵感的来源。涂尔干的经典著作《宗教生活的基本形式》展现了社会学家如何深入探究某种知识形式的本质。更重要的是，涂尔干还暗示了他的研究成果可能与科学知识的研究相关。然而，这些暗示却无人理会。

The cause of the hesitation to bring science within the scope of a thorough-going sociological scrutiny is lack of nerve and will. It is believed to be a foredoomed enterprise. Of course, the failure of nerve has deeper roots than this purely psychological characterisation suggests, and these will be investigated later. Whatever the cause of the malady, its symptoms take the form of a priori and philosophical argumentation. By these means sociologists express their conviction that science is a special case, and that contradictions and absurdities would befall them if they ignored this fact. Naturally philosophers are only too eager to encourage this act of self-abnegation (e.g. Lakatos (1971), Popper (1966)).
之所以迟迟不愿将科学纳入彻底的社会学考察范围，是因为缺乏勇气和意愿。人们普遍认为这是一项注定失败的事业。当然，这种勇气的缺失远比这种纯粹的心理描述更为根深蒂固，这一点将在后文探讨。无论其根源何在，其表现形式都是先验的、哲学式的论证。社会学家正是通过这种方式表达了他们的信念：科学是一个特例，如果忽视这一事实，就会出现各种矛盾和荒谬之处。哲学家们自然非常乐意鼓励这种自我否定（例如拉卡托斯 (1971)、波普尔 (1966)）。

It will be the purpose of this book to combat these arguments and inhibitions. For this reason the discussions which follow will sometimes, though not always, have to be methodological rather than substantive. But I hope they will be positive in their effect. Their aim is to put weapons in the hands of those engaged in constructive work to help them attack critics, doubters and sceptics.
本书旨在反驳这些论点和偏见。因此，接下来的讨论有时（尽管并非总是如此）将侧重于方法论而非实质内容。但我希望这些讨论能产生积极的影响。其目的是为从事建设性工作的人们提供武器，帮助他们反击批评者、怀疑者和质疑者。

I shall first spell out what I call the strong programme in the sociology of knowledge. This will provide the framework within which detailed objections will then be considered. Since a priori arguments are always embedded in background assumptions and attitudes it will be necessary to bring these to the surface for examination as well. This will be the second major topic and it is here that substantial sociological hypotheses about our conception of science will begin to emerge. The third major topic will concern what is perhaps the most difficult of all the obstacles to the sociology of knowledge, namely mathematics and logic. It will transpire that the problems of principle involved are not, in fact, unduly technical. I shall indicate how these subjects can be studied sociologically.
我首先要阐述我所谓的知识社会学的核心纲领。这将为后续对各种反对意见的详细探讨提供框架。由于先验论证总是根植于既有的背景假设和态度之中，因此也有必要将这些假设和态度揭示出来进行考察。这将是第二个主要议题，也是关于我们科学观念的重要社会学假设开始浮现之处。第三个主要议题将涉及知识社会学面临的或许是最棘手的障碍，即数学和逻辑。事实证明，其中涉及的原则性问题实际上并非过于技术性。我将阐述如何从社会学的角度研究这些学科。

The Strong Programme
强计划

The sociologist is concerned with knowledge, including scientific knowledge, purely as a natural phenomenon. The appropriate definition of knowledge will therefore be rather different from that of either the layman or the philosopher. Instead of defining it as true belief—or perhaps, justified true belief—knowledge for the sociologist is whatever people take to be knowledge. It consists of those beliefs which people confidently hold to and live by. In particular the sociologist will be concerned with beliefs which are taken for granted or institutionalised, or invested with authority by groups of people. Of course knowledge must be distinguished from mere belief. This can be done by reserving the word ‘knowledge’ for what is collectively endorsed, leaving the individual and idiosyncratic to count as mere belief.
社会学家关注的是知识，包括科学知识，将其纯粹视为一种自然现象。因此，知识的恰当定义与普通人或哲学家的定义截然不同。社会学家并不将知识定义为真信念——或者说，经过论证的真信念——而是将其定义为人们所认为的知识。它包含人们深信不疑并奉行的信念。社会学家尤其关注那些被视为理所当然、制度化或被群体赋予权威的信念。当然，知识必须与单纯的信念区分开来。这可以通过将“知识”一词保留给那些被集体认可的事物来实现，而将个人和特立独行的观点视为单纯的信念。

Our ideas about the workings of the world have varied greatly. This has been true within science just as much as in other areas of culture. Such variation forms the starting point for the sociology of knowledge and constitutes its main problem. What are the causes of this variation, and how and why does it change? The sociology of knowledge focuses on the distribution of belief and the various factors which influence it. For example: how is knowledge transmitted; how stable is it; what processes go into its creation and maintenance; how is it organised and categorised into different disciplines or spheres?
我们对世界运行方式的理解千差万别。科学领域和其他文化领域亦是如此。这种差异构成了知识社会学的出发点，也是其主要研究课题。造成这种差异的原因是什么？它如何变化？又为何变化？知识社会学关注的是信念的传播及其各种影响因素。例如：知识是如何传播的？它的稳定性如何？知识的产生和维护涉及哪些过程？它是如何被组织和分类到不同学科或领域的？

For sociologists these topics call for investigation and explanation and they will try to characterise knowledge in a way which accords with this perspective. Their ideas therefore will be in the same causal idiom as those of any other scientist. Their concern will be to locate the regularities and general principles or processes which appear to be at work within the field of their data. The aim will be to build theories to explain these regularities. If these theories are to satisfy the requirement of maximum generality they will have to apply to both true and false beliefs, and as far as possible the same type of explanation will have to apply in both cases. The aim of physiology is to explain the organism in health and disease; the aim of mechanics is to understand machines which work and machines which fail; bridges which stand as well as those which fall. Similarly the sociologist seeks theories which explain the beliefs which are in fact found, regardless of how the investigator evaluates them.
对社会学家而言，这些主题需要研究和解释，他们会尝试以符合这种视角的方式来描述知识。因此，他们的思想与其他科学家一样，都遵循因果关系的语言。他们关注的是在其数据领域中寻找那些似乎起作用的规律和普遍原则或过程。其目标是构建理论来解释这些规律。如果这些理论要满足最大程度的普遍性要求，它们就必须适用于真假信念，并且尽可能地在两种情况下都采用同一种解释。生理学的目标是解释健康和疾病状态下的机体；力学的目标是理解运转的机器和失效的机器；屹立的桥梁和坍塌的桥梁。同样，社会学家寻求的是能够解释实际存在的信念的理论，无论研究者如何评价这些信念。

Some typical problems in this area which have already yielded interesting findings may serve to illustrate this approach. First, there have been studies of the connections between the gross social structure of groups and the general form of the cosmologies to which they have subscribed. Anthropologists have found the social correlates, and the possible causes of our having anthropomorphic and magical world-views as distinct from impersonal and naturalistic one (Douglas (1966 and 1970)). Second, there have been studies which have traced the connections between economic, technical and industrial developments and the content of scientific theories. For example, the impact of practical developments in water and steam technology on the content of theories in thermodynamics has been studied in great detail. The causal link is beyond dispute (Kuhn (1959), Cardwell (1971)). Third, there is much evidence that features of culture which usually count as non-scientific greatly influence both the creation and the evaluation of scientific theories and findings. Thus Eugenic concerns have been shown to underlie and explain Francis Galton’s creation of the concept of the coefficient of correlation in statistics. Again the general political, social and ideological standpoint of the geneticist Bateson has been used to explain his role of sceptic in the controversy over the gene theory of inheritance (Coleman (1970), Cowan (1972), Mackenzie (1981)). Fourth, the importance that processes of training and socialisation have in the conduct of science is becoming increasingly documented. Patterns of continuity and discontinuity, of reception and rejection, appear to be explicable by appeal to these processes. An interesting example of the way in which a background in the requirements of a scientific discipline influences the assessment of a piece of work is afforded by Lord Kelvin’s criticisms of the theory of evolution. Kelvin calculated the age of the sun by treating it as an incandescent body cooling down. He found that it would have burnt itself out before evolution could have reached its currently observable state. The world is not old enough to have allowed evolution to have run its course, so the theory of evolution must be wrong. The assumption of geological uniformity, with its promise of vast stretches of time, had been rudely pulled from beneath the biologist’s feet. Kelvin’s arguments caused dismay. Their authority was immense and in the 1860’s they were unanswerable; they followed with convincing rigour from convincing physical premises. By the last decade of the century the geologists had plucked up courage to tell Kelvin that he must have made a mistake. This newfound courage was not because of any dramatic new discoveries, indeed, there had been no real change in the evidence available. What had happened in the interim was a general consolidation in geology as a discipline with a mounting quantity of detailed observation of the fossil record. It was this growth which caused a variation in the assessments of probability and plausibility: Kelvin simply must have left some vital but unknown fac tor out of consideration. It was only with the understanding of the sun’s nuclear sources of energy that his physical argument could be faulted. Geologists and biologists had no foreknowledge of this, they simply had not waited for an answer (Rudwick (1972), Burchfield (1975)). This example also serves to make another point. It deals with social processes internal to science, so there is no question of sociological considerations being confined to the operation of external influences.
该领域一些已取得有趣发现的典型问题或许可以阐释这种方法。首先，已有研究探讨了群体的宏观社会结构与其所信奉的宇宙观之间的联系。人类学家发现了人类拟人化和魔法世界观（区别于非人格化的自然主义世界观）的社会关联及其可能成因（Douglas (1966, 1970)）。其次，已有研究追溯了经济、技术和工业发展与科学理论内容之间的联系。例如，水和蒸汽技术的实际发展对热力学理论内容的影响已得到深入研究。其因果关系毋庸置疑（Kuhn (1959), Cardwell (1971)）。第三，大量证据表明，通常被认为是非科学的文化特征对科学理论和发现的产生和评价都产生了重大影响。因此，优生学的考量已被证明是弗朗西斯·高尔顿在统计学中提出相关系数概念的根本原因和解释。同样，遗传学家贝特森的总体政治、社会和意识形态立场也被用来解释他在基因遗传理论之争中持怀疑态度的原因（Coleman (1970), Cowan (1972), Mackenzie (1981)）。第四，培训和社会化过程在科学实践中的重要性正日益得到证实。连续性与非连续性、接受与拒绝的模式似乎都可以通过诉诸这些过程来解释。 开尔文勋爵对进化论的批判，生动地展现了科学学科背景如何影响对作品的评价。开尔文将太阳视为一个逐渐冷却的炽热天体，以此计算太阳的年龄。他发现，在进化达到目前可观测到的状态之前，太阳就已经燃烧殆尽。地球的年龄不足以让进化完成整个过程，因此进化论必然是错误的。地质均一性的假设，以及由此而来的漫长岁月，被生物学家们无情地推翻了。开尔文的论证令人震惊。在 19 世纪 60 年代，这些论证极具权威性，几乎无人能反驳；它们建立在令人信服的物理学前提之上，并以严谨的论证方式展开。到了 19 世纪末，地质学家们终于鼓起勇气，告诉开尔文他一定是犯了错误。这种新获得的勇气并非源于任何重大的新发现，事实上，现有证据并没有发生真正的变化。在此期间，地质学作为一门学科经历了整体的巩固，对化石记录的详细观察数量与日俱增。正是这种发展导致了对概率和合理性评估的差异：开尔文必然遗漏了一些至关重要但未知的因素。只有在理解了太阳的核能来源之后，他的物理论证才有可能被推翻。地质学家和生物学家对此一无所知，他们只是没有等待答案的出现（Rudwick (1972), Burchfield (1975)）。这个例子也说明了另一点。 它研究的是科学内部的社会过程，因此不存在社会学考量局限于外部影响作用的问题。

Finally, mention must be made of a fascinating and controversial study of the physicists of Weimar Germany. Forman (1971) uses their academic addresses to show them taking up the dominant, anti-scientific ‘Lebensphilosophie’ surrounding them. He argues ‘that the movement to dispense with causality in physics which sprang up so suddenly and blossomed so luxuriantly in Germany after 1918, was primarily an effort by German physicists to adapt the content of their science to the values of their intellectual environment’ (p. 7). The boldness and interest of this claim derives from the central place of a causality in modern quantum theory.
最后，必须提及一项关于魏玛德国物理学家的引人入胜且颇具争议的研究。福尔曼（1971）利用他们的学术演讲，揭示了他们如何接受当时盛行的反科学“生活哲学”。他认为，“1918年后在德国突然兴起并蓬勃发展的物理学因果关系摒弃运动，主要是德国物理学家试图使其科学内容适应其知识环境价值观的努力”（第7页）。这一论断的大胆和趣味性源于因果关系在现代量子理论中的核心地位。

The approaches that have just been sketched suggest that the sociology of scientific knowledge should adhere to the following four tenets. In this way it will embody the same values which are taken for granted in other scientific disciplines. These are:
以上概述的方法表明，科学知识社会学应遵循以下四个原则。如此，它便能体现其他科学学科所秉持的相同价值观。这四个原则是：

1 It would be causal, that is, concerned with the conditions which bring about belief or states of knowledge. Naturally there will be other types of causes apart from social ones which will cooperate in bringing about belief.
1. 它指的是因果关系，即关注导致信念或知识状态产生的条件。当然，除了社会因素之外，还会有其他类型的原因共同促成信念的产生。

2 It would be impartial with respect to truth and falsity, rationality or irrationality, success or failure. Both sides of these dichotomies will require explanation.
2. 它在真假、理性与非理性、成功与失败方面都将保持公正。这些二元对立的两面都需要解释。

3 It would be symmetrical in its style of explanation. The same types of cause would explain, say, true and false beliefs.
3. 它的解释方式是对称的。例如，同样的因果关系可以解释真假信念。

4 It would be reflexive. In principle its patterns of explanation would have to be applicable to sociology itself. Like the requirement of symmetry this is a response to the need to seek for general explanations. It is an obvious requirement of principle because otherwise sociology would be a standing refutation of its own theories.
4. 这将是自反性的。原则上，它的解释模式必须适用于社会学本身。如同对称性要求一样，这是对寻求普遍解释的需求的回应。这是一个显而易见的原则性要求，否则社会学就等于对其自身理论的持续反驳。

These four tenets, of causality, impartiality, symmetry and reflexivity, define what will be called the strong programme in the sociology of knowledge. They are by no means new, but represent an amalgam of the more optimistic and scientific strains to be found in Durkheim (1938), Mannheim (1936) and Znaniecki (1965).
因果性、公正性、对称性和反思性这四个原则，定义了知识社会学中所谓的强纲领。它们绝非新颖之作，而是融合了涂尔干（1938）、曼海姆（1936）和兹纳涅茨基（1965）等人的较为乐观和科学的观点。

In what follows I shall try to maintain the viability of these tenets against criticism and misunderstanding. What is at stake is whether the strong programme can be pursued in a consistent and plausible way. Let us therefore turn to the main objections to the sociology of knowledge to draw out the full significance of the tenets and to see how the strong programme stands up to criticism.
接下来，我将努力维护这些原则的有效性，使其免受批评和误解。关键在于，这一强有力的纲领能否以连贯且合理的方式贯彻执行。因此，让我们转向对知识社会学的主要反对意见，以充分阐明这些原则的意义，并考察这一强有力的纲领如何经受住批评。

The Autonomy of Knowledge
知识自主性

One important set of objections to the sociology of knowledge derives from the conviction that some beliefs do not stand in need of any explanation, or do not stand in need of a causal explanation. This feeling is particularly strong when the beliefs in question are taken to be true, rational, scientific or objective.
对知识社会学的一大反对意见源于这样一种信念：某些信念无需任何解释，或者无需因果解释。当这些信念被认为是真实的、理性的、科学的或客观的时，这种观点就尤为强烈。

When we behave rationally or logically it is tempting to say that our actions are governed by the requirements of reasonableness or logic. The explanation of why we draw the conclusion we do from a set of premises may appear to reside in the principles of logical inference themselves. Logic, it may seem, constitutes a set of connections between premises and conclusions and our minds can trace out these connections. As long as someone is being reasonable then the connections themselves would seem to provide the best explanation for the beliefs of the reasoner. Like an engine on rails, the rails themselves dictate where it will go. It is as if we can transcend the directionless push and pull of physical causality and harness it, or subordinate it, to quite other principles and let these determine our thoughts. If this is so then it is not the sociologist or the psychologist but the logician who will provide the most important part of the explanation of belief.
当我们理性或合乎逻辑地行事时，很容易认为我们的行为受制于合理性或逻辑的要求。我们从一系列前提中得出特定结论的原因，似乎就蕴藏在逻辑推理的原则本身之中。逻辑似乎构成了一系列前提与结论之间的联系，而我们的思维能够追溯这些联系。只要一个人是理性的，那么这些联系本身似乎就能为推理者的信念提供最佳解释。就像火车在铁轨上行驶一样，铁轨本身决定了它的行驶方向。这仿佛意味着我们可以超越物理因果关系的无方向性推拉，驾驭它，或者说使之服从于其他原则，并让这些原则来决定我们的思想。如果真是如此，那么对信念的解释中最重要的部分，就不是社会学家或心理学家，而是逻辑学家了。

Of course, when someone makes mistakes in their reasoning then logic itself is no explanation. A lapse or deviation may be due to the interference of a whole variety of factors. Perhaps the reasoning is too difficult for the limited intelligence of the reasoner, perhaps he or she is inattentive, or too emotionally involved in the subject under discussion. As when a train goes off the rails, a cause for the accident can surely be found. But we neither have, nor need, commissions of enquiry into why accidents do not happen.
当然，当一个人的推理出现错误时，逻辑本身并不能解释错误。失误或偏差可能是由多种因素干扰造成的。或许推理过程对于推理者的智力而言过于复杂，或许他或她注意力不集中，又或许他或她过于投入于讨论的主题。就像火车脱轨一样，事故的原因肯定可以找到。但是，我们既没有，也不需要成立调查委员会来探究为什么事故不会发生。

Arguments such as these have become a commonplace in contemporary analytical philosophy. Thus in ‘The Concept of Mind’ (1949) Ryle says: ‘Let the psychologist tell us why we are deceived; but we can tell ourselves and him why we are not deceived’ (p. 308). This approach may be summed up by the claim that nothing makes people do things that are correct but something does make, or cause, them to go wrong (cf. Hamlyn (1969), Peters (1958)).
诸如此类的论证在当代分析哲学中已司空见惯。例如，莱尔在《心灵的概念》（1949）中写道：“让心理学家告诉我们为什么我们会受骗；但我们也可以告诉我们自己和心理学家为什么我们没有受骗”（第308页）。这种方法可以概括为：没有任何因素促使人们做正确的事，但确实存在某种因素促使或导致人们犯错（参见哈姆林（1969）、彼得斯（1958））。

The general structure of these explanations stands out clearly. They all divide behaviour or belief into two types: right and wrong, true or false, rational or irrational. They then invoke sociological or psychological causes to explain the negative side of the division. Such causes explain error, limitation and deviation. The positive side of the evaluative divide is quite different. Here logic, rationality and truth appear to be their own explanation. Here psycho-social causes do not need to be invoked.
这些解释的总体结构清晰可见。它们都将行为或信念分为两类：对与错、真与假、理性与非理性。然后，它们援引社会学或心理学原因来解释这种划分的消极面。这些原因解释了错误、局限性和偏差。评价性划分的积极面则截然不同。在这里，逻辑、理性以及真理本身似乎就足以解释一切。在这里，无需援引社会心理因素。

Applied to the field of intellectual activity these views have the effect of making a body of knowledge an autonomous realm. Behaviour is to be explained by appeal to the procedures, results, methods and maxims of the activity itself. It makes successful and conventional intellectual activity appear self-explanatory and self-propelling. It becomes its own explanation. No expertise in sociology or psychology is required: only expertise in the intellectual activity itself.
这些观点若应用于智力活动领域，便会使知识体系成为一个独立的领域。行为的解释应当诉诸于活动本身的程序、结果、方法和准则。这使得成功且常规的智力活动显得不言自明、自发运转。它本身就成为了一种解释。无需具备社会学或心理学方面的专业知识，只需精通智力活动本身即可。

A currently fashionable version of this position is to be found in Lakatos’s (1971) theory about how the history of science ought to be written. This theory was explicitly meant to have implications for the sociology of science as well. The first prerequisite, says Lakatos, is that a philosophy or methodology of science be chosen. These are accounts of what science ought to be, and of what steps in it are rational. The chosen philosophy of science becomes the framework on which hangs all the subsequent work of explanation. Guided by this philosophy it ought to be possible to display science as a process which exemplifies its principles and develops in accord with its teachings. In as far as this can be done then science has been shown to be rational in the light of that philosophy. This task, of showing that science embodies certain methodological principles, Lakatos calls either ‘rational reconstruction’ or ‘internal history’. For example, an inductivist methodology would perhaps stress the emergence of theories out of an accumulation of observations. It would therefore focus on events like Kepler’s use of Tycho Brahe’s observations when formulating the laws of planetary motion.
目前，拉卡托斯（Lakatos，1971）关于科学史写作方式的理论体现了这种观点的流行版本。该理论明确旨在对科学社会学产生影响。拉卡托斯指出，首要前提是选择一种科学哲学或方法论。这些理论阐述了科学应有的本质，以及科学发展过程中哪些步骤是理性的。所选的科学哲学成为所有后续解释工作的框架。在这一哲学的指导下，科学应当展现为一个体现其原则并按照其教义发展的过程。如果能够做到这一点，那么根据该哲学，科学就被证明是理性的。拉卡托斯将这​​项证明科学体现某些方法论原则的任务称为“理性重建”或“内部历史”。例如，归纳主义方法论或许会强调理论是从观察的积累中产生的。因此，它将重点关注开普勒在制定行星运动定律时利用第谷·布拉赫的观测结果等事件。

It will never be possible, however, to capture all of the diversity of actual scientific practice by this means. Lakatos therefore insists that internal history will always need to be supplemented by an ‘external history’. This looks after the irrational residue. It is a matter which the philosophical historian will hand over to the ‘external historian’ or the sociologist. Thus, from an inductivist standpoint the role of Kepler’s mystical beliefs about the majesty of the sun would require a non-rational or external explanation.
然而，这种方法永远无法涵盖实际科学实践的所有多样性。因此，拉卡托斯坚持认为，内部历史始终需要“外部历史”的补充。外部历史负责处理非理性因素。哲学史学家会将这项工作交给“外部历史学家”或社会学家。因此，从归纳主义的立场来看，开普勒关于太阳威严的神秘信仰的作用需要一种非理性的或外部的解释。

The points to notice about this approach are first that internal history is self-sufficient and autonomous. To exhibit the rational character of a scientific development is sufficient explanation in itself of why the events took place. Second, not only are rational reconstructions autonomous; they also have an important priority over external history or sociology. The latter merely close the gap between rationality and actuality. This task is not even defined until internal history has had its say. Thus:
这种方法的要点在于：首先，内部历史是自足且独立的。展现科学发展的理性特征本身就足以解释事件发生的原因。其次，理性重构不仅是独立的，而且比外部历史或社会学具有重要的优先性。后者仅仅是弥合理性与现实之间的鸿沟。而这项任务只有在内部历史做出阐述之后才能明确。因此：

internal history is primary, external history only secondary, since the most important problems of external history are defined by internal history. External history either provides non-rational explanation of the speed, locality, selectiveness etc. of historical events as interpreted in terms of internal history; or when history differs from its rational reconstruction, it provides an empirical explanation of why it differs. But the rational aspect of scientific growth is fully accounted for by one’s logic of scientific discovery (1971, p. 9).
内部历史是首要的，外部历史只是次要的，因为外部历史最重要的问题是由内部历史界定的。外部历史要么对历史事件的速度、地域性、选择性等现象提供非理性的解释（这些现象是根据内部历史的视角来解读的），要么在历史与其理性重构存在差异时，提供经验性的解释来说明这种差异的原因。但是，科学发展的理性方面完全可以由科学发现的逻辑来解释（1971，第9页）。

Lakatos then answers the question of how to decide which philosophy should dictate the problems of external history or sociology. Alas for externalists the answer represents yet a further humiliation. Not only is their function derivative; it now transpires that the best philosophy of science, according to Lakatos, is one which minimises this role. Progress in philosophy of science is to be measured by the amount of actual history which can be exhibited as rational. The better the guiding methodology the more of actual science is rendered safe from the indignity of empirical explanation. The sociologist is allowed a crumb of comfort from the fact that Lakatos is only too pleased to grant that there will always be some irrational events in science that no philosophy will ever be able or willing to rescue. He instances here unsavoury episodes of Stalinist intervention in science like the Lysenko affair in biology.
拉卡托斯随后回答了如何决定哪种哲学应该指导外部历史或社会学问题的问题。对外部论者而言，这个答案无疑是雪上加霜。他们的功能不仅是衍生性的；而且现在看来，根据拉卡托斯的说法，最好的科学哲学恰恰是能够最大限度减少这种作用的哲学。科学哲学的进步应该以能够被理性地展现出来的实际历史的数量来衡量。指导方法论越好，实际科学就越能免于遭受经验解释的羞辱。社会学家可以从拉卡托斯乐于承认科学中总会存在一些非理性事件，而任何哲学都无法或不愿对其进行拯救这一事实中获得一丝安慰。他在此举例说明了斯大林主义干预科学的令人不齿的事件，例如生物学中的李森科事件。

These refinements however are less important than the general structure of the position. It does not matter how the central principles of rationality are chosen, or how they might change. The central point is that, once chosen, the rational aspects of science are held to be self-moving and self-explanatory. Empirical or sociological explanations are confined to the irrational.
然而，这些细化远不如该立场的总体结构重要。理性核心原则如何选择，或者它们如何变化，都无关紧要。关键在于，一旦选定，科学的理性方面就被认为是自发的、自解释的。经验主义或社会学的解释则被局限于非理性领域。

What can it mean to say that nothing makes people do or believe things which are rational or correct? Why in that case does the behaviour take place at all? What prompts the internal and correct functioning of an intellectual activity if the search for psychological and sociological causes is only deemed appropriate in the case of irrationality or error? The theory that must tacitly underlie these ideas is a goal-directed or teleological vision of knowledge and rationality.
如果说没有任何因素促使人们做出或相信理性或正确的行为，这究竟意味着什么？在这种情况下，这种行为为何会发生？如果只有在非理性或错误的情况下才需要探究心理和社会因素，那么是什么促成了智力活动的内在正确运作？这些观点背后必然隐含着一种目标导向或目的论的知识和理性观。

Suppose that it is assumed that truth, rationality and validity are our natural goals and the direction of certain natural tendencies with which we are endowed. We are rational animals and we naturally reason justly and cleave to the truth when it comes within our view. Beliefs that are true then clearly require no special comment. For them, their truth is all the explanation that is needed of why they are believed. On the other hand this self-propelling progress towards truth may be impeded or deflected and here natural causes must be located. These will account for ignorance, error, confused reasoning and any impediment to scientific progress.
假设真理、理性与有效性是我们与生俱来的目标，也是我们与生俱来的某些自然倾向的方向。我们是理性的动物，自然而然地会进行公正的推理，并在真理进入我们的视野时坚持真理。那么，那些为真的信念显然无需特别解释。对它们而言，其真理性本身就是人们相信它们的全部理由。另一方面，这种自我驱动的追求真理的过程可能会受到阻碍或偏离，这时就需要探究其自然原因。这些原因可以解释无知、错误、混乱的推理以及任何阻碍科学进步的因素。

Such a theory makes a great deal of sense of what is written in this area even if it seems implausible at first sight to impute it to contemporary thinkers. It even appears to have intruded itself into the thinking of Karl Mannheim. Despite his determination to set up causal and symmetrical canons of explanation, his nerve failed him when it came to such apparently autonomous subjects as mathematics and natural science. This failure expressed itself in passages such as the following, from ‘Ideology and Utopia’:
这种理论对这一领域的著述做出了许多合理的解释，即便乍看之下，将其归于当代思想家似乎有些牵强。它甚至似乎已经渗透到卡尔·曼海姆的思想中。尽管他决心建立因果对称的解释准则，但在面对数学和自然科学这类看似独立的学科时，他却显得力不从心。这种失败体现在《意识形态与乌托邦》中的以下段落中：

The existential determination of thought may be regarded as a demonstrated fact in those realms of thought in which we can show . . . that the process of knowing does not actually develop historically in accordance with immanent laws, that it does not follow only for the ‘nature of things’ or from ‘pure logical possibilities’, and that it is not driven by an ‘inner dialectic’. On the contrary, the emergence and the crystallization of actual thought is influenced in many decisive points by extra-theoretical factors of the most diverse sort (1936, p. 239).
在某些思想领域，我们可以证明……认知过程实际上并非按照内在规律在历史中发展，并非仅仅源于“事物的本质”或“纯粹的逻辑可能性”，也并非由“内在辩证法”驱动。相反，实际思想的产生和形成在许多关键点上受到各种各样的超理论因素的影响（1936，第239页）。

Here social causes are being equated with ‘extra-theoretical’ factors. But where does this leave behaviour conducted in accord with the inner logic of a theory or governed by theoretical factors? Clearly it is in danger of being excluded from sociological explanation because it functions as the base-line for locating those things which do require explanation. It is as if Mannheim slipped into sharing the sentiments expressed in the quotations from Ryle and Lakatos and said to himself, ‘When we do what is logical and proceed correctly, nothing more needs to be said.’ But to see certain sorts of behaviour as unproblematic is to see them as natural. In this case what is natural is proceeding correctly, that is via or towards the truth. So here too the teleological model is probably at work.
这里，社会原因被等同于“理论之外”的因素。但是，如果行为符合某种理论的内在逻辑或受理论因素支配，那么这种行为又该如何解释呢？显然，它有可能被排除在社会学解释之外，因为它构成了定位那些需要解释的事物的基准。这就像曼海姆不知不觉地认同了赖尔和拉卡托斯引述的观点，并自言自语道：“当我们做合乎逻辑的事情，并且行事正确时，就无需再多说什么了。”然而，将某些行为视为理所当然，就等于将其视为自然而然。在这种情况下，自然而然的就是正确地行事，也就是通过真理或朝着真理前进。因此，目的论模式可能也在这里发挥作用。

How does this model of knowledge relate to the tenets of the strong programme? Clearly it violates them in a number of serious ways. It relinquishes a thorough-going causal orientation. Causes can only be located for error. Thus the sociology of knowledge is confined to the sociology of error. In addition it violates the requirements of symmetry and impartiality. A prior evaluation of the truth or rationality of a belief is called for before it can be decided whether it is to be counted as self-explanatory or whether a causal theory is needed. There is no doubt that if the teleological model is true then the strong programme is false.
这种知识模型与强纲领的原则有何关联？显然，它在诸多方面严重违背了这些原则。它放弃了彻底的因果导向，认为只能为错误寻找原因。因此，知识社会学被局限于错误社会学。此外，它还违反了对称性和公正性的要求。在决定某个信念是否具有自解释性或是否需要因果理论之前，必须先对其真伪或合理性进行评估。毫无疑问，如果目的论模型为真，那么强纲领就是错误的。

The teleological and causal models, then, represent programmatic alternatives which quite exclude one another. Indeed, they are two opposed metaphysical standpoints. This may make it appear that it is necessary to decide at the outset which is true. Doesn’t the sociology of knowledge depend on the teleological view being false? So doesn’t this have to be established before the strong programme dare proceed? The answer is ‘no’. It is more sensible to look at matters the other way round. It is unlikely that any decisive, independent grounds could be adduced ‘a priori’ to prove the truth or falsity of such major metaphysical alternatives. Where objections and arguments are proposed against one of the two theories it will be found that they depend on and presuppose the other, and so beg the question at issue. All that can be done is to check the internal consistency of the different theories and then see what happens when practical research and theorising is based upon them. If their truth can be decided at all it will only be after they have been adopted and used, not before. So the sociology of knowledge is not bound to eliminate the rival standpoint. It only has to separate itself from it, reject it, and make sure that its own house is in logical order.
因此，目的论模型和因果论模型代表了两种完全互斥的纲领性选择。事实上，它们是两种对立的形而上学立场。这或许会让人觉得，必须一开始就决定哪一种是正确的。知识社会学难道不是建立在目的论观点为假的基础上的吗？那么，在强有力的纲领得以推进之前，难道不应该先证明这一点吗？答案是否定的。更明智的做法是反过来思考。不太可能先验地提出任何决定性的、独立的理由来证明如此重要的形而上学选择是真还是假。当有人对其中一种理论提出反对意见和论证时，会发现这些意见和论证依赖于另一种理论并为其预设，从而犯了循环论证的错误。我们所能做的，就是检验不同理论的内部一致性，然后观察当基于这些理论进行实践研究和理论构建时会发生什么。如果说这些观点的真假最终能够得到判定，那也只能在它们被采纳和运用之后，而非之前。因此，知识社会学并非一定要消除对立的观点，而只需与它们划清界限，摒弃它们，并确保自身的体系逻辑严密。

These objections to the strong programme are thus not based on the intrinsic nature of knowledge but only on knowledge viewed from the standpoint of the teleological model. Reject that model and all its associated distinctions, evaluations and asymmetries go with it. It is only if that model has a unique claim to attention that its corresponding patterns of explanation are binding upon us. Its mere existence, and the fact that some thinkers find it natural to use it, do not endow it with probative force.
因此，这些对强纲领的反对并非基于知识的内在本质，而仅仅基于从目的论模型视角看待知识。摒弃该模型及其所有相关的区分、评价和不对称性。只有当该模型具有独特的关注点时，其相应的解释模式才对我们具有约束力。该模型的存在本身，以及某些思想家认为运用该模型是自然而然的，并不能赋予其证明力。

In its own terms the teleological model is no doubt perfectly consistent and there are perhaps no logical reasons why anyone should prefer the causal approach to the goal-directed view. There are, however, methodological considerations which may influence the choice in favour of the strong programme.
就其自身而言，目的论模型无疑完全自洽，或许也没有任何逻辑理由表明人们会更倾向于因果论方法而非目标导向的观点。然而，方法论上的考量可能会影响人们选择强纲领模型。

If explanation is allowed to hinge on prior evaluations, then the causal processes that are thought to operate in the world will come to reflect the pattern of these evaluations. Causal processes will be made to etch out the pattern of perceived error, throwing into relief the shape of truth and rationality. Nature will take on a moral significance, endorsing and embodying truth and right. Those who indulge their tendencies to offer asymmetrical explanations will thus have every opportunity to represent as natural what they take for granted. It is an ideal recipe for turning one’s gaze away from one’s own society, values and beliefs and attending only to deviations from them.
如果允许解释依赖于先前的评价，那么人们认为在世界中运行的因果过程就会反映出这些评价的模式。因果过程将被用来勾勒出人们感知到的错误模式，从而凸显真理和理性的本质。自然将获得道德意义，成为真理和正义的化身和象征。那些沉溺于提供非对称解释的人，便有充分的机会将他们习以为常的事物描绘成自然现象。这无疑是将人们的目光从自身的社会、价值观和信仰上移开，而只关注与之相悖之处的理想方法。

Care is needed not to overstate this point, for the strong programme does exactly the same thing in certain respects. It is also based on values, for example: the desire for generality of a specific kind and for a conception of the natural world as morally empty and neutral. So it too insists on giving nature a certain role with respect to morality, albeit of a negative kind. This means that it too represents as natural what it takes for granted.
需要注意的是，不要过分强调这一点，因为强纲领在某些方面也做了同样的事情。它也基于价值观，例如：对某种特定类型的普遍性的渴望，以及对自然界道德空洞和中立的观念。因此，它也坚持赋予自然在道德方面某种角色，尽管是消极的。这意味着，它也将那些它视为理所当然的事物视为自然。

What may be said, however, is that the strong programme possesses a certain kind of moral neutrality, namely the same kind as we have learned to associate with all the other sciences. It also imposes on itself the need for the same kind of generality as other sciences. It would be a betrayal of these values, of the approach of empirical science, to choose to adopt the teleological view. Obviously these are not reasons which could compel anyone to adopt the causal view. For some they may be precisely the reasons that would incline them to reject causality and adopt asymmetrical, teleological conceptions. But these points do make clear the ramifications of the choice and expose those values that are going to inform the approach to knowledge. From this type of confrontation, then, the sociology of knowledge can proceed, if it so chooses, without let or hinderance.
然而，可以肯定的是，强有力的纲领具有某种道德中立性，这种中立性与我们通常认为所有其他科学所具有的道德中立性相同。它也像其他科学一样，要求自身具备同样的普遍性。选择目的论的观点，就等于背叛了这些价值观，背叛了经验科学的方法。显然，这些理由不足以迫使任何人接受因果论的观点。对某些人来说，这些理由恰恰可能促使他们拒绝因果关系，转而接受不对称的目的论观念。但这些要点确实阐明了选择的意义，并揭示了那些将影响知识观的价值观。因此，知识社会学可以从这种对抗中出发，如果愿意的话，畅通无阻地向前发展。

The Argument from Empiricism
经验主义论证

The premise underlying the teleological model was that causality is associated with error or limitation. This represents an extreme form of asymmetry and so stands as the most radical alternative to the strong programme with its insistence on symmetrical styles of explanation. It may be, however, that the strong programme can be criticised from a less extreme standpoint. Is it not plausible to say that some causes bring about erroneous belief whilst others bring about true belief? If it further transpires that certain types of causes are systematically correlated with true and false belief, respectively, then here is another basis for rejecting the symmetrical standpoint of the strong programme.
目的论模型的基本前提是因果关系与错误或局限性相关。这代表了一种极端形式的不对称性，因此是对坚持对称解释方式的强纲领最激进的替代方案。然而，或许可以从一个不那么极端的视角来批判强纲领。难道不能说，某些原因导致错误的信念，而另一些原因导致正确的信念吗？如果进一步证明某些类型的原因分别与正确的信念和错误的信念存在系统性的关联，那么这就构成了拒绝强纲领对称立场的另一个依据。

Consider the following theory: social influences produce distortions in our beliefs whilst the uninhibited use of our faculties of perception and our sensory-motor apparatus produce true beliefs. This praise for experience as a source of knowledge can be seen as encouraging individuals to rely on their own physical and psychological resources for getting to know the world. It is a statement of faith in the power of our animal capacities for knowledge. Give these full play and their natural, but causal, operation will yield knowledge tested and tried in practical interaction with the world. Depart from this path, rely on one’s fellows, and one will be prey to superstitious stories, myth and speculation. At best these stories will be second-hand belief rather than first-hand knowledge. At worst the motives behind them will be corrupt, the product of liars and tyrants.
请思考以下理论：社会影响会扭曲我们的信念，而不受约束地运用我们的感知能力和感觉运动系统则会产生真实的信念。这种对经验作为知识来源的推崇，可以被视为鼓励人们依靠自身的生理和心理资源来认识世界。这是一种对我们动物性认知能力的信念。充分发挥这些能力，它们自然而然的因果运作将产生经受过实践检验的知识。如果偏离这条道路，依赖他人，就会成为迷信故事、神话和臆测的受害者。这些故事充其量只是二手信息，而非第一手知识。而最糟糕的是，它们背后的动机是腐败的，是谎言和暴君的产物。

It is not difficult to recognise this picture. It is a version of Bacon’s warning to avoid the Idols of the Market Place and the Theatre. Much of standard empiricism represents a refined and rarified statement of this approach to knowledge. Although the current fashion amongst empiricist philosophers is to avoid the psychological rendering of their theory the basic vision is not too dissimilar to that sketched above. I shall therefore refer to the above theory without more ado as empiricism.
不难辨认出这幅图景。它呼应了培根关于远离市场和剧院偶像的警告。许多标准的经验主义理论正是对这种知识观的一种精炼而独特的阐述。尽管目前经验主义哲学家们倾向于避免从心理学角度解读他们的理论，但其基本理念与上述描述并无太大差异。因此，我将不再赘述，直接将上述理论称为经验主义。

If empiricism is correct then once again the sociology of knowledge is really the sociology of error, belief or opinion, but not knowledge as such. This conclusion is not as extreme as that derived from the teleological model of knowledge. It amounts to a division of labour between the psychologist and sociologist where the former would deal with real knowledge, the latter with error or something less than knowledge. The total enterprise would nevertheless be naturalistic and causal. There is therefore no question, as there was with the teleological model, of being confronted with a choice between a scientific perspective and a standpoint which embodies quite different values. Here the battle has to be fought entirely within science’s own territory. Is the boundary between truth and error correctly drawn by this empiricist conception of knowledge? There are two shortcomings in empiricism which suggest that it is not.
如果经验主义是正确的，那么知识社会学实际上就是谬误、信念或观点的社会学，而非知识本身的社会学。这一结论并不像目的论知识模型所得出的结论那样极端。它相当于心理学家和社会学家之间的一种分工：前者研究真正的知识，后者研究谬误或某种低于知识的东西。尽管如此，整个研究仍然是自然主义的、因果性的。因此，不像目的论模型那样，我们无需在科学视角和体现截然不同价值观的立场之间做出选择。在这里，争论必须完全在科学自身的领域内进行。这种经验主义的知识观是否正确地划定了真理与谬误之间的界限？经验主义存在两个缺陷，表明它并没有正确划定界限。

First, it would be wrong to assume that the natural working of our animal resources always produces knowledge. They produce a mixture of knowledge and error with equal naturalness, and through the operation of one and the same type of cause. For example, a medium level of anxiety will often increase the learning and successful performance of a task compared with a very low level, but the performance will then drop again if the anxiety level gets too high. As a laboratory phenomenon the point is fairly general. A certain level of hunger will facilitate an animal’s retention of information about its environment, as in a rat’s learning of a laboratory maze for food. A very high level of hunger may well produce urgent and successful learning of the whereabouts of food, but it will lower the natural ability to pick up cues which are irrelevant to the current, overriding concern. These examples suggest that different causal conditions may indeed be associated with different patterns of true and false belief. However, they do not show that different types of cause correlate simply with true and false belief. In particular they show that it is incorrect to put psychological causes all on one side of this divide, as naturally leading to truth.
首先，认为动物的自然运作总是产生知识是错误的。它们自然而然地产生知识和错误，而且都是由同一种原因造成的。例如，中等程度的焦虑通常会比极低的焦虑水平提高学习能力和任务完成率，但如果焦虑水平过高，完成率又会下降。作为一种实验室现象，这一点相当普遍。一定程度的饥饿感会促进动物记住有关环境的信息，例如老鼠在实验室迷宫中寻找食物。极度饥饿感或许能促使动物迅速且成功地找到食物，但也会降低它们捕捉与当前首要关注点无关线索的自然能力。这些例子表明，不同的因果条件可能确实与不同的真假信念模式相关联。然而，它们并不能证明不同类型的原因与真假信念之间存在简单的对应关系。它们尤其表明，将所有心理原因都归为这一分界线的一边是错误的，认为心理原因自然而然地会导致真理。

No doubt this shortcoming could be corrected. Perhaps all that the counter-examples show is that psychological learning mechanisms have an optimum working arrangement and that they produce error when they are thrown out of focus. It may be insisted that when our perceptual apparatus is operating under normal conditions, and performing its functions properly, then it brings about true belief. This revision of the doctrine may be granted because there is a far more important objection to it to be considered.
毫无疑问，这一缺陷是可以纠正的。或许反例仅仅表明，心理学习机制存在一个最佳运作状态，一旦偏离最佳状态就会产生错误。有人可能会坚持认为，当我们的感知器官在正常条件下运作并正确履行其功能时，它就能产生正确的信念。之所以允许对这一理论进行修正，是因为存在一个更为重要的反对意见需要考虑。

The crucial point about empiricism is its individualistic character. Those aspects of knowledge which each of us can and has to furnish for himself may be adequately explained by this type of model. But how much of man’s knowledge, and how much of his science is built up by the individual relying simply on the interaction of the world with his animal capacities? Probably very little. The important question is: what analysis is to be given to the remainder? It is plausible to say that the psychological approach leaves out of account the social component of knowledge.
经验主义的关键在于其个人主义特征。我们每个人能够且必须自行提供的知识，或许可以用这种模型来充分解释。但是，人类的知识和科学中，有多少是个人仅仅依靠自身动物本能与世界的互动而构建起来的呢？可能少之又少。重要的问题是：如何分析剩余的知识？可以说，心理学方法忽略了知识的社会成分。

Does not individual experience, as a matter of fact, take place within a framework of assumptions, standards, purposes and meanings which are shared? Society furnishes the mind of the individual with these things and also provides the conditions whereby they can be sustained and reinforced. If the individual’s grasp of them wavers, there are agencies ready to remind him; if his view of the world begins to deviate there are mechanisms which encourage realignment. The necessities of communication help to sustain collective patterns of thought in the individual psyche. As well as the individual’s sensory experience of the natural world, there is, then, something that points beyond that experience, that provides a framework for it and gives it a wider significance. It fills out the individual’s sense of what that overall Reality is, that his experience is experience of.
事实上，个人经验难道不是在共享的假设、标准、目的和意义框架内发生的吗？社会为个人的心灵提供这些东西，也提供维持和强化它们的条件。如果个人对这些要素的理解动摇了，就会有相应的机制提醒他；如果他的世界观开始偏离，就会有相应的机制鼓励他重新调整。沟通的必要性有助于维持个人心理中的集体思维模式。因此，除了个人对自然世界的感官体验之外，还有某种东西指向这种体验之外，为它提供了一个框架，并赋予它更广泛的意义。它完善了个人对整体现实的感知，而他的经验正是对整体现实的体验。

The knowledge of a society designates not so much the sensory experience of its individual members, or the sum of what may be called their animal knowledge. It is rather, their collective vision or visions of Reality. Thus the knowledge of our culture, as it is represented in our science, is not knowledge of a reality that any individual can experience or learn about for himself. It is what our best attested theories, and our most informed thoughts tell us is the case, despite what the appearances may say. It is a story woven out of the hints and glimpses that we believe our experiments offer us. Knowledge then, is better equated with Culture than Experience.
一个社会的知识并非指其成员的感官体验，也并非指他们所谓的“动物性知识”的总和，而是他们集体对现实的认知或理解。因此，我们文化中体现在科学中的知识，并非任何个体都能亲身体验或了解的现实知识。它是由我们最可靠的理论和最深入的思考告诉我们的事实，即便表面现象可能并非如此。它是由我们相信实验所提供的线索和启示编织而成的故事。因此，知识更应等同于文化，而非经验。

If this designation of the word ‘knowledge’ is accepted then the distinction between truth and error is not the same as the distinction between (optimum) individual experience and social influence. Rather it becomes a distinction within the amalgam of experiences and socially mediated beliefs that make up the content of a culture. It is a discrimination between rival mixtures of experience and belief. The same two ingredients occur in true and false beliefs and so the way is open for symmetrical styles of explanation which invoke the same types of cause.
如果接受“知识”一词的这种定义，那么真理与谬误之间的区别就不再等同于（最佳）个人经验与社会影响之间的区别。相反，它变成了一种在构成文化内容的经验和社会中介信念的混合体内部进行的区分。它是对相互竞争的经验和信念混合物的辨别。真假信念都包含相同的两个要素，因此，对称的解释方式为可能，即援引相同类型的因果关系。

One way of putting this point which may assist its recognition and acceptance is to say that what we count as scientific knowledge is largely ‘theoretical’. It is largely a theoretical vision of the world that, at any given time, scientists may be said to know. It is largely to their theories that scientists must repair when asked what they can tell us about the world. But theories and theoretical knowledge are not things which are given in our experience. They are what give meaning to experience by offering a story about what underlies, connects and accounts for it. This does not mean that theory is unresponsive to experience. It is, but it is not given along with the experience it explains, nor is it uniquely supported by it. Another agency apart from the physical world is required to guide and support this component of knowledge. The theoretical component of knowledge is a social component, and it is a necessary part of truth, not a sign of mere error.
阐明这一点的一种方式或许有助于人们理解和接受：我们所认为的科学知识在很大程度上是“理论性的”。它主要是一种关于世界的理论愿景，科学家们在任何特定时刻都可以说自己了解这种愿景。当被问及他们能告诉我们关于世界什么时，科学家们很大程度上必须依赖他们的理论。但是，理论和理论知识并非源于我们的经验。它们通过提供一个关于经验的内在联系、解释和基础的叙事，赋予经验意义。这并不意味着理论对经验无动于衷。它确实会回应经验，但它并非与它所解释的经验同时存在，也并非完全由经验支撑。除了物质世界之外，还需要另一种力量来引导和支撑知识的这一组成部分。知识的理论组成部分是社会组成部分，它是真理的必要组成部分，而非仅仅是错误的标志。

Two major sources of opposition to the sociology of knowledge have now been discussed and both have been rejected. The teleological model was indeed a radical alternative to the strong programme but there is not the slightest compulsion to accept it. The empiricist theory is implausible as a description of what we in fact count as our knowledge. It provides some of the bricks but is silent on the designs of the varying edifices that we build with them. The next step will be to relate these two positions to what is perhaps the most typical of all objections to the sociology of knowledge. This is the claim that it is a self-refuting form of relativism.
我们已经讨论了知识社会学面临的两大主要反对意见，并且都予以驳斥。目的论模型的确是强纲领论的激进替代方案，但我们完全没有理由接受它。经验主义理论作为对我们实际认知知识的描述，显得牵强附会。它提供了一些基础，但对于我们用这些基础构建的各种知识体系的设计却只字未提。下一步，我们将把这两种立场与知识社会学最典型的反对意见联系起来，即认为它是一种自相矛盾的相对主义。

The Argument from Self-Refutation
自我反驳论证

If someone’s beliefs are totally caused and if there is necessarily within them a component provided by society then it has seemed to many critics that these beliefs are bound to be false or unjustified. Any thorough-going sociological theory of belief then appears to be caught in a trap. For are not sociologists bound to admit that their own thoughts are determined, and in part even socially determined? Must they not therefore admit that their own claims are false in proportion to the strength of this determination? The result appears to be that no sociological theory can be general in its scope otherwise it would reflexively enmesh itself in error and destroy its own credibility. The sociology of knowledge is thus itself unworthy of belief or it must make exceptions for scientific or objective investigations and hence confine itself to the sociology of error. There can be no self-consistent, causal and general sociology of knowledge, especially not scientific knowledge.
如果一个人的信念完全由社会因素造成，并且其中必然包含社会因素，那么在许多批评者看来，这些信念注定是错误的或不合理的。任何彻底的社会学信念理论似乎都陷入了困境。因为社会学家难道不应该承认他们自身的思想是受社会因素决定的吗？他们难道不应该承认，他们自身的论断与这种决定因素的强度成正比吗？其结果似乎是，任何社会学理论都无法具有普遍性，否则它就会不由自主地陷入谬误，并丧失自身的信誉。因此，知识社会学本身就不值得信赖，或者它必须为科学或客观的研究做出例外，从而将自身局限于谬误社会学。不可能存在自洽的、因果的和普遍的知识社会学，尤其不可能存在科学知识的社会学。

It can be seen at once that this argument depends on one or the other of the two conceptions of knowledge discussed above, namely the teleological model or a form of individualistic empiricism. The conclusion follows, and it only follows, if these theories are first granted. This is because the argument takes as its premise their central idea that causation implies error, deviation or limitation. This premise may be in the extreme form that any causation destroys credibility or in the weaker form that only social causation has this effect. One or the other is crucial for the argument.
显而易见，这一论证依赖于上文讨论的两种知识观之一，即目的论模型或某种形式的个人主义经验主义。结论的成立，也只有在这些理论首先成立的前提下才能成立。这是因为该论证以它们的核心思想为前提，即因果关系意味着错误、偏差或局限性。这一前提可以极端地表现为任何因果关系都会破坏可信度，也可以较弱地表现为只有社会因果关系才会产生这种影响。二者必居其一，对论证至关重要。

These premises have been responsible for a plethora of feeble and badly argued attacks on the sociology of knowledge. Mostly the attacks have failed to make explicit the premises on which they rest. If they had, their weakness would have been more easily exposed. Their apparent strength has derived from the fact that their real basis was hidden or simply unknown. Here is an example of one of the much better forms of this argument which does make quite clear the standpoint from which it derives.
这些前提导致了对知识社会学的大量薄弱且论证不力的攻击。大多数攻击未能明确阐述其所依据的前提。如果它们这样做了，其弱点就更容易被揭露。它们表面上的强势源于其真实基础的隐藏或根本未知。以下是一个此类论证中较为优秀的例子，它清晰地阐明了其立场。

Grünwald, an early critic of Mannheim, is explicit in his statement of the assumption that social determination is bound to enmesh a thinker in error. In the introduction to Mannheim’s ‘Essays on the Sociology of Knowledge’ (1952) Grünwald is quoted as saying: ‘it is impossible to make any meaningful statement about the existential determination of ideas without having any Archimedean point beyond all existential determination . . .’ (p. 29). Grünwald goes on to draw the conclusion that any theory, such as Mannheim’s, which suggests that all thought is subject to social determination must refute itself. Thus: ‘No long argument is needed to show beyond doubt that this version of sociologism, too, is a form of scepticism and therefore refutes itself. For the thesis that all thinking is existentially determined and cannot claim to be true claims itself to be true’ (p. 29).
格林瓦尔德是曼海姆的早期批评者，他明确指出，社会决定论必然会使思想家陷入谬误之中。在曼海姆的《知识社会学论文集》（1952）的导言中，格林瓦尔德被引述说：“如果不存在超越一切存在决定论的阿基米德点，就不可能对观念的存在决定论做出任何有意义的陈述……”（第29页）。格林瓦尔德进而得出结论：任何认为所有思想都受制于社会决定论的理论，例如曼海姆的理论，都必然自相矛盾。因此：“无需长篇论证就能毫无疑问地表明，这种社会学主义也是一种怀疑论，因此自相矛盾。因为‘所有思想都受存在决定论支配且不能自称真’这一论点，本身就自称是真。”（第29页）

This would be a cogent objection against any theory that did indeed assert that existential determination implied falsity. But its premise should be challenged for what it is: a gratuitous assumption and an unrealistic demand. If knowledge does depend on a vantage point outside society and if truth does depend on stepping above the causal nexus of social relations, then we may give them up as lost.
这可以作为对任何声称存在决定论蕴含谬误的理论的有力反驳。但其前提本身就应该受到质疑：它是一种毫无根据的假设和不切实际的要求。如果知识确实依赖于社会之外的视角，如果真理确实依赖于超越社会关系的因果纽带，那么我们或许应该放弃它们，因为它们已经失传了。

There are a variety of other forms of this argument. One typical version is to observe that research into the causation of belief is itself offered to the world as being correct and objective. Therefore, the argument goes, the sociologist assumes that objective knowledge is possible, so not everybody’s beliefs can be socially determined. As the historian Lovejoy (1940) put it: ‘Even they, then, necessarily presuppose possible limitations or exceptions to their generalisations in the act of defending them’ (p. 18). The limitations the ‘sociological relativists’ are said necessarily to presuppose are designed to make room for criteria of factual truth and valid inference. So this objection, too, depends on the premise that factual truth and valid inference would be violated by beliefs that are determined, or at least socially determined.
这种论证还有其他多种形式。一种典型的版本是指出，对信念成因的研究本身就被认为是正确且客观的。因此，这种论证认为，社会学家假定客观知识是可能的，所以并非所有人的信念都由社会决定。正如历史学家洛夫乔伊（Lovejoy，1940）所言：“即使是他们，在捍卫其概括的过程中，也必然预设了这些概括可能存在的局限性或例外”（第 18 页）。据说，“社会学相对主义者”必然预设的这些局限性，旨在为事实真理和有效推理的标准留出空间。因此，这种反对意见也依赖于这样一个前提：事实真理和有效推理会被那些由社会决定的信念所违背，或者至少是受社会决定的信念。

Because these arguments have become so taken for granted their formulation has become abbreviated and routine. They can now be given in such condensed versions as the following, provided by Bottomore (1956): ‘For if all propositions are existentially determined and no proposition is absolutely true, then this proposition itself, if true, is not absolutely true, but is existentially determined’ (p. 52).
由于这些论证已被视为理所当然，它们的表述也变得简短而常规。现在，它们可以用如下简略版本来表述，如 Bottomore (1956) 所提供：“因为如果所有命题都是存在性决定的，并且没有一个命题是绝对正确的，那么这个命题本身，如果为真，也不是绝对正确的，而是存在性决定的”（第 52 页）。

The premise, that causation implies error, on which all these arguments depend has been exposed and rejected. The arguments can therefore be disposed of along with them. Whether a belief is to be judged true or false has nothing to do with whether it has a cause.
所有这些论证都依赖于一个前提，即因果关系必然导致错误，而这个前提已被揭露并驳斥。因此，这些论证也随之被驳斥。判断一个信念是真还是假，与它是否有原因无关。

The Argument from Future Knowledge
来自未来知识的论证

Social determinism and historical determinism are closely related ideas. Those who believe there are laws governing social processes and societies will wonder if there are also laws governing their historical succession and development. To believe that ideas are determined by social milieu is but one form of believing that they are, in some sense, relative to the actor’s historical position. It is therefore not surprising that the sociology of knowledge has been criticised by those who believe that the very idea of historical laws is based on error and confusion. One such critic is Karl Popper (1960). It will be the purpose of this section to refute these criticisms as far as they may be applied to the sociology of knowledge.
社会决定论和历史决定论是密切相关的概念。那些相信社会进程和社会存在规律的人会质疑，是否存在支配其历史演进和发展的规律。认为观念受社会环境决定，实际上就是认为观念在某种意义上与行动者的历史地位相关。因此，知识社会学受到那些认为历史规律本身就建立在谬误和混乱之上的人的批评也就不足为奇了。卡尔·波普尔（1960）就是这样一位批评者。本节的目的在于反驳这些批评，尤其是在它们适用于知识社会学的范围内。

The reason why the search for laws is held to be wrong is that if they could be found they would imply the possibility of prediction. A sociology which furnished laws could permit the prediction of future beliefs. In principle it would seem to be possible, to know what the physics of the future would be like just as it is possible to predict future states of a mechanical system. If the laws of the mechanism are known along with a knowledge of its initial position, and the masses and forces on its parts, then all the future positions may be predicted.
之所以认为寻找规律是错误的，是因为如果规律能够被找到，就意味着预测的可能性。一种能够提供规律的社会学或许就能预测未来的信仰。原则上，似乎可以像预测机械系统的未来状态一样，了解未来的物理规律。如果已知机械体的规律、初始位置以及各部件的质量和受力情况，那么所有未来的位置都可以预测。

Popper’s objection to this ambition is partly informal and partly formal. He informally observes that human behaviour and society just do not furnish the same spectacle of repeated cycles of events as do some limited portions of the natural world. So long-term predictions are hardly realistic. This much may be certainly granted.
波普尔对这种雄心壮志的反对意见既有非正式的，也有正式的。他非正式地指出，人类行为和社会并不像自然界某些特定部分那样，呈现出事件重复循环的壮观景象。因此，长期预测几乎是不现实的。这一点当然是可以肯定的。

The nub of the argument, however, is a logical point about the nature of knowledge. It is impossible, says Popper, to predict future knowledge. The reason is that any such prediction would itself amount to the discovery of that knowledge. The way we behave depends on what we know so behaviour in the future will depend on this unpredictable knowledge and this too will be unpredictable. This argument appears to depend on a peculiar property of knowledge and to result in a gulf between the natural sciences and the social sciences in as far as they dare to touch humans as knowers. It suggests that the aspirations of the strong programme with its search for causes and laws is misguided and that something more modestly empirical is called for. Perhaps sociology should again restrict itself to no more than a chronicle of errors or a catalogue of external circumstances which help or hinder science.
然而，这一论证的核心在于对知识本质的一个逻辑性思考。波普尔认为，预测未来的知识是不可能的。原因在于，任何此类预测本身就相当于发现了该知识。我们的行为取决于我们已知的知识，因此未来的行为将取决于这种不可预测的知识，而这种知识本身也是不可预测的。这一论证似乎依赖于知识的一种特殊属性，并导致自然科学和社会科学之间出现鸿沟，因为它们都不敢触及作为认知主体的人类。它暗示，以探寻原因和规律为目标的强纲领的抱负是误入歧途的，我们需要一种更为谦逊的经验主义方法。或许社会学应该再次将自身限制在对错误的记录或对促进或阻碍科学的外部环境的罗列之中。

In fact the point which Popper makes is a correct though trite one which, properly understood, merely serves to emphasise the similarities rather than the differences between the social and the natural sciences. Consider the following argument which moves along exactly the same steps as Popper’s but would, if correct, prove that the physical world is unpredictable. This will jerk our critical faculties into action. The argument is this: It is impossible to make predictions in physics which utilise or refer to physical processes of which we have no knowledge. But the course of the physical world will depend in part on the operation of these unknown factors. Therefore the physical world is unpredictable.
事实上，波普尔的观点虽然老生常谈，但却是正确的。正确理解后，它仅仅强调了社会科学和自然科学之间的相似之处，而非差异。请看以下论证，其步骤与波普尔的论证完全相同，但如果正确，则可证明物理世界是不可预测的。这将促使我们运用批判性思维。论证如下：在物理学中，我们不可能对那些利用或涉及我们一无所知的物理过程做出预测。但物理世界的运行在一定程度上取决于这些未知因素的运作。因此，物理世界是不可预测的。

Naturally the objection will be raised that all that this proves is that our predictions will often be wrong, not that nature is unpredictable. Our predictions will be falsified in as far as they fail to take into account relevant facts that we did not know were involved. Exactly the same rejoinder can be made to the argument against historical laws. Really Popper is offering an inductive argument based on our record of ignorance and failure. All that it points to is that our historical and sociological predictions will usually be false. The reason for this is correctly located by Popper. It is that people’s future actions will often be contingent on things which they will know, but which we do not know now, and of which we therefore take no account when we make the prediction. The correct conclusion to be drawn for the social sciences is that we are unlikely to make much headway predicting the behaviour and beliefs of others unless we know at least as much as they do about their situation. There is nothing in the argument which need discourage the sociologist of knowledge from developing conjectural theories on the basis of empirical and historical case studies and testing them by further studies. Limited knowledge and the vast scope for error will ensure that these predictions will mostly be false. On the other hand the fact that social life depends on regularity and order gives grounds for hope that some progress will be possible. It is worth remembering that Popper himself sees science as an endless vista of refuted conjectures. Since this vision was not intended to intimidate natural scientists there is no reason why it should appear in this light when it is applied to the social sciences—despite the fact that this is how Popper has chosen to present it.
当然，有人会提出异议，认为这只能证明我们的预测常常是错误的，而不是自然本身不可预测。我们的预测之所以会被证伪，是因为它们未能考虑到我们当时并不知晓的相关事实。同样的道理也适用于反对历史规律的论点。实际上，波普尔提出的归纳论证是基于我们过往的无知和失败。它所指向的仅仅是，我们的历史和社会学预测通常是错误的。波普尔正确地指出了其中的原因。人们未来的行为往往取决于他们未来会知道但我们现在却不知道的事情，因此我们在进行预测时并没有考虑到这些因素。对于社会科学而言，正确的结论是：除非我们至少像他们一样了解自身处境，否则我们很难在预测他人的行为和信念方面取得多大进展。论证中没有任何内容足以阻止知识社会学家基于经验和历史案例研究发展推测性理论，并通过进一步研究加以检验。知识的局限性和误差的巨大空间注定了这些预测大多是错误的。另一方面，社会生活依赖于规律性和秩序这一事实，为取得一些进步提供了希望。值得注意的是，波普尔本人将科学视为一个充满被驳斥的推测的无尽领域。既然这种观点并非意在吓退自然科学家，那么当它应用于社会科学时，就没有理由呈现出这种景象——尽管波普尔正是这样表达的。

But still the objection must be met: doesn’t the social world present us with mere trends and tendencies and not the genuine law-like regularity of the natural world? Trends, of course, are merely contingent and superficial drifts rather than reliable necessities within phenomena. The answer is that this distinction is spurious. Take the orbiting planets, which are the usual symbols of law rather than trend. In fact the solar system is a mere physical tendency. It endures because nothing disturbs it. There was a time when it did not exist and it is easy to imagine how it might be disrupted: a large gravitating body could pass close by it, or the sun could explode. Nor do the basic laws of nature even require the planets to move in ellipses. They only happen to orbit round the sun because of their conditions of origin and formation. Whilst obeying the same law of attraction their trajectories could be very different. No: the empirical surface of the natural world is dominated by tendencies. These tendencies wax and wane because of an underlying tattle of laws, conditions and contingencies. Our scientific understanding seeks to tease out those laws which, as we are prone to say, are ‘behind’ observable states of affairs. The contrast between the natural and social worlds on which the objection depends fails to compare like with like. It compares the laws found to underly physical tendencies with the purely empirical surface of social tendencies.
然而，我们仍然需要回应这样的质疑：社会世界难道不只是呈现出各种趋势和倾向，而非自然界那种真正遵循规律的规律吗？当然，趋势仅仅是偶然的、表面的漂移，而非现象中可靠的必然规律。答案是，这种区分是虚假的。以环绕太阳运行的行星为例，它们通常被视为规律而非趋势的象征。事实上，太阳系仅仅是一种物理趋势。它之所以能够存在，是因为没有任何事物能够扰乱它。曾经，太阳系并不存在，我们很容易想象它会如何被扰乱：一个巨大的引力天体可能从它附近经过，或者太阳可能爆炸。自然界的基本规律甚至并不要求行星沿椭圆轨道运行。它们之所以恰好围绕太阳运行，仅仅是因为它们的起源和形成条件所致。尽管遵循着同样的引力定律，它们的运行轨迹却可能截然不同。不，自然界的经验表象是由各种趋势主导的。这些趋势的兴衰更替源于一系列潜在的规律、条件和偶然因素。我们的科学理解力求揭示那些我们常说的“隐藏在可观察事物背后的规律”。这种反对意见所依赖的自然界与社会界的对比，实际上并非同类比较。它将物理趋势背后的规律与社会趋势纯粹的经验表象进行了比较。

Interestingly, the word ‘planet’ originally meant ‘wanderer’. Planets attracted attention precisely because they did not conform to the general tendencies visible in the night sky. Kuhn’s historical study of astronomy, ‘The Copernican Revolution’ (1957), is a record of just how difficult it was to find regularities beneath the tendencies. Whether there are any underlying social laws is a matter for empirical enquiry, not philosophical debate. Who knows what wandering, aimless, social phenomena will turn into symbols of law-like regularity? The laws that do emerge may well not govern massive historical tendencies, for these are probably complex blends like the rest of nature. The law-like aspects of the social world will deal with the factors and processes which combine to produce empirically observable effects. Professor Mary Douglas’s brilliant anthropological study ‘Natural Symbols’ (1973) shows what such laws may look like. The data is incomplete, her theories are still evolving, like all scientific works it is provisional, but patterns can be glimpsed.
有趣的是，“行星”一词最初的意思是“游荡者”。行星之所以引人注目，恰恰是因为它们不符合夜空中可见的普遍规律。库恩的天文学史著作《哥白尼革命》（1957）记录了在这些规律之下寻找规律是多么困难。是否存在任何潜在的社会法则，这需要通过实证研究来探究，而非哲学辩论。谁又能预知那些游荡的、漫无目的的社会现象最终会演变成具有规律性的象征呢？最终出现的法则或许并不能支配大规模的历史趋势，因为这些趋势很可能像自然界的其他事物一样，是各种复杂因素的混合体。社会世界的规律性将着重于那些共同作用产生可观察结果的因素和过程。玛丽·道格拉斯教授的杰出人类学著作《自然符号》（1973）展现了这类法则的可能面貌。数据尚不完整，她的理论仍在发展中，就像所有科学著作一样，这只是暂时的，但可以从中看出一些规律。

In order to bring the discussion of laws and predictions down to earth it may be useful to conclude with an example. This will show the sort of law the sociologist of science actually looks for. It will also help to clarify the abstract terminology of ‘law’, and ‘theory’ which has little practical currency in the conduct of either the sociology or history of science.
为了使关于规律和预测的讨论更贴近实际，举例说明或许有所帮助。这将展现科学社会学家实际寻找的规律类型。同时，这也有助于澄清“规律”和“理论”这些抽象术语，因为它们在科学社会学或科学史的研究中几乎没有实际应用价值。

The search for laws and theories in the sociology of science is absolutely identical in its procedure with that of any other science. This means that the following steps are to be found. Empirical investigation will locate typical and recurrent events. Such investigation might itself have been prompted by some prior theory, the violation of a tacit expectation or practical needs. A theory must then be invented to explain the empirical regularity. This will formulate a general principle or invoke a model to account for the facts. In doing so it will provide a language with which to talk about them and may sharpen perception of the facts themselves. The scope of the regularity may be seen more clearly once an explanation of its first vague formulation has been attempted. The theory or model may, for example, explain not only why the empirical regularity occurs but also why, sometimes, it does not occur. It may act as a guide to the conditions on which the regularity depends and hence the causes for deviation and variation. The theory, therefore, may prompt more refined empirical researches which in turn may demand further theoretical work: the rejection of the earlier theory or its modification and elaboration.
科学社会学中对规律和理论的探寻，其程序与其他任何科学完全相同。这意味着需要遵循以下步骤：首先，通过实证调查发现典型且反复出现的事件。此类调查本身可能源于某种先验理论、对某种隐含预期的违背，或是出于实际需要。接下来，必须构建一个理论来解释这种经验规律。这将形成一个普遍原则或引入一个模型来解释事实。这样做不仅能提供一种讨论事实的语言，还能加深对事实本身的理解。一旦尝试解释其最初模糊的表述，规律的范围就能更清晰地展现出来。例如，该理论或模型不仅可以解释经验规律出现的原因，还可以解释它有时为何不出现。它还可以指导我们了解规律所依赖的条件，从而解释偏差和变异的原因。因此，该理论可能会促使人们进行更精细的实证研究，而这些研究反过来又可能需要进一步的理论工作：否定早期的理论，或者对其进行修改和完善。

All of these steps may be seen in the following case. It has often been noted that priority disputes about discoveries are a common feature of science. There was a famous dispute between Newton and Leibniz over the invention of the calculus; there was bitterness over the discovery of the conservation of energy; Cavendish, Watt and Lavoisier were involved in the dispute over the chemical composition of water; biologists like Pasteur, medical men like Lister, mathematicians like Gauss, physicists like Faraday and Davy all became embroiled in priority disputes. The approximately true generalisation can thus be formulated: discoveries prompt priority disputes.
以下案例便体现了上述所有步骤。人们常常注意到，关于发现的优先权之争是科学领域的一个常见现象。牛顿和莱布尼茨之间曾就微积分的发明展开过著名的争论；能量守恒定律的发现也曾引发激烈争论；卡文迪许、瓦特和拉瓦锡卷入了关于水化学成分的争论；巴斯德等生物学家、李斯特等医学家、高斯等数学家、法拉第和戴维等物理学家都曾陷入优先权之争。由此可以得出大致正确的结论：发现会引发优先权之争。

It is quite possible to sweep this empirical observation aside and declare it to be irrelevant to the true nature of science. Science as such, it may be said, develops according to the inner logic of scientific enquiry and these disputes are mere lapses, mere psychological intrusions into rational procedures. However a more naturalistic approach would simply take the facts as they are and invent a theory to explain them. One theory which has been proposed to explain priority disputes sees science as working by an exchange system. ‘Contributions’ are exchanged for ‘recognition’ and status—hence all those eponymous laws like Boyle’s Law and Ohm’s Law. Because recognition is important and scarce there will be struggles for it, hence priority disputes, (Merton (1957), Storer (1966)). The question then arises of why it is not obvious who has made a certain contribution: why is it possible for the matter to become one of dispute at all? Part of the answer is that because science depends so much on published and shared knowledge, a number of scientists are often in a position to make similar steps. The race will be a close one between near equals. But second, and more important, is the fact that discoveries involve more than empirical findings. They involve questions of theoretical interpretation and reinterpretation. The changing meaning of empirical results provides rich opportunities for misunderstanding and misdescription.
完全有可能将这一经验观察结果抛诸脑后，并宣称它与科学的真正本质无关。可以说，科学本身的发展遵循着科学探究的内在逻辑，而这些争论不过是疏忽，不过是理性程序中出现的心理干扰。然而，一种更自然主义的方法会直接接受事实，并构建理论来解释它们。一种解释优先权之争的理论认为，科学的运作遵循一种交换系统。“贡献”被用来交换“认可”和地位——因此才有了那些以人名命名的定律，例如波义耳定律和欧姆定律。由于认可既重要又稀缺，因此必然会存在争夺，从而产生优先权之争（默顿 (1957)，斯托勒 (1966)）。那么问题来了，为什么某项贡献的归属并不显而易见？为什么这个问题会演变成争论？部分原因在于，由于科学高度依赖已发表和共享的知识，许多科学家往往能够取得类似的进展。这场竞争将在实力相近的科学家之间展开。但其次，也是更重要的一点是，科学发现不仅仅涉及经验结果，还涉及理论解释和重新解释的问题。经验结果含义的不断变化为误解和误述提供了丰富的机会。

The discovery of oxygen will illustrate these complexities (Toulmin (1957)). Priestley is frequently credited with the discovery of oxygen, but this is not how he saw the matter. For him the new gas that he isolated was dephlogisticated air. It was a substance intimately connected with combustion processes as conceived in terms of the phlogiston theory. It required the rejection of that theory and its replacement by Lavoisier’s account of combustion before scientists saw themselves as dealing with a gas called oxygen. It is the theoretical components of science which give scientists the terms in which they see their own and other’s actions. Hence those descriptions of actions which are involved in the imputation of a discovery are precisely the ones which become problematic when important discoveries are taking place.
氧气的发现将阐明这些复杂性（图尔敏，1957）。普里斯特利常被认为是氧气的发现者，但这并非他本人的看法。对他而言，他分离出的新气体是脱燃素的空气。根据燃素理论，这种物质与燃烧过程密切相关。科学家们必须先摒弃燃素理论，代之以拉瓦锡的​​燃烧理论，才能意识到他们研究的是一种名为氧气的气体。正是科学的理论组成部分，赋予了科学家们看待自身和他人行为的框架。因此，在重大发现发生时，那些用于归因于某项发现的行为描述，恰恰会成为问题所在。

Now it should be possible to offer an account of why some discoveries are less prone to create priority disputes than others. The original empirical generalisation can be refined. This refinement, however, will not be a simple or arbitrary limitation on the scope of the generalisation. Rather, it will take the form of a discrimination between different types of discovery prompted by the above reflections on the exchange theory. This allows for an improved statement of the empirical law: discoveries at times of theoretical change prompt priority disputes; those at times of theoretical stability do not.
现在，我们可以解释为什么有些发现比其他发现更不容易引发优先权之争。最初的经验概括可以进一步完善。然而，这种完善并非简单地或武断地限制概括的范围，而是基于上述对交换理论的思考，对不同类型的发现进行区分。这使得我们可以更准确地表述经验规律：理论变革时期的发现会引发优先权之争，而理论稳定时期的发现则不会。

Naturally the matter does not rest here. First, the refined version of the law has to be checked to see if it is empirically plausible. This, of course, means checking a prediction about the beliefs and behaviour of scientists. Second, another theory needs to be developed to make sense of the new law. There is no need to go into more detail although the point may be made that a theory has been formulated which performs this task. It is provided by T. S. Kuhn in his paper The Historical Structure of Scientific Discovery (1962a) and his book ‘The Structure of Scientific Revolutions’ (1962). More will be said about this view of science in a subsequent chapter.
当然，事情远不止于此。首先，必须检验这条定律的修正版本是否符合经验事实。这当然意味着要检验关于科学家信念和行为的预测。其次，需要发展出另一套理论来解释这条新定律。无需赘述，但值得一提的是，已经有人提出了一套理论来完成这项任务。这套理论出自 T.S.库恩的论文《科学发现的历史结构》（1962a）和著作《科学革命的结构》（1962）。关于这种科学观，我们将在后续章节中进行更详细的阐述。

It does not matter for the present whether the exchange model, or Kuhn’s account of science, is correct. What is at issue is the general way in which empirical findings and theoretical models relate, interact and develop. The point is that they work here in exactly the same way as they do in any other science.
目前来看，交换模型或库恩的科学理论是否正确并不重要。关键在于经验发现和理论模型之间相互关联、相互作用和发展的一般方式。关键在于，它们在这里的运作方式与其他任何科学领域完全相同。
